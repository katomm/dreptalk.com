# Code of Conduct

DRepTalk is part of the Cardano community, and everyone taking part in this project, in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and the forum itself, is expected to follow the [Cardano Community Code of Conduct](https://cardano.org/community-code-of-conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep the focus on governance and the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination are not welcome here. (aka common sense)

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this code of conduct, please report it privately to [dreptalk.padding436@passmail.net](mailto:dreptalk.padding436@passmail.net). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that do not align with it.
