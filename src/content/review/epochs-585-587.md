## The same plan, a different instrument

[Budget: ₳5M Loan for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/90cf51975f9c19c291bac3e57364a89507e64daa0a2d35e356f87e668482155600/) closed at the start of epoch 587 with 65.09% of counted DRep power behind it, on 163 yes ballots, 64 no and 17 abstain, and six committee members voting yes with one abstaining.

The [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) has the previous chapter of the same story. Two withdrawal requests for this listing plan had expired two windows earlier in the single digits, with all seven committee members finding them unconstitutional for a specific reason: the constitution permits no withdrawal that is not made under a budget in effect, and none was. This proposal is that missing budget, and it changes the offer as well as the paperwork. Instead of a grant it asks for ₳5M as a loan repaid with interest, which its text calls the first loan based treasury request in Cardano governance, with a board of advisers drawn from exchange and foundation leadership to oversee the work. The terms are on the page: interest of 2.44% a year, which the document derives from the staking reward rate plus a small margin, repayment beginning no later than the end of the third year and the whole balance settled within five, with Intersect administering the funds. What the money buys is the same as before, listings of the SNEK token on centralised exchanges and retail brokers, liquidity for its ada pairs and the legal and compliance costs listings require.

Whether DReps came round because of the loan structure, because the constitutional defect was fixed, or because a budget vote is a lower stakes decision than a payment, is not something the record says. Nobody published a reason for changing position, and the honest statement is the comparison itself: the same plan drew single digit support as a grant request and 65.09% as a loan budget.

## A regional plan at 10.52%

[Cardano in Oceania: A community-led strategic plan for investing in growth.](/ga/89e3a4f09122d669d6f6c82dd894e02826e8497be7eb036233bafc0ee4fc666500/) closed at the start of epoch 586 with 10.52% of counted DRep power behind it, on 47 yes ballots to 94 no and 33 abstain. Six committee members found it constitutional, which is the committee doing its job and saying nothing about whether the plan was any good.

The plan asked the community for ₳778,000 for training and mentoring local advocates, a hackathon, meetups, governance workshops and marketing across the region, to be released milestone by milestone by an administrator named in its document, with a disputed milestone open to appeal to the DReps.

The two results sit oddly together and the gap between them should not be read as a verdict on regions versus tokens. One was a loan the treasury would get back, the other a plan for spending. They are different kinds of ask, put to the same electorate in the same weeks.

## A second attempt at the constitution

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.3](/ga/c882f194684d672316212f01efc6d28177e8965b7cd6956981fe37cc6715961e00/) was filed in epoch 586, after the revision covered in the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) fell short. It carries the change that one wanted, removing the budget mechanism and folding its requirements into the withdrawal action itself, so that a request like the listing plan would not need a separate budget first. It adds a few things the earlier version did not: definitions, a requirement that proposal documents stay immutable once submitted, and treasury audit safeguards applied to every withdrawal rather than some. That last one is concrete: the duties to appoint independent auditors, publish reports, designate an administrator and keep the money in separate auditable accounts had applied only to withdrawals above ₳1M in a two year period, and the revision would make them the general rule. Its deadline was the start of epoch 593, and the [review of epochs 591 to 593](/governance-review/epochs-591-593/) has the result.

## Still open, and quiet

The liquidity fund, the strategy draft and the withdrawal for the governance tooling were all still being voted on at the close. Nothing was paid in these three epochs, and nothing had been since epoch 578, so ₳78.0M of the ₳350M ceiling remained where it was.

The window recorded 309 ballots, 294 from DReps, 6 from pools and 9 from committee members, with final ballots from 117 distinct DReps. The power delegated to DReps reached ₳5.66B at epoch 586, the highest reading up to then, held by 861 DReps. The count with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs fell from 341 to 316, which is the rolling window moving rather than DReps leaving: the payout epochs with their thousands of ballots were passing out of it. The treasury reading rose ₳8.2M to ₳1.61B.
