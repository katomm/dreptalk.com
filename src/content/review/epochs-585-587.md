## The same plan, a different instrument

[Budget: ₳5M Loan for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/90cf51975f9c19c291bac3e57364a89507e64daa0a2d35e356f87e668482155600/) closed at the start of epoch 587 with 65.1% of DRep power behind it, on 163 yes ballots, 64 no and 17 abstain, and six committee members voting yes with one abstaining. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no.

The [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) has the previous chapter of the same story. Two withdrawal requests for this listing plan had expired two windows earlier in the single digits, with all seven committee members finding them unconstitutional for a specific reason: the constitution permits no withdrawal that is not made under a budget in effect, and none was. This proposal is that missing budget, and it changes the offer as well as the paperwork. Instead of a grant it asks for ₳5M as a loan repaid with interest, which its text calls the first loan based treasury request in Cardano governance, with a board of advisers drawn from exchange and foundation leadership to oversee the work. The terms are on the page: interest of 2.44% a year, which the document derives from the staking reward rate plus a small margin, repayment beginning no later than the end of the third year and the whole balance settled within five, with Intersect administering the funds. What the money buys is the same as before, listings of the SNEK token on centralised exchanges and retail brokers, liquidity for its ada pairs and the legal and compliance costs listings require.

The DReps who backed it wrote about the loan rather than about the earlier refusals. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) called it "the first loan-based Treasury request", complementing the grant model, and noted that the borrower had already put 4,500,000 dollars of its own money into listings on three large exchanges. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) looked at repayment: the borrower's own application had 749,811 transactions in a year, second only to one decentralised exchange, and an estimated 1,437,600 ada of annual revenue on public analytics, "a source of loan repayment". The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) wrote that the intention to repay "with a clearly defined schedule is what makes this proposal a no brainer", and [CryptoCrow](/dreps/drep1ytfwpmt2fvdnyvlswyjyggpf8alwkpm7ha6cua9nsak525ssmvm57/) that listings are "a solid boon" for native assets.

The no votes were about the same loan from the other side. [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) wrote that success was defined only as "achieving listings" or "increasing liquidity" with no numerical targets, that no cash flow forecast showed several million ada could be repaid, and that if repayments were missed "the Treasury effectively provides an unsecured loan and the community absorbs all the risk". [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) had looked at what the earlier self funded listings had produced: daily trading volumes of 88,000 dollars on one exchange, 30,000 on another and 2,000 on a third, and no lasting change in on chain activity beyond "a short-term spike". The same plan drew single digit support as a grant request and 65.1% as a loan budget, and none of the quoted rationales compares the two.

## A regional plan at 10.5%

[Cardano in Oceania: A community-led strategic plan for investing in growth.](/ga/89e3a4f09122d669d6f6c82dd894e02826e8497be7eb036233bafc0ee4fc666500/) closed at the start of epoch 586 with 10.5% of DRep power behind it, on 47 yes ballots to 94 no and 33 abstain. Six committee members found it constitutional, which is the committee doing its job and saying nothing about whether the plan was any good.

The plan asked the community for ₳778,000 for training and mentoring local advocates, a hackathon, meetups, governance workshops and marketing across the region, to be released milestone by milestone by an administrator named in its document, with a disputed milestone open to appeal to the DReps.

The largest DReps sent it elsewhere. Yoroi abstained, writing that events, hackathons and community activities "better suited" to Catalyst, and YUTA voted no for the same reason, adding that "many similar initiatives have received funding in the past, yet few have delivered clear, measurable outcomes". [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) abstained because it was advancing "a unified global marketing initiative" of its own and recommended the proposer fold the activities into that plan. The [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted yes, calling the plan "strategically sound", and asked for a narrower focus and baseline data before any withdrawal. CardanoYoda voted no because 37 of 39 recent proposals had been approved, many for booths and marketing, and projects in the same category "should be submitted and reviewed together" so that they compete. [hix_coffeepool☕️](/dreps/drep1ygnh2uf4wkc8ldgfxwz7rzuga3m8jtqew9xh5g3n587mg6g3ge0sj/) voted no because the plan did not say who would receive and manage the money.

The two results sit oddly together and the gap between them should not be read as a verdict on regions versus tokens. One proposed a loan repayable to the treasury, the other spending on regional activities. They are different kinds of ask, put to the same electorate in the same weeks.

## A second attempt at the constitution

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.3](/ga/c882f194684d672316212f01efc6d28177e8965b7cd6956981fe37cc6715961e00/) was filed in epoch 586, after the revision covered in the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) fell short. It carries the change that one wanted, removing the budget mechanism and folding its requirements into the withdrawal action itself, so that a request like the listing plan would not need a separate budget first. It adds a few things the earlier version did not: definitions, a requirement that proposal documents stay immutable once submitted, and treasury audit safeguards applied to every withdrawal rather than some. That last one is concrete: the duties to appoint independent auditors, publish reports, designate an administrator and keep the money in separate auditable accounts had applied only to withdrawals above ₳1M in a two year period, and the revision would make them the general rule. Its deadline was the start of epoch 593, and the [review of epochs 591 to 593](/governance-review/epochs-591-593/) has the result.

## Still open, and quiet

The liquidity fund, the strategy draft and the withdrawal for the governance tooling were all still being voted on at the close. Nothing was paid in these three epochs, and nothing had been since epoch 578, so ₳78.0M of the ₳350M ceiling remained where it was. For proposers, the window established that a plan refused as a payment could return as a budget and a loan.
