## Power peaks as ballot activity falls

The power delegated to DReps peaked at ₳5.66B in epoch 586, between ₳5.64B at epoch 585 and ₳5.62B at epoch 587, while DRep ballots fell from 140 in epoch 585 to 104 in epoch 586 and 50 in epoch 587. Three highs up to then sit in this window: the ₳5.66B at epoch 586, and at epoch 585 both the most DReps holding power, 864, and the most unequal spread of that power, a Gini coefficient of 0.9341.

The count of DReps with at least one ballot in the last 12 epochs fell for the seventh epoch running, from 383 at epoch 580 to 316 at epoch 587. The window's 309 ballots, superseded votes included, came 294 from DReps, 9 from committee members and 6 from pools, with 117 DReps and 3 pools casting a final ballot.

[EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) had the largest net decline the record lists for epochs 584 to 587, down ₳28.8M from ₳336.5M to ₳307.7M.

## Two signals, and 55 points between them

[Cardano in Oceania: A community-led strategic plan for investing in growth.](/ga/89e3a4f09122d669d6f6c82dd894e02826e8497be7eb036233bafc0ee4fc666500/) described a plan to grow the ecosystem in the region through community building, hackathons and marketing. It closed at the start of epoch 586 with 10.52% of counted DRep power behind it, on 47 yes votes, 94 no and 33 abstain. In ada, ₳0.44B voted yes, ₳1.41B voted no and ₳1.52B abstained. The abstention ballots cast in epoch 585, its last epoch of voting, carried ₳1.23B at that epoch's power readings. Five of its twelve largest DRep voters abstained, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) among them. Six committee members voted yes and one cast no ballot. One pool voted yes and seven no.

[Budget: ₳5M Loan for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/90cf51975f9c19c291bac3e57364a89507e64daa0a2d35e356f87e668482155600/) ended its voting at the start of epoch 587. Its 163 yes votes carried ₳3.49B, or 65.09% of counted DRep power, against ₳0.52B behind 64 no votes, with 17 abstentions. According to its proposal text, the budget sought a repayable loan of five million ada for exchange listings for a community token. The withdrawal that followed the signal is in the [review of epochs 597 to 599](/governance-review/epochs-597-599/). Six committee members voted yes and Cardano Japan Council abstained. Three pools voted yes and five no.

## A constitution revision is filed

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.3](/ga/c882f194684d672316212f01efc6d28177e8965b7cd6956981fe37cc6715961e00/) was submitted in epoch 586. It followed [CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.0](/ga/207b226e110e13bb18b119fcd313520e0fcd060b2bc9fb9a5e5bc6e94ab10f3b00/), whose expiry at the start of epoch 581 with 55.61% is covered in the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/). The new text drew 26 yes ballots, 8 no and 3 abstain across the roles in its first two epochs and remained open, with voting scheduled to end at the start of epoch 593. Three other proposals were open at the close, a stablecoin liquidity budget, a vote on the long term vision and strategy and a ₳1.15M maintenance withdrawal, which the [review of epochs 588 to 590](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/) takes up. The treasury gained ₳8.2M between the epoch 585 and epoch 587 readings, to ₳1.61B.
