## The constitution is ratified at the start of epoch 541

[Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) was ratified at the start of epoch 541 and took effect at the start of epoch 542. Its stored tally records 385 DRep yes ballots, 22 no and 6 abstain, 84.9% of DRep power against a 75% bar, with ₳3.06B of power on the yes ballots and ₳80.6M on the no. Stake pools have no vote on a constitution.

All seven members of the interim committee found it constitutional: the Cardano Atlantic Council, Input Output and the Cardano Foundation in epoch 539, and the Eastern Cardano Council, the Cardano Japan Council, the Intersect Constitutional Council and EMURGO in epoch 540. The Intersect Constitutional Council wrote that the text in the action is identical to the version voted on in Buenos Aires and Nairobi, checked by comparing the hashes of both documents and against photographs of the voting results in the convention hall. Input Output went through the six criteria the interim constitution sets for ratifying its replacement and recorded how each was met. The Cardano Atlantic Council wrote that the process the interim constitution laid out had been followed through dozens of workshops to the conventions in Buenos Aires and Nairobi, where 95% of the delegates approved the text.

The action was put forward by the convention delegates and the Constitution Ratification Working Group of the Civics Committee of Intersect. Its document records 63 workshops held in 52 countries between July and October 2024 with more than 1,400 participants, which elected 63 voting delegates and 63 alternates, and a convention in December 2024 at which the delegates approved the final text by a 95% margin. It was filed in epoch 537, on 30 January 2025, one day before the interim constitution's deadline for submitting its replacement on chain, and the new text carries an amendment process for its own revision.

## Pi Lanningham votes yes despite faults, HeptaSean votes no

[YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the DRep with the most delegated power at epoch 541, voted yes in epoch 537, writing that the text "prevents arbitrary interpretation" and that rejecting it "risks costly reconstitution". The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) called it "a living document". Pi Lanningham voted yes in epoch 539 while listing "material faults that should be addressed in a future revision", because "we could iterate on this forever and never reach consensus". HOSKY wrote that the text reflects the will of the delegates at the convention in Argentina, that there are aspects it disagrees with, and that "compromise is essential".

Rodrigo, who had attended the workshops in Buenos Aires and Santiago, wrote as a Chilean that seeking a perfect constitution had left the country "stuck with the old one". Hephaestus Stake Pool voted yes with reservations about the process, including what it called the "shepherding" of the workshop conversations.

Tempo.Vote voted no in epoch 540, writing that the text "does not include adequate guardrails" against misuse or mismanagement of the treasury and calling the handling of treasury withdrawals its most glaring issue. HeptaSean voted no the same epoch because proposals for "a leaner and less ambiguous constitution" had been sidelined at the workshops and the convention, with only minor corrections allowed. The DRep $brock voted no in epoch 538 because a technology based constitution should not rest on "subjective interpretation of what is 'reasonable'", concerns it wrote had been raised weeks before submission. Cardano Advocate voted no in epoch 540 over the one token one vote model, which it wrote makes governance exclusive for newcomers.

## A proposal to lower the treasury cut to 10% is filed

[Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%](/ga/941502b0aa104c850d197923259444d2b57cab7af18b63143775465aaacc84f500/), filed in epoch 539, asks to lower the treasury cut, the share of each epoch's rewards from the reserves and fees that goes to the treasury before stakers are paid, from 20% to 10%. Its document lists no author and argues that the 20% was set at the launch of Shelley and never reassessed, that halving it would raise staking rewards by about 12.5%, and that the new constitution permits a treasury cut between 10% and 30%. It needed 67% of DRep power and two thirds of the committee, with no pool vote. Ballots cast on it in epoch 540 carried ₳813.1M of DRep power against it and ₳312.0M for it.
