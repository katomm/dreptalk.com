## A year of workshops, ratified at one boundary

[Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) was ratified at the start of epoch 541. Its stored tally records 385 DRep yes ballots, 22 no and 6 abstain, 84.9% of counted DRep power against a 75% bar, with ₳3.06B of power for it and ₳0.08B against. All seven committee members voted yes. Stake pools have no vote on a constitution at all.

The document itself was not written in those four epochs. Its own text records 63 workshops held in 52 countries between July and October 2024, with more than 1,400 participants, each workshop electing one voting delegate and one alternate, and 63 delegates approving the final text at a convention in December 2024 by a 95% margin. The interim constitution had built its own replacement into itself: it was meant to be temporary, carried only the minimum the first upgrade needed, and required a successor to be submitted on chain no later than 31 January 2025. This proposal went on chain on 30 January, with a day to spare.

What the new text adds is the part the interim document left out. Governance needs rules, parameters and guardrails written down somewhere, and this is where they now live, together with the values the workshops agreed on and a process for amending the text later. The treasury tax proposal in the next section leans on exactly that: it cites the range the constitution permits for the parameter it wants to change, which is a thing no proposal could have done a week earlier.

The vote itself ran in two parts. DRep support arrived early, 142 yes ballots in the proposal's first epoch carrying ₳1,409.6M, and kept coming. The committee voted later: three members yes in epoch 539, the remaining four in epoch 540, and ratification followed at the next boundary. Which of the two bars was cleared last cannot be read off the record, because no DRep share is reconstructed for the earlier boundaries. The constitution took effect one epoch after ratification, in the [review of epochs 542 to 544](/governance-review/epochs-542-544/).

## Who gets the reward pot

[Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%](/ga/941502b0aa104c850d197923259444d2b57cab7af18b63143775465aaacc84f500/), submitted in epoch 539, is about money rather than rules. Every epoch Cardano mints a pot of rewards out of its reserves and adds the transaction fees to it. Before any of it reaches the people who stake their ada, the treasury takes a fifth off the top, and that fifth is what funds every proposal asking for a payout. The proposal wanted to make it a tenth.

Its argument is that the share was set at the launch of Shelley and never looked at again, that halving it would raise staking rewards by about 12.5%, roughly from 4.0% to 4.5% a year, and that the constitution ratified in the same window permits anything between 10% and 30%. The counter argument needs no document: an ada that does not go into the treasury is an ada paid out now instead of funding a proposal later, and the treasury had yet to pay for anything at all. As an economic parameter the change needed 67% of counted DRep power and two thirds of the committee, with no pool vote.

DReps leaned against it from the start. The 120 ballots it drew in epoch 540 were 49 yes to 69 no, and the no ballots that epoch carried ₳813.1M of DRep power against ₳312.0M behind the yes. It was still open at the close of epoch 541 with a deadline at the start of epoch 546, and the [review of epochs 545 to 547](/governance-review/epochs-545-547/) has the result.

## Participation catches up with delegation

Epoch 540 was the busiest epoch for DReps up to then, 187 ballots across the two proposals, and the count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 251 at epoch 538 to 363 at epoch 541, the most up to then. 296 distinct DReps cast a final ballot in the window, out of the 766 holding power. The power delegated to them grew ₳402.1M to ₳3.64B, the highest reading up to then, while the ten largest held a slightly smaller slice of it, 43.87% at epoch 538 and 43.23% at epoch 541. The treasury reading rose ₳13.2M to ₳1.68B, with nothing paid out of it yet.
