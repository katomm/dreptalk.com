## Committee support, then ratification

[Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) was ratified at the start of epoch 541. Its stored tally records 84.9% of counted DRep power behind it, on 385 yes votes, 22 no and 6 abstain, against a 75% bar. Yes ballots amounted to ₳3.06B of DRep power, no ballots to ₳0.08B and abstentions to ₳0.17B. Of its twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 541, eleven voted yes and [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) abstained. Stake pools have no vote on a new constitution.

DRep support began in epoch 537, when 142 yes ballots carrying ₳1,409.6M arrived in the proposal's first epoch. The committee came later: Cardano Atlantic Council, Cardano Foundation and a third member voted yes in epoch 539, and Eastern Cardano Council, Intersect Constitutional Council, Emurgo and a seventh member followed in epoch 540. The action needed 75% of counted DRep power and two thirds of the active committee members after abstainers are left out, five of seven here. The three votes of epoch 539 were short of that, the four of epoch 540 brought the total to seven, and the first boundary after them was the start of epoch 541. It took effect one epoch later, in the [review of epochs 542 to 544](/governance-review/epochs-542-544/).

## A tax cut arrives, and epoch 540 fills with ballots

[Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%](/ga/941502b0aa104c850d197923259444d2b57cab7af18b63143775465aaacc84f500/) was submitted in epoch 539, a proposal to halve the share of the reward pot that goes to the treasury before staking rewards are paid. As a change to an economic parameter it needed 67% of counted DRep power and two thirds of the committee, with no pool threshold. Across the roles it drew 9 yes ballots, 6 no and 1 abstain in epoch 539, then 49 yes, 69 no and 2 abstain in epoch 540, when the DRep no ballots carried ₳813.1M of DRep power against ₳312.0M behind the yes, and 17 yes, 36 no and 5 abstain in epoch 541. It remained open at the close of epoch 541, with voting scheduled to end at the start of epoch 546, and the [review of epochs 545 to 547](/governance-review/epochs-545-547/) has its result.

Epoch 540 recorded 192 ballots across the two proposals, 120 on the tax cut and 72 on the constitution, 187 of them from DReps and five from committee members, which made it the busiest epoch for DReps up to then, superseded votes included. The window recorded 413 ballots, 404 from DReps, 9 from committee members and none from pools, and final ballots came from 296 distinct DReps.

## The delegated power grows as the largest holding falls

The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳3.24B at epoch 538 to ₳3.64B at epoch 541, the highest reading up to then, and 766 DReps held some of it. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the largest, had the largest listed decline for epochs 537 to 541, ₳26.1M from ₳393.1M to ₳367.0M, while the total grew. The ten largest DReps held 43.87% of the power at epoch 538 and 43.23% at epoch 541. With no enacted withdrawal recorded, the treasury rose ₳13.2M between the epoch 538 and epoch 541 readings, to ₳1.68B. The count of DReps with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 251 to 363, the most up to then, and the tax cut remained open for them.
