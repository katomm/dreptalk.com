## Three payments, and the difference between paid and approved

At the start of epoch 575 the treasury paid ₳168.4M across three withdrawals. ₳96.8M of it went on [the core engineering payment](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e01/), a year of engineering on the node and the protocol itself and the largest single payment in this record. ₳69.5M went on [the community funding payment](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e17/), the programme through which the community hands out smaller grants of its own, and ₳2.2M on [the rollup payment](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e00/), for work on settling batches of transactions on the main chain rather than each one individually.

27 more requests cleared their votes at the boundaries of epochs 574 and 575, ₳78.1M between them, and none of that had been paid by the close. A withdrawal is ratified at one boundary and paid at the next, so approved money still sits in the treasury and still counts as unspent against the ceiling. The ₳180.1M the ceiling still allowed at epoch 575 therefore overstates what was actually free: ₳78.1M of it was already promised.

The two figures in the treasury readings are worth keeping apart as well. ₳168.4M went out, but the balance fell ₳155.9M between the epoch 573 and epoch 575 readings, because the treasury kept taking its share of rewards over the same epochs. The gross payment and the change in the balance are different measurements and neither substitutes for the other.

Nine of the original 39 requests were still being voted on at the close, all nine of them from the transaction that carried 37 of the batch, with the boundary to epoch 577 as their deadline. The [review of epochs 576 to 578](/governance-review/epochs-576-578/) has the rest.

## One plan, two requests, and a factor of a million

Two withdrawal actions for the same idea arrived in epoch 573. Both want to put a Cardano native token onto large exchanges, with liquidity provision, legal work and an advisory board, on the argument that Cardano native tokens are barely present on the platforms most people actually use. Its document asks for 5,000,000 ada.

[Withdraw ₳5M for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/1d6aea56e6a523b13e03ae30ff24ebf9852543b4f7d415c7e50f55f6baaa4e3d00/) asks the ledger for exactly that. [Cardano Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/4ff43f1eab5252ef00ba6f1429dc23715e3899834f10c067e8065c8364d5559a00/), filed in the same epoch, carries a payload of ₳5. The record stores what each action asks the ledger for, and in the second case that is five ada against a document asking for five million, which is what an amount entered in the wrong unit looks like. Both had the boundary to epoch 580 as their deadline.
