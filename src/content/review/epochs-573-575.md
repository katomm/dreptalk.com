## What arrived while the round was decided

Epoch 573 brought a proposal to replace the interim committee and two withdrawals for an exchange listing plan, for ₳5M and for five ada, and a proposed revision of the constitution followed in epoch 574. All four remained open, and all four are in the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/).

The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳5.30B at epoch 573 to ₳5.39B at epoch 575, and the number of DReps holding delegated power reached 845 at epoch 575, the highest up to then. The count with a ballot in the last 12 epochs went from 336 to 343. The spending period, epochs 532 to 612, had used ₳169.9M of its ₳350M limit by epoch 575, leaving ₳180.1M, across four withdrawals: the three paid at epoch 575 and the ₳1.5M paid in epoch 571.
