## DReps and the committee clear the bundle, pool stake falls short

[Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada and increase Plutus Memory Limits (Part 2)](/ga/ab474223d40e2e3540555364be27e161a809c33651408f43d84acff10c0ba30600/) expired at the boundary to epoch 653. It paired two changes: the minimum pool fee would have fallen from 170 ada to 75, and the Plutus memory limit would have risen from 16,500,000 to 17,500,000 units per transaction and from 72,000,000 to 77,500,000 per block.

The DReps cleared the 67% it needed, at 68.6% of DRep power, and the committee stood at 71.4%, five members yes, against two thirds. The pools gave it 34.5% of stake against 51%, on 147 yes ballots, 59 no and 41 abstain. More pools voted yes than no, but the pool share is measured in stake, and a pool that did not vote counts as no.

Pools had a vote on the action because of the memory half. A parameter change asks pools only when it touches security parameters, and the document behind the second action states that a change of the minimum pool fee alone does not require their approval. Hephaestus Stake Pool (HEPHY), voting yes on the bundle, had written that the fee "is not actually voted on by SPOs" and that bundling it with the memory change "muddies the waters about who is actually voting on what".

## Cerkoryn's fee cut returns on its own

One epoch after the bundle expired, in epoch 654, [Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada](/ga/75e7882a8ef2bc39517bffbfb654e89f525def5a8364d81e11fc5facafc6dd9b00/) was submitted with the same cut and nothing else. Its document names Cerkoryn as author, also of the parameter proposal behind it, and describes the cut as an interim step toward a proportional minimum margin. It needs 67% of DRep power and two thirds of the committee, and no pool share. Voting on it runs until the boundary to epoch 661.

When this period closed it stood at 6.2% of DRep power, on 20 yes ballots, 13 no and 2 abstain, with ₳533.9M of power abstaining. In the committee, Leandros BSP had voted yes. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), yes on the bundle, abstained. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [CryptoCrow](/dreps/drep1ytfwpmt2fvdnyvlswyjyggpf8alwkpm7ha6cua9nsak525ssmvm57/) and the [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), all yes on the bundle, had not voted.

[Martin Lang](/dreps/drep1ytwmwvtd0a8lr45ssner2tjxzv5y8q03w3606yeald9mdmgmwecja/), a DRep and pool operator, wrote that a minimum margin parameter has to come before any further cut. [hix_coffeepool☕️](/dreps/drep1ygnh2uf4wkc8ldgfxwz7rzuga3m8jtqew9xh5g3n587mg6g3ge0sj/) wrote that 75 ada per epoch is below the cost of running a pool and that the previous cut did not resolve the same pressure. [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) called its abstention constructive: it voted yes on the bundle, then looked at what the previous cut from 340 ada to 170 achieved, and does not endorse this one in its current form.

On the yes side, [Cerkoryn](/dreps/drep1yglrf4el8gghum239fggvfrau25k2576y4dvcz65r2ukj8sqpsc2k/) wrote that the fee "props up pools that cannot operate as cost-efficiently".

For pools and their delegators the minimum fee stayed at 170 ada through these epochs.

## Pitkind and Leandros BSP join the Cardano committee

[Update Constitutional Committee 2026](/ga/729daaf2f9f89f842a61f6e3ebf7e57d16d6fa4116e29c13114780cb3909085000/) was ratified at the boundary to epoch 653 and took effect at the boundary to epoch 654, with 72.0% of DRep power and 56.2% of pool stake. The committee does not vote on its own composition. Four seats now run through epoch 799: Phil_uplc and Cardano Curia stay on with new terms, Leandros BSP and Pitkind are new. Tingvard, Eastern Cardano Council and Ace Alliance keep terms through epoch 726. The outgoing members, as YUTA wrote in its yes rationale, were KtorZ and the Cardano Japan Council.

## The Ikigai reimbursement at 65.2% as of the close of epoch 655

[Ikigai's deposit reimbursement](/ga/7d37220b71806410cc8adfbdafbad494e1ce3fdc674ab13c88a72b3b27de78d900/) asks for ₳103,000. Its document describes a 100,000 ada deposit on an early info action that a node bug kept from being returned, plus 3,000 ada for staking rewards forgone. Its voting ends at the boundary to epoch 656. As of the close of epoch 655 it held 65.2% of DRep power against the 67% a withdrawal needs, on 129 yes ballots, 5 no and 7 abstain. The committee stood at 71.4%, with Pitkind the one no ballot and Phil_uplc not voting.

Yoroi and EMURGO were among the 7 abstentions, which together took ₳805.8M of power out of the count. Yoroi wrote that it was withholding its vote "in light of the current situation" and would reassess later. EMURGO wrote that it prefers to withhold judgment until the situation reaches resolution. The [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted yes. It wrote that this is a resubmission with no change to metadata or reward account, after an earlier submission fell short of the DRep threshold.

## Governance incentives request at 1.7% as of the close of epoch 655

[Governance Incentives Framework 2026](/ga/f57f8fcb4e83ad1b6f58bffd4a302aacbb5a90874c83c5f84a7f8b4bb43ce9bc00/) asks for ₳4.2M. Its document describes research, modelling and a controlled pilot to evaluate governance incentive mechanisms before any of them goes to governance for adoption. As of the close of epoch 655 it stood at 1.7% of DRep power, on 12 yes ballots, 98 no and 19 abstain, and at 14.3% in the committee, with Tingvard, Ace Alliance, Eastern Cardano Council and Pitkind finding it unconstitutional. YUTA wrote that it does not build consensus formation, which it calls the real bottleneck, into its success conditions.

## OpenZeppelin asks for ₳11.8M

It prices delivery at 1,831,000 US dollars at 0.16 dollars per ada and adds a 3% fee for Intersect as administrator. Its document states that OpenZeppelin has not received treasury funds before.

When the period closed it stood at 0.9% of DRep power, on 6 yes ballots, 23 no and 1 abstain. YUTA voted no. [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) wrote that an Input Output repository already describes a reusable contracts library of this kind. At ₳11.8M the request fits under the ₳42.6M left below the ₳500M spending limit for epochs 613 to 713. No withdrawal was paid in these epochs, and the treasury rose by ₳7.5M, from ₳1.35B at the start of epoch 653 to ₳1.36B at the start of epoch 655.
