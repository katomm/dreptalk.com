## In force

[Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) took effect at the start of epoch 542, one epoch after its ratification, which the [review of epochs 538 to 541](/governance-review/epochs-538-541/) covers. It had 84.9% of counted DRep power against a 75% bar, on 385 yes votes, 22 no and 6 abstain. The ballots themselves carried ₳3.06B of yes, ₳0.08B of no and ₳0.17B abstaining, and the share leaves the abstentions out while counting power without a ballot on the no side. All seven committee members had voted yes, the last of them in epoch 540. Stake pools have no vote on a new constitution. Of its twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 544, eleven voted yes and [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) abstained. The support had come early: 142 yes ballots in epoch 537, its first epoch, carrying ₳1,409.6M of DRep power at that epoch's readings.

According to its text, the document sets out the guardrails every later governance action has to respect, among them that a treasury withdrawal needs a net change limit for the period and a budget approved by ada holders. The spending limit signals that followed are in the [review of epochs 545 to 547](/governance-review/epochs-545-547/).

## The first proposal filed under it

[Defining the Cardano Vision and Roadmap for 2025 and beyond](/ga/56f39054758f1a3cedc1de9225d66bf270b62dfdbfbc5399f1d6d43aceffc63600/) was submitted in epoch 542, the epoch the constitution took effect, and was the only proposal filed in the window. According to its text it asked the DReps to weigh in on a development roadmap drawn up within Intersect, as input for the budget discussions then beginning. Across the roles it drew 8 yes ballots in epoch 542, 46 yes, 3 no and 4 abstain in epoch 543 and 33 yes, 1 no and 2 abstain in epoch 544, and the DRep yes ballots of epoch 543 alone carried ₳782.8M at that epoch's power readings. Its voting was scheduled to end at the start of epoch 549, and the [review of epochs 548 to 553](/governance-review/epochs-548-553/) has its result. The other open action, [Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%](/ga/941502b0aa104c850d197923259444d2b57cab7af18b63143775465aaacc84f500/), had been filed in epoch 539 with voting scheduled to end at the start of epoch 546, and the [review of epochs 545 to 547](/governance-review/epochs-545-547/) has its result.

## A smaller top ten

The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳3.71B at epoch 542 to ₳3.88B at epoch 544, ₳169.5M more and the highest reading up to then, with 792 DReps holding some of it. The ten largest held 42.94% of it at epoch 544, their smallest share up to then and down from 50.16% at epoch 522, while the Gini measure of the whole spread, 0.9197, was the highest up to then. Power outside the ten largest accounted for a larger share while inequality across all DReps with power increased, and the two measures do not say which smaller delegations grew.

The rolling count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 368 to 386, the highest up to then. The treasury gained ₳8.7M between the epoch 542 and epoch 544 readings, to ₳1.69B, and the record lists no withdrawal.
