## The delegated power nearly doubles

The delegated DRep power grew ₳576.8M between epochs 517 and 522, from ₳0.65B to ₳1.23B, the highest reading up to then, spread across 518 DReps. The count with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs reached 159 at epoch 520, the most up to then, and read 148 at epoch 522. Of the 518 DReps holding power at epoch 522, 371 had cast no ballot in the previous twelve epochs. DRep ballots per epoch ran 19, 15, 16, 20, 0 and 3, and epoch 521 was the first epoch without a recorded DRep ballot in the series that begins at epoch 508. With no enacted withdrawal listed, the treasury finished epoch 522 at ₳1.60B, ₳22.8M above its epoch 517 balance. Of the 154 ballots cast in the window, superseded votes included, 73 came from DReps, 70 from pools and 11 from committee members, and final ballots represented 70 distinct DReps and 65 pools, with three proposals open for the next window.
