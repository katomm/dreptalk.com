## More DReps vote yes on K, more delegated power votes no

[Should K increased?](/ga/7fd6429add8f2611ad8d48c0cc49101463093aec285faea402e8cfde78ea58d700/), put forward by Atrium Lab in epoch 514, asked whether Cardano should raise K, the target number of stake pools, which sets the saturation point beyond which more stake no longer raises a pool's rewards. The document puts K at 500. It also states that it is no suggestion to change anything and that its goal was to get more people voting before the next hard fork. The action closed at the start of epoch 521 with 15.5% of DRep power.

More DReps voted yes than no, 88 to 54 with 22 abstaining, but the no ballots carried ₳347.9M of delegated power, the yes ballots ₳153.1M and the abstentions ₳119.0M. By power at epoch 522 the largest no ballots came from the [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), JAZZ, Martin Lang and AKYO, while [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the largest DRep, had abstained in epoch 515 for the reasons the [review of epochs 514 to 516](/governance-review/epochs-514-516/) quotes. Pools cast 51 yes ballots, 29 no and 4 abstain.

CIP-1694 sets every threshold of an information action at 100% and rules out its ratification, and in the bootstrap phase then in force a change to K itself needed only a committee vote. The 15.5% is an on-chain record of opinion with no effect on the parameter.

## The Eternl DRep Committee votes no, Hephaestus Stake Pool wants a smaller step

The Eternl DRep Committee voted no in epoch 518, writing that a raise "would only raise Cardano's operational costs without adding any real value" on a network that had run stably for over four years. Chris of Straightpool voted no in epoch 520, writing that the reward sharing scheme should be addressed as a whole rather than by "manipulating k in isolation". HeptaSean voted no in the same epoch, writing that the supporting links left the impression of a "pure promotion stunt" and that it was not sure Cardano could sustain 500 pools, let alone 1,000. InputEndorsers changed its ballot to no in epoch 519, citing the Cardano Foundation's committee finding and agreeing that the action lacked relevant supporting materials.

Hephaestus Stake Pool voted yes in epoch 520 but wrote that K should not rise as far as the 1,000 often put forward, and that a step to 588 would bring the saturation point back to its level of December 2020, when K was set to 500. HAZEL, a stake pool, voted yes in epoch 519 for a step to 750, writing that so much held ada is inactive that doubling K would cost many holders their rewards, often for long periods.

## Three committee members find the K question unconstitutional

The Cardano Foundation found the action unconstitutional in epoch 519, the Cardano Japan Council and the Intersect Constitutional Council in epoch 520. The Cardano Japan Council wrote that it discusses a change to K but lacks "the necessary foundational research and objective indicators". The Intersect Constitutional Council found its supporting material, three links to Atrium Lab, not sufficiently relevant to the question, and wrote that it had neither supported nor opposed any change to K. The Cardano Atlantic Council abstained in epoch 519 and the Eastern Cardano Council in epoch 520, the latter because the action by its own text measures no sentiment towards another action that can be enacted on chain. Emurgo and Input Output cast no ballot.

## Ikigai, the note of thanks, closes at 41.4%

[Cardanoの生きがい - Ikigai -](/ga/59fd353253eb177e2104e8f23ea4c63e3d32ef95c7865d03e90d3884424dc1db00/), submitted in epoch 510, thanks Input Output, EMURGO and the Cardano Foundation and asks for no change on chain or off it. It closed at the start of epoch 517 with 41.4% of DRep power. It drew 104 yes ballots, 3 no and 16 abstain, ₳271.8M of power for it and ₳3.2M against, so most of its no side was power that cast no ballot. Pools cast 44 yes ballots to 1 no. The Cardano Japan Council, the Cardano Foundation and the Intersect Constitutional Council voted yes, the Cardano Atlantic Council, the Eastern Cardano Council and Input Output abstained, and Emurgo cast no ballot. All of its ballots were cast in epochs 510 to 516, and the [review of epochs 508 to 513](/governance-review/epochs-508-513/) and the [review of epochs 514 to 516](/governance-review/epochs-514-516/) quote selected reasons.

## Intersect files three proposals still open at the close

[Intersect's first filing](/ga/51f495aa23f4b3b3aa90afde4a0e67823bb7ac4ac65f5ffbb138373b863f2f7400/) of a Plutus cost model update arrived in epoch 518, and Intersect's governance actions repository records that it was found not to have the desired effect on chain and that its metadata address was broken on purpose. [Plutus V3 Cost Model Parameter Changes Prior to Chang#2](/ga/b2a591ac219ce6dcca5847e0248015209c7cb0436aa6bd6863d0c1f152a60bc500/) followed in epoch 519 under the same title and abstract, with prices for 12 new Plutus operations that the hard fork to protocol version 10 would add, recommended by Intersect's Parameter Committee and approved unanimously by its Technical Steering Committee. Neither drew a DRep or pool ballot.

[Rename the Chang 2 Hard Fork to the Plomin Hard Fork](/ga/fff0df644d328a5367212f45bab59060bde3c4091dc96c723062896fd619731400/), filed in epoch 522 by Intersect for its Hard Fork Working Group, asks for that hard fork to be named in memory of Matthew Plomin, a community contributor who died in November 2024.

## Delegation to DReps grows by ₳576.8M to ₳1.23B

The power delegated to DReps grew ₳576.8M between epochs 517 and 522, from ₳651.8M to ₳1.23B, the highest reading since on-chain governance began, held by 518 DReps. Of those, 371 had cast no ballot in the previous twelve epochs. DReps cast 73 ballots in the six epochs, with none recorded in epoch 521, after voting on the K question had ended and before the Plomin name was filed. Of the 154 ballots cast across the window, superseded votes included, pools cast 70 and committee members 11, with final ballots from 70 distinct DReps and 65 pools. The treasury stood at ₳1.60B at epoch 522, ₳22.8M above its epoch 517 reading, and withdrawals from it were not yet possible under the bootstrap rules.
