## The committee that was appointed is replaced by one that was chosen

[Replace Interim Constitutional Committee](/ga/47a0e7a4f9383b1afc2192b23b41824d65ac978d7741aca61fc1fa16833d111100/) was ratified at the start of epoch 580 and took effect at the start of epoch 581. DReps gave it 78.6% of counted power, on 261 yes ballots to 5 no, and stake pools 55.99% of counted stake against a 51% bar. The committee had no vote on it, which is the point: a body cannot hold a veto over its own replacement.

The [review of epochs 542 to 544](/governance-review/epochs-542-544/) noted the clock this ran against. The members sitting until now had been appointed for the trial phase, with a term the constitution set to expire that autumn, and this is the vote that filled the seats deliberately rather than provisionally.

What the record shows about the new body is narrower than the story around it. It lists the members that had an authorised voting key at epoch 581: four of them, two with keys authorised back in epochs 507 and 508 and two authorised in epochs 578 and 581. Their terms end at two different epochs, 653 for two of them and 726 for the others, which is the staggering that keeps a committee from turning over all at once. A seat, an authorised key and the minimum size the protocol requires are three separate things, and the snapshot behind this edition holds no value for the third at all.

## A revision that would have removed the budget rule

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.0](/ga/207b226e110e13bb18b119fcd313520e0fcd060b2bc9fb9a5e5bc6e94ab10f3b00/) expired at the start of epoch 581 with 55.61% of counted DRep power behind it, on 160 yes ballots, 53 no and 25 abstain. A new constitution needs 75%, so the DRep result decided this on its own: no committee position could have carried it.

Most of what it proposed was housekeeping. Its own text lists wording fixes, clearer definitions, corrected typos and the deletion of provisions nobody had used, among them codes of conduct for DReps and pool operators. One change was substantive: it would have removed the rule that a treasury withdrawal has to be covered by a budget that is in effect.

## The rule that rule was about

That rule had just done something. [Withdraw ₳5M for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/1d6aea56e6a523b13e03ae30ff24ebf9852543b4f7d415c7e50f55f6baaa4e3d00/) and [Cardano Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/4ff43f1eab5252ef00ba6f1429dc23715e3899834f10c067e8065c8364d5559a00/), the pair of requests for the same token listing plan covered in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/), both expired at the start of epoch 580, at 5.65% and 5.53% of counted DRep power. All seven committee members found both of them unconstitutional, and the published reason is exact: the constitution permits no withdrawal that is not being made under a budget then in effect, and no budget covered these.

So the plan changed shape. In epoch 580 [Budget: ₳5M Loan for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/90cf51975f9c19c291bac3e57364a89507e64daa0a2d35e356f87e668482155600/) was submitted: a budget rather than a withdrawal, which is the missing step the committee named, and a loan rather than a grant, which is a different offer to the treasury. Six committee members voted yes on it and one abstained. Its deadline was the start of epoch 587, and the [review of epochs 585 to 587](/governance-review/epochs-585-587/) has the result.

Read together, the two votes at these boundaries point in opposite directions. The committee enforced the budget requirement unanimously, and a revision that would have deleted it fell 19 points short of its bar.

## The numbers

The window recorded 401 ballots, 335 from DReps, 61 from pools and 5 from committee members, with final ballots from 175 distinct DReps and 59 pools. The power delegated to DReps rose ₳97.1M to ₳5.63B at epoch 581, the highest reading up to then, held by 853 DReps, with the ten largest holding 49.05% of it. The treasury reading rose ₳8.3M to ₳1.58B, and of the ₳350M the ceiling allows through epoch 604, ₳272.0M had been paid and ₳78.0M was left.
