## Seven seats change hands

[Replace Interim Constitutional Committee](/ga/47a0e7a4f9383b1afc2192b23b41824d65ac978d7741aca61fc1fa16833d111100/) was ratified at the start of epoch 580 and took effect at the start of epoch 581. According to its text, the interim committee's term ran out with epoch 580, and the seven new members had been chosen by DReps in an election Intersect hosted from May to July, with three seats for a longer term and four for a shorter one so that future elections are staggered. The DReps gave it 78.6% of counted power against a 67% bar, on 261 yes votes, 5 no and 10 abstain, and all twelve of its largest DRep voters voted yes. DReps and pools vote on a committee change, the committee itself does not, and pool support stood at 55.99% against a 51% bar: 382 pools voted yes, 5 no and 7 abstained, with stake that cast no ballot counted as no. Seven seats were filled, but the record lists four members with an authorized hot key at epoch 581, two with terms to epoch 653 and two to epoch 726, and those four are the members it counts as active at the decisions below.

## The v2.0 revision falls short at the same boundary

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.0](/ga/207b226e110e13bb18b119fcd313520e0fcd060b2bc9fb9a5e5bc6e94ab10f3b00/) expired at the start of epoch 581, the epoch in which the committee changed, with 55.61% of counted DRep power behind it, on 160 yes votes, 53 no and 25 abstain, against a 75% bar. The DRep shortfall alone decided it. ₳2.47B voted yes, ₳0.94B voted no and ₳1.24B abstained. In epoch 580, its last epoch of voting, abstention ballots carried ₳905M and yes ballots ₳874M at that epoch's power readings. Among the twelve largest voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 581, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) abstained and [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) and [Army of Spies](/dreps/drep1ygqgayvx8yzsaj9hprja3l6jy3v4px9z3u8uvecuvm3f92ce7mckx/) voted no.

The handover changed which committee ballots counted. Of the four members counted as active at the decision, Cardano Atlantic Council had voted yes, Eastern Cardano Council had abstained and two had cast no ballot, one yes over four minus one abstainer, or 33.33%. The ballots of five departed interim members were set aside, and the record flags the committee as below its minimum size at that decision. For comparison, [Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) had been enacted in epoch 542 on 84.9%, and a later revision is covered in the [review of epochs 591 to 593](/governance-review/epochs-591-593/).

## Seven no votes on each listing withdrawal

[Withdraw ₳5M for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/1d6aea56e6a523b13e03ae30ff24ebf9852543b4f7d415c7e50f55f6baaa4e3d00/) asked, according to its text, for ₳5M to fund exchange listings for a community token, and [Cardano Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/4ff43f1eab5252ef00ba6f1429dc23715e3899834f10c067e8065c8364d5559a00/), filed in the same epoch, asked for five ada. ## ₳5.63B delegated

The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳5.53B at epoch 579 to ₳5.63B at epoch 581. Both the total and its concentration, a Gini coefficient of 0.9332, were highs up to then. The 401 ballots comprised 335 from DReps, 61 from pools and 5 from committee members, superseded votes included, and final ballots came from 175 distinct DReps and 59 distinct pools.

After the run of 37 withdrawals that ended in epoch 578, the treasury paid nothing in these three epochs and gained ₳8.3M between the epoch 579 and epoch 581 readings, to ₳1.58B.
