## The committee that was appointed is replaced by one that was chosen

[Replace Interim Constitutional Committee](/ga/47a0e7a4f9383b1afc2192b23b41824d65ac978d7741aca61fc1fa16833d111100/) was ratified at the start of epoch 580 and took effect at the start of epoch 581. DReps gave it 78.6% of their power, on 261 yes ballots to 5 no, and stake pools 55.99% of counted stake against a 51% bar. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no. The committee had no vote on it, which is the point: a body cannot hold a veto over its own replacement.

The [review of epochs 542 to 544](/governance-review/epochs-542-544/) noted the clock this ran against. The members sitting until now had been appointed for the trial phase, with a term the action's own document says was set to end with epoch 580, and this is the vote that filled the seats deliberately rather than provisionally. The document records the results of an election Intersect hosted: seven seats, three of them for a term of 146 epochs and four for 73, with the candidate ranked highest choosing a term length first and the others following in order. It removes the four appointed members, Cardano Foundation, Input Output Global, EMURGO and Intersect, and three of the elected bodies were already sitting on the interim committee.

The yes votes were about the election rather than the names. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) called the process "transparent, accessible, and independently verified", and [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) dated it, from 5 May to 6 July 2025, and noted that approval avoids a gap after the interim term ends. The [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) had endorsed seven candidates through its own assessment and found the final composition "aligned with our recommendations". [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) named what was new: for the first time none of the members is affiliated with a founding entity, and it would have supported the change even if the elected members had not been its own choices.

What the record shows about the new body is narrower than the story around it. It lists the members that had an authorised voting key at epoch 581: four of them, two with keys authorised back in epochs 507 and 508 and two authorised in epochs 578 and 581. Their terms end at two different epochs, 653 for two of them and 726 for the others, which is the staggering that keeps a committee from turning over all at once. A seat, an authorised key and the minimum size the protocol requires are three separate things, and the snapshot behind this edition holds no value for the third at all.

## A revision that would have removed the budget rule

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.0](/ga/207b226e110e13bb18b119fcd313520e0fcd060b2bc9fb9a5e5bc6e94ab10f3b00/) expired at the start of epoch 581 with 55.61% of DRep power behind it, on 160 yes ballots, 53 no and 25 abstain. A new constitution needs 75%, so the DRep result decided this on its own. Most of what it proposed was housekeeping: its own text lists wording fixes, clearer definitions, corrected typos and the deletion of provisions nobody had used, among them codes of conduct for DReps and pool operators. The central change was to the budget rule: it would have removed the rule that a treasury withdrawal has to be covered by a budget that is in effect.

Yoroi and EMURGO abstained, and for the same two reasons. Yoroi wrote that removing the budget information action "weakens financial transparency around treasury withdrawals" and that the proposal "moved on-chain while feedback was still being raised". [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) wrote that the removal "weakens the link between treasury withdrawals and long-term priorities" and that consensus had not been reached. Both also objected to changes in the safeguard that makes treasury funded addresses abstain automatically. The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) voted no after going through the text line by line and found a new disclosure requirement for people holding several roles "not enforcable", since an anonymous DRep can also be an anonymous pool operator. [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) agreed with the changes in principle but not with a roadmap information action in place of the budget one, which he expects to be "too prone to have the process captured and centralized".

The yes side wanted the budget step gone. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) wrote that the revision supports builders more efficiently while strengthening financial soundness, adding disclosure duties in exchange for the budget step and freeing withdrawals under 1,000,000 ada in two years from audit requirements. SASA wrote that the two step process had "created significant burdens for both DReps and proposers, with limited practical benefits", and [Phil UPLC](/dreps/drep1yg343cyuckglj48a6gpcey7fkfcy5x5f9g65wme3ne9q2mgaedmkm/) that the scope was too large for one amendment but that the redundancy of separate budget and withdrawal proposals was "so unbelievably bad" that he voted for it anyway. The Cardano Foundation voted yes and answered the process objection directly: the drafting had no workshops or convention, "we do not agree that this should be a requirement", and the proposal followed the amendment process as the constitution requires.

The committee changed underneath this vote, and its members knew it. The Intersect Constitutional Council wrote at the end of epoch 578 that its ballot would be recorded but not counted once the new committee took office, and cast an abstention as its last act. The Cardano Japan Council found the revision unconstitutional, because the constitution expects amendments to follow review in community forums and this one skipped the information action stage entirely. The Eastern Cardano Council abstained, finding the formal requirements met but "significant process-related concerns regarding participatory legitimacy". None of that decided anything: the DRep share did.

## The budget rule in practice

That rule had just done something. [Withdraw ₳5M for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/1d6aea56e6a523b13e03ae30ff24ebf9852543b4f7d415c7e50f55f6baaa4e3d00/) and [Cardano Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/4ff43f1eab5252ef00ba6f1429dc23715e3899834f10c067e8065c8364d5559a00/), the pair of requests for the same token listing plan covered in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/), both expired at the start of epoch 580, at 5.65% and 5.53% of DRep power. Their documents are the same text: the Snek Foundation asked the community for ₳5M to get the SNEK token listed on centralised exchanges and retail brokers, to provide liquidity for its ada pairs and to pay the legal and compliance costs listings require, with an advisory board to oversee the spending. Only the first carried that sum on chain. The second asked for ₳5M in its document but its withdrawal on chain was ₳5. All seven committee members found both of them unconstitutional, and the Eastern Cardano Council's published reason is exact: the constitution permits no withdrawal that is not being made under a budget then in effect, and no budget covered these.

The DReps did not need the committee to reach the same result, and their reasons point forward. Yoroi wrote that the request "lacks a clear funding structure that ensures tangible returns for the ecosystem" and encouraged the team to return "starting with an info budget action". EMURGO wanted listing money tied to shared infrastructure, demonstrable return "or repayment to the Treasury". The Eternl DRep Committee noted that the plan included exchanges the token was already listed on, and added that with "a proper treasury repayment plan" it might vote in favour in the future. YUTA recommended "a treasury repayment plan", measurable targets and an analysis of what the token's existing listing on a large exchange had done. Dave voted no because the proposal "lacks a repayment or profit-share model". CardanoYoda argued from the other end, that a meme coin should drive activity on chain and that listings on centralised exchanges work against that, with around 40,000 users of the token against more than 1,300,000 ada stakers. [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) pointed out that the foundation had already self funded over 4,000,000 dollars of listings and saw a precedent for private listings paid from the treasury. The Cardano Foundation abstained on both, to give the team "space to complete this work" on a revised proposal.

So the plan changed shape, in the direction four of those ballots had asked for. In epoch 580 [Budget: ₳5M Loan for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion - Powered by Snek](/ga/90cf51975f9c19c291bac3e57364a89507e64daa0a2d35e356f87e668482155600/) was submitted: a budget rather than a withdrawal, which is the missing step the committee named, and a loan rather than a grant, which is the repayment plan the DReps named. Its document sets interest at 2.44% a year, promises the whole balance settled within five years and names Intersect as the administrator of the funds. Its deadline was the start of epoch 587, and the [review of epochs 585 to 587](/governance-review/epochs-585-587/) has the result.

Read together, the two votes at these boundaries left the budget requirement standing: the committee applied it to the listing requests unanimously, and the revision that would have deleted it fell 19 points short of its bar.
