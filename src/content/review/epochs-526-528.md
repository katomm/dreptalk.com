## In force one epoch after ratification

[Plutus V3 Cost Model Parameter Changes Prior to Chang#2](/ga/b2a591ac219ce6dcca5847e0248015209c7cb0436aa6bd6863d0c1f152a60bc500/) took effect at the start of epoch 526, one epoch after its ratification, which the [review of epochs 523 to 525](/governance-review/epochs-523-525/) covers together with the other parameter change of that boundary, an untitled one that expired. According to its text, the change updated the execution cost model for Plutus scripts of the third version ahead of the second Chang upgrade, the upgrade the signal below proposed to rename. It had been ratified on seven committee yes votes and no DRep or pool ballot, as the bootstrap rules provided, and the tally is in that review.

## The rename signal in its last epoch

[Rename the Chang 2 Hard Fork to the Plomin Hard Fork](/ga/fff0df644d328a5367212f45bab59060bde3c4091dc96c723062896fd619731400/), submitted in epoch 522, was scheduled to end its voting at the start of epoch 529, and the [review of epochs 529 to 534](/governance-review/epochs-529-534/) has its close. As of the close of epoch 528 it stood at 76.54% of counted DRep power, with six committee yes votes and one abstention as of that close. The window's ballots on it ran 23 yes, 1 no and 2 abstain in epoch 526, 29 yes, 2 no and 5 abstain in epoch 527 and 35 yes with 1 abstention in epoch 528, across the roles. The DRep yes ballots of epoch 528 amounted to ₳353.0M at that epoch's power readings, against ₳57.4M across epochs 526 and 527 together, and the DRep abstentions of epoch 527 amounted to ₳69.6M. Five of the seven committee ballots came in these epochs: Cardano Foundation and Emurgo voted yes and Eastern Cardano Council abstained in epoch 527, and two more members voted yes in epoch 528.

## ₳376.5M in one epoch

The delegated DRep power grew ₳399.9M across the window, from ₳1.74B at epoch 526 to ₳2.14B at epoch 528, the highest reading up to then, and the step from epoch 527 to 528 alone accounted for ₳376.5M. The number of DReps holding power reached 592, and the count with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 192 to 205, the most up to then. The ten largest DReps held 44.40% of the power at epoch 526 and 44.79% at epoch 528. The treasury grew ₳8.9M to ₳1.62B over the window, and no withdrawal was enacted. The window recorded 98 ballots, 67 from DReps, 26 from pools and 5 from committee members, superseded votes included, and final ballots came from 65 distinct DReps and 26 pools.
