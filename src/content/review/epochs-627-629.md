## 0.23 points short

[Pebble + Gerolamo - HLabs 2026 Budget](/ga/b11527fbcdc9d41e8f497de64a029a18673a5eefc413718459046f0b7a1a665600/) asked the treasury for ₳8.0M for a year of work on tooling that, by its own account, most Cardano developers use directly or through libraries built on it. When its voting closed at the start of epoch 628 it stood at 66.77% of counted DRep power. The bar is 67%. It expired 0.23 percentage points short, on 140 yes ballots, 43 no and 20 abstain, and it was the only thing decided in these three epochs.

Six committee members voted yes and one no, 85.71% against the committee's two thirds bar.

Among the twelve largest DReps on its ballot list, ranked by delegated power at epoch 629, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) abstained, [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) and [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted no, and the other eight voted yes.

HLabs was back within an epoch. [Pebble & Ecosystem maintenance: TypeScript core of Cardano](/ga/4206ae0bc11ba74f9eb86cd561ba684e44f0f9dfe12bc8f23911422a7e0a75f300/), from the same firm, was submitted in epoch 629, one epoch after the first request expired and for ₳4.6M instead of ₳8.0M.

The smaller figure is a smaller job, not the same job at a discount. The expired request covered twelve months with three headline deliveries: maintenance through an upcoming hard fork, a production ready light node, and a production ready smart contract language. The new one funds two engineering tracks, the language and the maintenance of the TypeScript tooling most Cardano developers depend on, and the light node is not among them. Its path is in the [review of epochs 633 to 635](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/).

## Eight new requests for ₳97.2M

Eight new requests were submitted in epochs 627 to 629, asking for ₳97.2M between them, and all eight were still open when the window closed.

Revised requests were returning to the ballot, sometimes before the original had expired. [Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore](/ga/7b42570b685aa6085c8345f894a6b04df06cb6fb17f9f252cfe891ceae705ddc00/) came in epoch 627, ₳7.8M from the Cardano Foundation for that one conference on its own. Its text is precise about what changed: the request is decoupled from the separate sponsorship of a second conference, and the summit budget itself is cut by 22%, which it puts at 550,000 dollars, by going from three stages to two and by raising the Foundation's own internal contribution from 250,000 to 380,000 dollars. The 22% is measured against the summit part of the earlier request rather than against the ₳14.1M the bundle asked for in total. Where the HLabs request came back smaller, the conference request came back split.

The bundle it was written to replace, [Cardano Summit 2026 and TOKEN2049 Singapore](/ga/bd91fa7ea9b4e09f76cbde3abb0b564ffc60b27e18af464ccec9bef9c718d08700/), reached its own deadline exactly at the close of the window, at 10.01% of counted DRep power as of that close.

The largest of the eight was a ₳39.8M request for two years of core infrastructure work, submitted in epoch 628, and the second largest [Cardano Vision 2026: Human Centred, Scalable, Post Quantum Secure - IO Research](/ga/5ad10ad39f49a0f93ac91e70482f114fdbcbc05b20ac774dbe23a82efbcb820a00/) at ₳32.9M, submitted in epoch 629.

## Nothing paid

The treasury paid nothing in the window. Between the readings at epoch 627 and epoch 629 its balance rose by ₳7.6M, from ₳1.62B to ₳1.63B. DReps cast 1,159 ballots: 374 in epoch 627, 299 in epoch 628 and 486 in epoch 629. Committee members cast another 17. Those totals include superseded votes, and 153 DReps cast a final ballot somewhere in the window. No stake pool ballot fell in it.
