## Protocol version ten

The bootstrap phase ended at the start of epoch 537. [Hard Fork to Protocol Version 10 ("Plomin" Hard Fork)](/ga/0b19476e40bbbb5e1e8ce153523762e2b6859e7ecacbaf06eae0ee6a447e79b900/), submitted in epoch 529, was ratified at the start of epoch 536 and took effect one epoch later. Stake pools and committee members voted on it, and DRep approval was not required under the bootstrap rules, which is why the record holds no DRep ballot on it. Stake pools gave it 65.99% of counted stake against a 51% bar, on 666 yes ballots, 4 no and 35 abstain, with the stake that cast no ballot counted on the no side. All seven committee members voted yes, two of them in epoch 534 and the other five in epoch 535. According to its text and the governance design it implements, the upgrade ended the bootstrap phase and let DRep votes count on the kinds of action they had been excluded from.

## 146 ballots after eight silent epochs

[Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) was submitted in epoch 537, the epoch the hard fork took effect, and drew 142 DRep yes ballots, 3 no and 1 abstain in that epoch, with ₳1,409.6M recorded against the yes ballots at epoch 537 power readings, superseded ballots included. The record shows no DRep ballot from epoch 529 to epoch 536, epochs in which the only open proposal was the hard fork and DRep votes did not count on it, and then 146 in epoch 537, the most in a single epoch up to then. All twelve of its largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 537, voted yes, [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) and [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) among them. It remained undecided at the close of epoch 537, with voting scheduled to end at the start of epoch 544, and the [review of epochs 538 to 541](/governance-review/epochs-538-541/) follows it.

## Five in six DReps silent

At epoch 536, 673 DReps held delegated power and 575 of them, more than five in six, had cast no ballot in the previous twelve epochs. The count with a recent ballot fell from 119 at epoch 535 to 99 at epoch 536 and rose to 190 at epoch 537 with the constitution ballots. At epoch 537, 686 DReps held ₳3.00B, the highest total up to then and ₳161.4M above the epoch 535 reading of ₳2.83B. With no enacted withdrawal recorded, the treasury rose ₳8.9M between the epoch 535 and epoch 537 readings, to ₳1.66B. The window's 243 ballots, superseded votes included, were 146 from DReps, 92 from pools and 5 from committee members, with final ballots from 137 DReps and 91 pools, and the constitution's vote had six epochs to run.
