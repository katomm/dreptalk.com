## The rules change at one boundary

At the start of epoch 537 [Hard Fork to Protocol Version 10 ("Plomin" Hard Fork)](/ga/0b19476e40bbbb5e1e8ce153523762e2b6859e7ecacbaf06eae0ee6a447e79b900/) took effect, and the list of things Cardano governance could decide stopped being a short one. Until that boundary a proposal could adjust a protocol parameter, start a hard fork, or record an opinion, and nothing else: no payment out of the treasury, no change to the constitutional committee, no constitution of its own. From the boundary on, all of those were possible, and none of them could be settled without the DReps, whose votes had counted for nothing until then.

The proof arrived immediately. [Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution](/ga/8c653ee5c9800e6d31e79b5a7f7d4400c81d44717ad4db633dc18d4c07e4a4fd00/) was submitted in epoch 537 itself, the first proposal of a kind that had been impossible one epoch earlier, and DReps cast 142 yes ballots on it in that epoch against 3 no and 1 abstain. Its deadline was the start of epoch 544, though a proposal can be ratified as soon as it has the votes, and the [review of epochs 538 to 541](/governance-review/epochs-538-541/) has the decision.

## How the hard fork itself was decided

The upgrade that gave DReps their vote was one they had no vote on. It needed the committee and the stake pools, and it got both: 65.99% of counted pool stake against a 51% bar, with abstaining stake set aside and stake that cast no ballot counted against, plus 4 pool no ballots and 35 abstentions. All seven committee members voted yes, two in epoch 534 and the other five in epoch 535. Ratification followed at the start of epoch 536 and the upgrade itself one epoch later, the interval every ratified action waits before it takes effect. The [review of epochs 529 to 534](/governance-review/epochs-529-534/) covers the six epochs of pool voting that led here.

## A dormant electorate wakes up in one epoch

The eight epochs before this boundary hold no DRep ballot at all, and at epoch 536, 575 of the 673 DReps holding delegated power had none in the previous twelve epochs. That was not indifference: for eight epochs there had been nothing open they were entitled to vote on. The moment there was, the count of DReps with a recent ballot went from 99 at epoch 536 to 190 at epoch 537, and 137 distinct DReps cast a final ballot inside this window.

At the epoch 537 reading, 686 DReps held ₳3.00B of delegated power, ₳161.4M more than at epoch 535 and the highest reading up to then. The window recorded 243 ballots across the roles, superseded votes included: 146 from DReps, 92 from pools and 5 from committee members. The treasury reading rose ₳8.9M to ₳1.66B, still with nothing paid out of it, though for the first time a proposal could now ask.
