## Yes votes carry ₳1.64B on the fee cut, no votes ₳539.5M

[The fee cut](/ga/75e7882a8ef2bc39517bffbfb654e89f525def5a8364d81e11fc5facafc6dd9b00/) expired at the start of epoch 661. It would have lowered the minimum fee a stake pool must charge from 170 ada to 75 and changed nothing else. It drew 87 yes votes, 37 no and 19 abstain.

The yes votes outweighed the no votes, but the yes side held 44.2%, because power that did not vote counts as no.

Five of the seven committee members voted yes, meeting the two-thirds requirement, and pool approval is not required, so the DRep side alone kept the cut from taking effect. For pools and their delegators the minimum fee stayed at 170 ada.

## The fee and Plutus memory bundle had 34.7% of pool stake

[Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada and increase Plutus Memory Limits (Part 2)](/ga/ab474223d40e2e3540555364be27e161a809c33651408f43d84acff10c0ba30600/) carried the same cut together with higher Plutus memory limits and expired at the start of epoch 653. It had 68.6% of DRep power, above the 67% the DReps needed, and 34.7% of pool stake, below the 51% the pools needed. Pools had a vote on it because of the memory limits, a vote they do not have on the fee alone. Yes votes on the bundle carried ₳3.50B of DRep power.

## YUTA and SIPO abstain on the fee cut

[YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the largest DRep, wrote that its own honest view is yes. What changed its judgment, YUTA wrote, was how pools had voted on the bundle: 59 of the 247 pools that voted on it voted no, and it read the default no of the pools that did not vote as "broad, silent opposition from operators on the ground". With the cut standing alone, YUTA wrote, pools have no vote "and that opposition has no path into the tally". It would consider yes on a proposal with a mitigation pools can accept, such as pairing it with a minimum margin. The fee proposal's document describes that parameter as one that scales with pool rewards instead of a fixed fee.

[SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) called its abstention constructive. It wrote that after the bundle it looked at what the 2023 cut from 340 ada to 170 achieved, citing an Input Output Research report that finds 340 ada still the most common fee at every pool size. On a model with average block rewards of epoch 653 and a zero margin, SIPO wrote, a further cut materially changes delegator returns only for pools of roughly ₳0.5M to ₳3M, which hold about 2.2% of active stake.

SIPO also wrote that the cut comes before a minimum margin exists, and warned that a later reversal would not force pools that had lowered their fee to raise it again. SIPO wrote that its own pools declare 340 ada and that it would vote yes on a cut paired with a minimum margin.

[Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) abstained as well, both citing "the current situation", as on the OpenZeppelin request. On 3 August EMURGO had announced that it would de-register its DReps in due course, to direct its resources to users affected by the security incident at SecondFi, the wallet service that replaced EMURGO's Yoroi wallet.

[Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) and the [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) did not vote, so their power counted as no.

The [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted yes, as it had on the bundle, writing that it sees "no change in circumstances that would warrant a different position". [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) voted yes and wrote that a minimum margin is the end goal and the cut is fine "as long as it's seen as a step towards that goal". [CryptoCrow](/dreps/drep1ytfwpmt2fvdnyvlswyjyggpf8alwkpm7ha6cua9nsak525ssmvm57/) and [Everstake](/dreps/drep1yt8p080ajks6zdnxd9z6a6q60p9sm9j5rl7tc63mfna8r6cnp4wr3/) also voted yes.

[SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) voted no, writing that 75 ada is not sufficient to support sustainable pool operations and that a lower floor may increase competitive pressure on operators to reduce fees further.

## The OpenZeppelin request for ₳11.8M expires at 4.0%

[The OpenZeppelin request](/ga/418df5986f50547ec4a709f1a5bce6b850753ad6614c873386b7c953fee84a9f00/) expired at the start of epoch 661 with 4.0% of DRep power, on 16 yes votes, 94 no and 17 abstain. It asked for ₳11,787,063 for 12 months of work administered by Intersect: three reference implementations, an audited contracts library for Cardano and a security retainer. Five of the seven committee members found it constitutional.

YUTA voted no, writing that the core deliverable duplicates the IO Developer Experience Initiative, already funded with ₳3,601,926, which promises "an OpenZeppelin-style collection of ready-to-use smart contracts". The Cardano Foundation voted no, objecting to "this proposal's scope, structure, and price". SIPO voted no and invited "a coordinated, narrower resubmission". Of the ten largest DReps, five voted no and none yes.

## Toto Finance, Trivolve Tech and Fairway ask for ₳32.5M
