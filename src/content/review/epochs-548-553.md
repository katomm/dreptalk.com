## Two ways to cap what the treasury may spend

Nothing could be paid out of the treasury until the DReps agreed a ceiling, and two proposals offered different ones. [Set 2025 Net Change Limit of 300M ADA, 2026 Net Change Limit of 250M ADA](/ga/7f320409d9998712ff3a3cdf0c9439e1543f236a3d746766f78f1fdbe1e06bf800/) asks for ₳300M this year and ₳250M the next, sized to what the treasury is forecast to take in, and settles both years in one vote. [2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/) asks for ₳350M across 72 epochs, from the start of epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604, sized to the previous year of actual income, and leaves the year after to its own decision. As of the close of this window it was still being voted on.

The two year proposal closed at the start of epoch 553 with 34.73% of counted DRep power behind it, on 138 yes ballots, 112 no and 14 abstain. The arithmetic deserves a second look, because ₳1.69B of delegated power voted yes on it and only ₳1.62B voted no. A share of counted power is not yes weight against no weight: abstaining power drops out, and every ada of delegated power that never cast a ballot is counted on the no side, along with the power parked on the always no confidence option. Most delegated power did not vote, and in this system not voting is a no.

The committee went four to one against it, with one abstention and one member casting no ballot at all, a share of 16.67%. One of those no votes is documented, and it is not about the money. The member found the proposal procedurally unconstitutional: its rationale failed to hash the contents of two of the documents it referenced, which the constitution requires of the off chain material behind a governance action, so the references could be changed afterwards without a trace. The same rationale says plainly that the amounts are not the objection, applauds the initiative, and says the committee wants DReps to settle on a limit. Whether the other three no votes turned on the amounts, the two year scope or something procedural is not something the record says, and none of them published a reason.

Its rival was one epoch from its own deadline. As of the close of epoch 553 the single year ₳350M proposal stood at 73.35% of counted DRep power, and the [review of epochs 554 to 556](/governance-review/epochs-554-556/) has the result.

## The roadmap closes with the committee in three minds

[Defining the Cardano Vision and Roadmap for 2025 and beyond](/ga/56f39054758f1a3cedc1de9225d66bf270b62dfdbfbc5399f1d6d43aceffc63600/) closed at the start of epoch 549 with 63.81% of counted DRep power behind it, on 243 yes ballots, 9 no and 21 abstain. It asked DReps to endorse an order of priorities drawn up by the product committee of Intersect: scaling the main chain, pulling liquidity in from other chains, building out the second layer, better developer tooling, programmable assets and several independent implementations of the node. Pools added 37 yes ballots and 2 abstentions, a yes stake share of 3.57%. The committee split three ways, three yes, one no and three abstentions, for a share of 75%, which on an opinion vote decides nothing either way.

## A rolling count peaks, and delegation keeps climbing

At epoch 548, 409 DReps had a ballot in the previous twelve epochs, the highest count up to then, and by epoch 553 it read 352. Neither figure is a count of DReps that voted in this window: the measure looks back twelve epochs, so the constitution and treasury tax votes of epochs 537 to 545 were still inside it at the start and dropping out of it by the end. Within the window itself, 300 distinct DReps cast a final ballot.

The total power delegated to DReps reached ₳4.82B at epoch 553, the highest reading up to then and up from ₳4.18B at epoch 548, with ₳298.9M of the rise falling between epochs 549 and 550 alone. Concentration went with it: the ten largest DReps held 41.29% of the total at epoch 548 and 47.32% at epoch 553. The window recorded 684 ballots, superseded votes included, 527 from DReps, 137 from pools and 20 from committee members. The treasury reading was ₳1.73B at epoch 553, ₳22.4M above epoch 548 and the highest up to then, with no withdrawal yet enacted through the governance process.
