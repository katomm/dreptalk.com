## A roadmap, and a committee in three parts

[Defining the Cardano Vision and Roadmap for 2025 and beyond](/ga/56f39054758f1a3cedc1de9225d66bf270b62dfdbfbc5399f1d6d43aceffc63600/) asked the DReps, according to its text, to weigh in on a development roadmap drawn up by a product committee within Intersect, as input for the budget discussions then under way. It closed at the start of epoch 549 with 63.81% of counted DRep power behind it, on 243 yes votes, 9 no and 21 abstain, and all twelve of its largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 553, voted yes. The committee split three ways: three members voted yes, Cardano Foundation and Emurgo among them, Intersect Constitutional Council voted no, and three abstained, Cardano Atlantic Council and Eastern Cardano Council among them, three yes over seven active members minus three abstainers, or 75%. Stake pools cast 37 yes ballots and 2 abstain, the yes ballots at 3.57% of stake.

## Two years of limits at once, at 34.73%

[Set 2025 Net Change Limit of 300M ADA, 2026 Net Change Limit of 250M ADA](/ga/7f320409d9998712ff3a3cdf0c9439e1543f236a3d746766f78f1fdbe1e06bf800/) proposed, as its title says, spending limits for two consecutive years in one action. The proposal, like the roadmap, was an information action, which records a signal and leads to no on chain change by itself. It closed at the start of epoch 553 with 34.73% of counted DRep power behind it, on 138 yes votes, 112 no and 14 abstain. Yes ballots amounted to ₳1.69B and no ballots to ₳1.62B, more yes than explicit no, yet only 34.73% of counted power, because power without a ballot and the always no confidence option count on the no side as well. Its twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power as of epoch 553, split six and six. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/), [BTBF_XSP](/dreps/drep1y2vyrqh0fa4qh4tmy7322va6ryk78uh6szppezaft0watyqp5hdwx/), [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/), [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) and [P](/dreps/drep1yftc8zs7gjcj4a9nxzplz4wg6cwweya0kxp8adnw59vsyrqvrysud/) voted yes. [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/), [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/), [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) and [Hermes Stakepool Germany (HRMS)](/dreps/drep1ygs5hngva3hymv7j4v0aewxpsjqskj9dnmy7j3jz5ven5zqvulq2t/) voted no. In epoch 552, its last epoch of voting, the no ballots amounted to ₳927M of DRep power against ₳294M behind yes, measured at that epoch and superseded ballots included. The committee went the other way from the roadmap: Cardano Foundation voted yes, Cardano Atlantic Council, Intersect Constitutional Council, Emurgo and a fourth member voted no, Eastern Cardano Council abstained and one active member cast no ballot. With the abstainer left out, six members were in the denominator and the single yes vote was a 16.67% share.

The competing proposal, [2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/), for a single period at ₳350M, was scheduled to end its voting at the start of epoch 554. As of the close of epoch 553 it stood at 73.35% of counted DRep power, on 201 yes votes, 123 no and 13 abstain, and the committee had recorded five yes, one no and one abstention as of that close. The [review of epochs 554 to 556](/governance-review/epochs-554-556/) follows it.

## ₳4.82B

The delegated power reached ₳4.82B at epoch 553, the highest reading up to then, against ₳4.18B at epoch 548, and ₳298.9M of the difference arrived between epochs 549 and 550 alone. The ten largest DReps held 41.29% of it at epoch 548 and 47.32% at epoch 553. The treasury, from which nothing had yet been withdrawn, read ₳1.73B at epoch 553, ₳22.4M above its epoch 548 reading and its highest up to then.
