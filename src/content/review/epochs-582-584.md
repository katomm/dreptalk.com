## Nine of the twelve largest, no ballot

Nine of the twelve largest DReps at epoch 584 had cast no ballot on any of the three proposals submitted in these epochs, and together they held ₳2.16B in delegated power, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) among them. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) had voted yes on all three. [CryptoCrow](/dreps/drep1ytfwpmt2fvdnyvlswyjyggpf8alwkpm7ha6cua9nsak525ssmvm57/) had supported the liquidity signal and opposed the maintenance withdrawal, and [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) had supported the liquidity signal and opposed the strategy vote.

The three arrived one per epoch. [Stablecoin DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/e5643c33f608642e329228a968770e5b19ef5f48ff1f698712e2ce864a49e3f000/) was submitted in epoch 582 and by epoch 584 was drawing far more yes ballots than no, 36 to 2 in that epoch. [Defining the Cardano 2030 Vision & Strategy](/ga/996edfe370b8e51be73541e75499a818461305e37c7fa0c1b193f2c587167cc700/) opened in epoch 583, and in epoch 584 its abstentions outnumbered its yes ballots, 12 to 5. [Withdraw ₳1,150,000 for GovTool 12 months active maintenance and development](/ga/d2db60c5307cb517c735e2d0138d2b6f10fc5b221d610fa187719bdc82af9a0300/) came in epoch 584 and opened with 24 yes ballots, 5 no and 2 abstain. The [review of epochs 588 to 590](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/) follows all three, and the [review of epochs 591 to 593](/governance-review/epochs-591-593/) has the maintenance withdrawal's result. Two earlier information actions were open as well, a growth plan for Oceania and a budget signal for a five million ada loan, both in the [review of epochs 585 to 587](/governance-review/epochs-585-587/).

## After the payout

Between epochs 575 and 578, 37 withdrawals paid out ₳270.5M, ₳168.4M of it in epoch 575 alone, and no further withdrawal was enacted through epoch 584. The balance, ₳1.82B at epoch 574, read ₳1.57B at epoch 578 and ₳1.59B at epoch 584, ₳229.1M below the epoch 574 reading. With one earlier withdrawal of ₳1.5M in epoch 571, the spending period had used ₳272.0M of its ₳350M across 38 withdrawals, and ₳78.0M was left.

## More DReps, more concentrated power

At epoch 584 the record counts 858 DReps with delegated power, the most up to then, and the Gini measure of how unevenly that power is spread reached its highest reading up to then, 0.9336. The total changed little between the epoch 582 and epoch 584 readings, from ₳5.62B to ₳5.61B. The delegated power of [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) fell from ₳371.7M at epoch 581 to ₳336.5M at epoch 584, a ₳35.2M decline and the largest listed for that interval.

DReps cast 301 of the 319 ballots, committee members 10 and pools 8, superseded ballots included, and final ballots came from 132 DReps and 5 pools. The rolling count of DReps with a ballot in the last 12 epochs fell from 376 at epoch 582 to 355 at epoch 584.
