## Nine requests, one transaction

Nine treasury requests shared a transaction and a deadline. They were submitted together in epoch 626, as governance action indices zero to eight of one hash, and their voting closed at the start of epoch 633. The Constitutional Committee, which votes only on whether an action is constitutional, found no fault with any of the nine, seven yes votes each with nothing against and nobody abstaining. The DReps did not treat them as a block.

Six went through and were paid one epoch later, ₳131.5M together in epoch 634. [IO: Consensus Initiative](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676202/) drew the strongest support of the nine at 87.99% of counted DRep power, [IO: Cardano Upgrades](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676201/) 86.42%, and the largest sum of all, ₳62.1M for the maintenance programme run with [Ensurable Systems](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676203/), 82.29%. The narrowest pass was [IO: Developer Experience Initiative](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676200/) at 67.88%, less than a point over the bar and the smallest request in the set at ₳3.6M.

Three ran out, and together they had asked for ₳30.6M. [Pogun](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676208/) reached 35.67%, [Midgard Labs](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676204/) 31.43% for layer two scaling work, and [Blockfrost](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676207/) 24.15% for indexing and maintenance. Nothing in the nine finished between 35.67% and 67.88%.

Two more withdrawals were paid at the start of epoch 635, taking the window to eight payments and ₳139.4M. One was ₳4.6M for [Pebble](/ga/4206ae0bc11ba74f9eb86cd561ba684e44f0f9dfe12bc8f23911422a7e0a75f300/) and the TypeScript core it maintains, at 69.34%, a smaller second request from the same firm after a larger one had expired in epoch 628, as the [review of epochs 627 to 629](/governance-review/epochs-627-629/) tells. The other was a conference sponsorship, below.

## What the power did

Count the ballots in ada rather than in heads and the six that passed look nothing like the three that did not. [IO: Consensus Initiative](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676202/) drew ₳5.12B of yes ballots against ₳157.6M of no. On each of the three that failed, more power voted against than for, and a further body of power roughly the size of the no side was recorded as abstaining.

What sank the three was explicit opposition. ₳2.12B voted no on Blockfrost, the heaviest opposition cast anywhere in the nine, and more weight voted against each of the three than for it.

The same split shows in the individual ballots. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), the largest DRep by delegated power at epoch 635 with ₳686.8M, voted yes on all six that passed and abstained on all three that did not. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), second at that snapshot with ₳475.7M, backed the same six and voted the other three down. Each of the three failures collected five abstentions among the twelve largest voters on its own ballot list.

## Yes to the sponsorship, no to the upgrade

The DReps cut finely elsewhere in the window too. Three of the requests decided here had started life as one: a bundle for two conferences in Singapore that expired in epoch 630, told in the [review of epochs 630 to 632](/governance-review/epochs-630-632/), and came back as a revised request for one of the two conferences and two sponsorship requests for the other. The [Baseline Sponsorship](/ga/3f0ff0d848cab1036621f92732fef5ea1cb6466305889777ee95496933dfd1e400/) asked for ₳3.3M and was paid at epoch 635 on 70.16% of counted power. The [Sponsorship Upgrade](/ga/b4b48b8b822bb0039d312795821339b3719dfc0f682d51064dae716a7610e10200/) on top of it asked for ₳1.77M more and reached 27.26%. Same event, same window: the DReps funded the baseline package and not the upgrade on top of it.

[Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore](/ga/7b42570b685aa6085c8345f894a6b04df06cb6fb17f9f252cfe891ceae705ddc00/) came closest of anything that expired, ₳7.8M finishing at 65.06%, 1.94 points short of the bar, on 129 yes votes against 61.

The clearest no of the window went to a request for ₳2.4M to identify individual durians without attaching a physical label to each fruit, a supply chain traceability proposal that would have put agricultural produce on Cardano. It drew 8 yes ballots against 132 and finished at 0.84% of counted power, and one committee member voted for it against five who did not, with the seventh casting no ballot. Of everything decided in these three epochs, it is the only action a majority of the committee opposed.

[Tweag](/ga/af15a6627176f13be28100d7aa96a40f103f63c98e553869e38ab135ff4292cd00/) asked for ₳39.8M to cover core infrastructure for two years and finished at 6.11%, on 44 yes votes against 85. Before that request expired at the start of epoch 635, a second one from the same team had already been submitted in epoch 634, for ₳18.3M over one year instead of two.

Across the window DReps cast 1,070 ballots and committee members 54, superseded votes included. In all, 204 DReps cast a final ballot in it.
