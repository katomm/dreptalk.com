## Nine requests, one transaction

Nine treasury requests shared a transaction and a deadline. They were submitted together in epoch 626, as governance action indices zero to eight of one hash, and their voting closed at the start of epoch 633. The Constitutional Committee, which votes only on whether an action is constitutional, found no fault with any of the nine, seven yes votes each with nothing against and nobody abstaining. The DReps did not treat them as a block. Seven of the nine came from one engineering company and its partner firms, and each of those names Intersect to hold the money and release it against milestones, with anything unspent going back to the treasury. The other two rode in the same transaction on their own terms.

Six went through and were paid one epoch later, ₳131.5M together in epoch 634. [IO: Consensus Initiative](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676202/) drew the strongest support of the nine at 87.99% of counted DRep power, ₳27.7M to take the Leios throughput upgrade from its public testnet to a release candidate. [IO: Cardano Upgrades](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676201/) took 86.42% for account addresses that can receive direct deposits, the design of a treasury able to hold stablecoins, and paying transaction fees in assets other than ada. The largest sum of all, ₳62.1M for the maintenance programme run with [Ensurable Systems](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676203/), nine areas from bug fixing and security reviews to release management for the second half of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027, took 82.29%. The narrowest pass was [IO: Developer Experience Initiative](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676200/) at 67.88%, less than a point over the bar and the smallest request in the set at ₳3.6M, for six months of work on tooling, documentation and onboarding for new developers.

Three ran out, and together they had asked for ₳30.6M. [Pogun](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676208/) reached 35.67% for a bitcoin lending market, a yield layer and a bridge, with a promise to return a fifth of its earnings to the treasury until the sum was repaid. [Midgard Labs](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676204/) reached 31.43% for layer two scaling work: shared data availability, the hardening of Hydra and the launch of an optimistic rollup. [Blockfrost](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676207/) reached 24.15% for a request split between an indexer that would let an operator serve a slice of the chain rather than all of it and a subsidy for the free tier the company says it has carried alone since it began. Nothing in the nine finished between 35.67% and 67.88%.

Two more withdrawals were paid at the start of epoch 635, taking the window to eight payments and ₳139.4M. One was ₳4.6M for [Pebble](/ga/4206ae0bc11ba74f9eb86cd561ba684e44f0f9dfe12bc8f23911422a7e0a75f300/), twelve months of work on a smart contract language and on the TypeScript libraries that other Cardano toolkits build on, at 69.34%, a smaller second request from the same firm after a larger one had expired in epoch 628, as the [review of epochs 627 to 629](/governance-review/epochs-627-629/) tells. The other was a conference sponsorship, below.

## What the power did

Count the ballots in ada rather than in heads and the six that passed look nothing like the three that did not. [IO: Consensus Initiative](/ga/73e171a4c0730b4b59ecae271ab89f12a9d56360b02920e1f95107dbdc1d676202/) drew ₳5.12B of yes ballots against ₳157.6M of no. Each of the three failures collected five abstentions among the twelve largest voters on its own ballot list.

## Yes to the sponsorship, no to the upgrade

The DReps cut finely elsewhere in the window too. Three of the requests decided here had started life as one: a bundle for two conferences in Singapore that expired in epoch 630, told in the [review of epochs 630 to 632](/governance-review/epochs-630-632/), and came back as three separate requests. For the conference sponsorship, the [Baseline Sponsorship](/ga/3f0ff0d848cab1036621f92732fef5ea1cb6466305889777ee95496933dfd1e400/) asked for ₳3.3M at a tier one step below the one in the bundle, its text citing the concerns about scale raised against the combined request, and was paid at epoch 635 on 70.16% of counted power. The [Sponsorship Upgrade](/ga/b4b48b8b822bb0039d312795821339b3719dfc0f682d51064dae716a7610e10200/) asked for ₳1.77M more to restore the higher tier, a larger booth and a keynote slot, and reached 27.26%. Its own document asked to be voted on together with the baseline and promised to return the whole sum within 30 days if it went through without it. Same event, same window: the DReps funded the baseline package and not the upgrade on top of it.

[Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore](/ga/7b42570b685aa6085c8345f894a6b04df06cb6fb17f9f252cfe891ceae705ddc00/) came closest of anything that expired, ₳7.8M finishing at 65.06%, 1.94 points short of the bar, on 129 yes votes against 61. Its text describes a budget cut by 22% against the bundle, from three stages to two, and a request decoupled from the sponsorship of the other conference.

The clearest no of the window went to a request for ₳2.4M to trace individual durians grown in Vietnam without a physical label on each fruit. It drew 8 yes ballots against 132 and finished at 0.84% of counted power, with one committee member for it and five against, and it is the only action decided in these three epochs that a majority of the committee opposed.

[Tweag](/ga/af15a6627176f13be28100d7aa96a40f103f63c98e553869e38ab135ff4292cd00/) asked for ₳39.8M for seventeen work packages of core infrastructure over two years, chief among them bringing the Peras finality upgrade to the main network, and finished at 6.11%, on 44 yes votes against 85. Before that request expired at the start of epoch 635, a second one from the same team had already been submitted in epoch 634, for ₳18.3M over one year instead of two, keeping three of the seventeen packages.

Across the window DReps cast 1,070 ballots and committee members 54, superseded votes included. In all, 204 DReps cast a final ballot in it.
