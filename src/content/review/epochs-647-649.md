## The limit had to move before the request could

[Cardano PRIME](/ga/529dccaadaa000746c22f1682574cb3f436eeba4d19710b90791a54226dc96d700/) asked the community for ₳120M to fund a twelve month programme to get decentralised finance protocols on Cardano ready for use, turn on incentives and build lasting liquidity in its markets, run by an outside firm under an oversight group, with Intersect holding the withdrawn funds and releasing them against milestones and published disbursement records. When it was submitted in epoch 642, the request did not fit under the spending limit in force. The net change limit for epochs 613 to 713, the cap on what withdrawals may take out of the treasury across that run, stood at ₳350M, and withdrawals since epoch 613 had used ₳335.6M of it before this window began and ₳337.4M by the end of epoch 649. That left ₳12.6M of room under the old ceiling at the window's end, against a request for ₳120M. The request says so itself: its document makes the withdrawal conditional on a limit having been agreed with enough remaining capacity to cover it in full at enactment.

The limit moved first. [Net Change Limit](/ga/a75645e0871f3dbb6207df867d9bd6a1a3a5befa40d68df6da651db4d6607fbf00/) had been submitted two epochs earlier, in epoch 640, to replace the ₳350M cap for the same period with one of ₳500M, on the argument that the practical room under the old limit was becoming constrained and that a higher cap would preserve the ability of DReps to consider credible requests on their merits. Its vote closed at the boundary to epoch 647 with 62.4% of DRep power behind it, above the simple majority its own text names for agreeing a limit, and that is the date the raise takes in this series. It is an info action, which the ledger neither ratifies nor executes, so the new ceiling is an agreement recorded by a vote rather than a rule the chain enforces.

Two epochs later the request it made room for went through. PRIME was ratified at the boundary to epoch 649 on 80.1% of DRep power and six committee votes, with its ₳120M due one epoch later, outside this window. A ceiling is not a payment, and every withdrawal still needs its own approval, but read together the two votes are one decision in two steps: the raise added ₳150M of room, and ₳120M of it was already spoken for by a request on the ballot. After that payment, ₳42.6M of the new ceiling remains for anything else until epoch 713, and DRepTalk tracks what has been drawn against it on the [treasury overview](/treasury/2026-27/).

## The two largest DReps sat out everything but one request

[Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) at ₳568.9M and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) at ₳297.2M, the largest and the fourth largest DReps at the end of the window, abstained on six of the seven actions decided here, the higher limit included. Both filed the same short rationale each time: Yoroi wrote that it was withholding its vote "in light of the current situation" and would reassess "once conditions allow for a considered decision", EMURGO that it preferred "to withhold judgment rather than vote without the certainty our mandate demands". The one action both voted yes on was PRIME, and both said so in as many words. Yoroi called it a "strategic exception" to a position it had "generally maintained", because stalling liquidity and growth programmes would harm the ecosystem. EMURGO wrote that its general stance "has been one of abstention" and that this specific proposal was too important to sit out. [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/), at ₳333.6M the third largest, abstained on all seven with no rationale in the record, and [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) at ₳183.7M abstained on the four withdrawals that ran out and voted yes on the limit and on PRIME.

That is where the four refusals were decided. DRep shares here are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no, so an abstention takes its weight out of the calculation where silence would have left it against the proposal. On Scalus, ₳2.19B of DRep power abstained against ₳1.60B for it and ₳0.39B against, more than yes and no together, and on Bifrost ₳2.07B abstained against ₳1.09B for and ₳1.12B against. Which means the 51.8% and 33.8% are readings of the DReps who chose to judge, with the three largest holders of power standing aside.

## What the voters wrote about the limit

The higher limit closed at 62.4% with ₳1.56B abstaining, and the rationales show a vote that was about PRIME as much as about the ceiling. The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) voted yes and wrote that raising the limit "to unsustainable amounts must not be the norm", but that it did not want to block the request by withholding a yes. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) voted yes and wrote that the ceiling "is not the place to judge the merits of individual proposals", and that raising it approves nothing by itself. Sebastien Guillemot changed his vote to yes on a guarantee, he wrote, from the node and Plutus teams to take the programme's feature requests seriously. The [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) abstained: the room under the old limit was close to exhausted, it wrote, but "no basis is given for the amount", and a new limit without a spending strategy was not one it could endorse.

The committee, which does not decide an info action, split on what it was looking at. One member voted no, writing that a text which designates a binding limit for the period "is not merely a statement of opinion", and another abstained as out of scope while asking whether ₳500M squares with the constitution's tenet against unreasonable spending. Read together, the ceiling that governs treasury spending until epoch 713 rests on a majority that its own text names as sufficient, with the three largest DReps outside the count and the largest institutional voter saying the number was never derived.

## Four requests ran out, one small one was paid

Six withdrawals were decided in this window, and four ran out. [Scalus](/ga/3234567a3f48ace25efc150f1369192615974e232ea06e0251701831d9e4486d00/) asked the community for ₳2.5M to continue nine months of work on a development platform whose components sit inside several of the toolkits other Cardano developers build with, so that teams depend on it without necessarily knowing they do. It reached 51.8% against the 67% bar a withdrawal needs, the nearest miss of the window. The Cardano Foundation voted yes and called it a proportionate resubmission, cut from ₳8.5M to ₳2.5M, and the Eternl DRep Committee wrote "they listened and the new proposal is much leaner". [Bifrost](/ga/dfd81f8db652fd9263e0ce3bef043b4823045ec3a5da10c817681ada7a23f03400/) asked for ₳12.3M for the first of two phases of a bridge that would bring bitcoin onto Cardano as a native asset, secured by the existing stake pool operators rather than by a custodian, and reached 33.8%. The Cardano Foundation voted no there, over the dependency on a second phase, missing vendor detail and concurrent requests from the same team. Both votes ended at the boundary to epoch 647. The committee, which judges only whether an action is constitutional, voted yes on each with six of its seven members, which has no bearing on funding.

[Global Order Book](/ga/c0660dfd01b0a2d9ba7bac02014370096242ec756031b57d78080844fbbe9c0500/) reached 26.7% and was the one request of the six the committee also turned down, at 42.9%, where three voted yes and four no. The no votes were about custody: the reward account named to receive the money, the members wrote, did not match the arrangement the constitution requires for treasury funds. On the DRep side the Eternl DRep Committee voted no because the request mixes public infrastructure with funding for the proposer's own products. [dOSPO and OMF](/ga/2f429bde312c0806bd16199da10f4145da9807161e99d4486174c6fb9a91f98300/) ran out one epoch later on 2.4%, the lowest DRep share of the seven decided here. One smaller request came through. [Daedalus](/ga/d8de068952df50c862fa1bce9b8180d3387976cbae0fb2c3d9ef84f0faaf64d600/) asked for ₳1.8M, the smallest of the six amounts here, took 77.1% and six committee votes, and was enacted at the boundary to epoch 647.
