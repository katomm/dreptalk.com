## The limit had to move before the request could

[Cardano PRIME](/ga/529dccaadaa000746c22f1682574cb3f436eeba4d19710b90791a54226dc96d700/) asked the community for ₳120M to fund a twelve month programme to get decentralised finance protocols on Cardano ready for use, turn on incentives and build lasting liquidity in its markets, run by an outside firm under an oversight group, with Intersect holding the withdrawn funds and releasing them against milestones and published disbursement records. When it was submitted in epoch 642, the request did not fit under the spending limit in force. The net change limit for epochs 613 to 713, the cap on what withdrawals may take out of the treasury across that run, stood at ₳350M, and withdrawals since epoch 613 had used ₳335.6M of it before this window began and ₳337.4M by the end of epoch 649. That left ₳12.6M of room under the old ceiling at the window's end, against a request for ₳120M. The request says so itself: its document makes the withdrawal conditional on a limit having been agreed with enough remaining capacity to cover it in full at enactment.

The limit moved first. [Net Change Limit](/ga/a75645e0871f3dbb6207df867d9bd6a1a3a5befa40d68df6da651db4d6607fbf00/) had been submitted two epochs earlier, in epoch 640, to replace the ₳350M cap for the same period with one of ₳500M, on the argument that the practical room under the old limit was becoming constrained and that a higher cap would preserve the ability of DReps to consider credible requests on their merits. Its vote closed at the boundary to epoch 647 with 62.4% of counted DRep power behind it, above the simple majority its own text names for agreeing a limit, and that is the date the raise takes in this series. It is an info action, which the ledger neither ratifies nor executes, so the new ceiling is an agreement recorded by a vote rather than a rule the chain enforces.

Two epochs later the request it made room for went through. PRIME was ratified at the boundary to epoch 649 on 80.1% of DRep power and six committee votes, with its ₳120M due one epoch later, outside this window. A ceiling is not a payment, and every withdrawal still needs its own approval, but read together the two votes are one decision in two steps: the raise added ₳150M of room, and ₳120M of it was already spoken for by a request on the ballot. After that payment, ₳42.6M of the new ceiling remains for anything else until epoch 713, and DRepTalk tracks what has been drawn against it on the [treasury overview](/treasury/2026-27/).

## Four requests ran out, one small one was paid

Six withdrawals were decided in this window, and four ran out. [Scalus](/ga/3234567a3f48ace25efc150f1369192615974e232ea06e0251701831d9e4486d00/) asked the community for ₳2.5M to continue nine months of work on a development platform whose components sit inside several of the toolkits other Cardano developers build with, so that teams depend on it without necessarily knowing they do. It reached 51.8% of counted DRep power against the 67% bar a withdrawal needs, the nearest miss of the window. [Bifrost](/ga/dfd81f8db652fd9263e0ce3bef043b4823045ec3a5da10c817681ada7a23f03400/) asked for ₳12.3M for the first of two phases of a bridge that would bring bitcoin onto Cardano as a native asset, secured by the existing stake pool operators rather than by a custodian, and reached 33.8%. Both votes ended at the boundary to epoch 647. The committee, which judges only whether an action is constitutional, voted yes on each with six of its seven members, and that has no bearing on funding: whether the treasury pays is a question for the DReps, and every body has to clear its bar at the same boundary.

[Global Order Book](/ga/c0660dfd01b0a2d9ba7bac02014370096242ec756031b57d78080844fbbe9c0500/) reached 26.7% and was the one request of the six the committee also turned down, at 42.9%, where three voted yes and four no. [dOSPO and OMF](/ga/2f429bde312c0806bd16199da10f4145da9807161e99d4486174c6fb9a91f98300/) ran out one epoch later on 2.4%, the lowest DRep share of the seven decided here, and on 57.1% in the committee, where four voted yes, none against, and three cast no ballot at all.

One smaller request came through. [Daedalus](/ga/d8de068952df50c862fa1bce9b8180d3387976cbae0fb2c3d9ef84f0faaf64d600/) asked for ₳1.8M, the smallest of the six amounts here, took 77.1% of DRep power and six committee votes, and was enacted at the boundary to epoch 647.

## Where the abstentions went

Four of the ten largest DReps abstained on every one of the four withdrawals that ran out: [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) at ₳568.9M, [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) at ₳333.6M, [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) at ₳297.2M and [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) at ₳183.7M.

An abstention is not the same as staying away. DRep power that casts no ballot is folded into the no side, while abstaining power is left out of the count altogether, so those ballots added no yes power and took their weight out of the calculation, where silence would have left it against the proposal. Three of the four voted yes on Cardano PRIME, and Blockdaemon abstained there too, on all seven actions decided in this window.

## Fewer voters, more concentrated power

Fewer DReps have voted recently. The count that cast at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs fell from 313 at epoch 647 to 302 at epoch 649, and epoch 649 itself saw 74 DRep ballots. In the window 109 DReps cast a final ballot, each counted once.

The power behind those votes is also more concentrated. The total delegated to DReps barely moved, from ₳5.27B to ₳5.28B. What moved is where it sits: the ten largest DReps held 49.9% of it at epoch 646 and 51.6% at epoch 649.
