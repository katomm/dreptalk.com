## Two bodies, two questions

[Scalus](/ga/3234567a3f48ace25efc150f1369192615974e232ea06e0251701831d9e4486d00/) asked the treasury for ₳2.5M and [Bifrost](/ga/dfd81f8db652fd9263e0ce3bef043b4823045ec3a5da10c817681ada7a23f03400/) for ₳12.3M, both for development work. Six of the seven committee members voted yes on each and one abstained on each, which is 100% of the ballots that count, well above the two thirds a withdrawal needs there. Neither secured the 67% of counted DRep power an approval needs: Scalus reached 51.8% and Bifrost 33.8%. The vote on both ended at the boundary to epoch 647, and every body has to clear its bar at the same boundary, so the money stayed where it was.

That gap is not a contradiction. Under the rules of Cardano governance the Constitutional Committee votes on whether an action is constitutional, while the DReps vote on whether it should happen, which for a treasury withdrawal means whether the treasury should pay for it. The committee's yes does not guarantee funding, and a DRep no does not necessarily imply a constitutional objection.

## Two that failed on both counts, and one that was paid

[Global Order Book](/ga/c0660dfd01b0a2d9ba7bac02014370096242ec756031b57d78080844fbbe9c0500/) missed both bars at once, at 26.7% of DRep power and 42.9% in the committee, where three voted yes and four no. [dOSPO and OMF](/ga/2f429bde312c0806bd16199da10f4145da9807161e99d4486174c6fb9a91f98300/) ran out one epoch later on 2.4%, the lowest DRep share of the seven decided here, and on 57.1% in the committee, where four voted yes, none against, and three cast no ballot at all.

One request came through. [Daedalus](/ga/d8de068952df50c862fa1bce9b8180d3387976cbae0fb2c3d9ef84f0faaf64d600/) asked for ₳1.8M, the smallest of the six amounts here, took 77.1% of DRep power and six committee votes, and was enacted at the boundary to epoch 647.

## The treasury gets ₳150M more room

Four requests expired, and the DReps widened the envelope the next ones will be judged against. The net change limit for epochs 613 to 713, the cap on what withdrawals may take out of the treasury across that run, went from ₳350M to ₳500M, agreed in [Net Change Limit](/ga/a75645e0871f3dbb6207df867d9bd6a1a3a5befa40d68df6da651db4d6607fbf00/) with 62.4% of counted DRep power behind it.

By the end of epoch 649, withdrawals since epoch 613 had used ₳337.4M, almost the whole of the old ₳350M allowance, and all but ₳1.8M of that was drawn before this window. The increase leaves ₳162.6M for the epochs still to come.

A ceiling is not a payment, and every withdrawal still needs its own approval. [Cardano PRIME](/ga/529dccaadaa000746c22f1682574cb3f436eeba4d19710b90791a54226dc96d700/) got that approval in this window, on 80.1% of DRep power and six committee votes, ratified at the boundary to epoch 649 with its ₳120M due one epoch later, outside this window. The limit itself is set by an info action, which the ledger neither ratifies nor executes, so the ceiling is an agreement recorded by a vote rather than a rule the chain enforces. DRepTalk tracks what has been drawn against it on the [treasury overview](/treasury/2026-27/).

## Where the abstentions went

Four of the ten largest DReps abstained on every one of the four withdrawals that ran out: [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) at ₳568.9M, [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) at ₳333.6M, [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) at ₳297.2M and [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) at ₳183.7M.

An abstention is not the same as staying away. DRep power that casts no ballot is folded into the no side, while abstaining power is left out of the count altogether, so those ballots added no yes power and took their weight out of the calculation, where silence would have left it against the proposal. Three of the four voted yes on Cardano PRIME, and Blockdaemon abstained there too, on all seven actions decided in this window.

## The numbers underneath

At the base of all this, fewer DReps are voting at all. The count that cast at least one ballot in the last 12 epochs fell from 313 at epoch 647 to 302 at epoch 649, against 342 at epoch 643, and epoch 649 itself saw 74 DRep ballots against 722 in epoch 643. Across all three bodies 341 ballots were cast in the window, replacement votes included. In the window 109 DReps cast a final ballot, each counted once.

The power behind those votes is also more concentrated. The power delegated to DReps barely moved, from ₳5.27B to ₳5.28B, a figure that leaves out the always abstain and always no confidence options, which are delegation targets rather than voters. What moved is where it sits: the ten largest DReps held 49.9% of it at epoch 646 and 51.6% at epoch 649, the most concentrated reading in a record reaching back to epoch 508.
