## 1,684 ballots in one epoch

DReps cast 300 ballots in epoch 630, 471 in epoch 631 and 1,684 in epoch 632, which on its own is more than the 1,257 of the three epochs before it together and the most in any single epoch since 573. Across the window DReps cast 2,455 ballots and committee members 74, superseded votes included, and 236 DReps cast a final ballot somewhere in it. No stake pool ballot was recorded in the three epochs. Everything in front of the voters was a treasury withdrawal, and those do not go to the pools.

Nine treasury requests shared the deadline. All submitted in one transaction in epoch 626, their voting closed at the boundary out of epoch 632, and in that last epoch they drew 1,033 ballots between them from DReps and committee members together, out of 1,732 cast on everything in front of the voters. As of that close six of the nine stood above the 67% bar and three below it. What the boundary made of that is the [next window's story](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/).

## No payments, one request expired

The treasury paid nothing in these three epochs. Between the readings at epoch 630 and epoch 632 its balance rose by ₳7.5M, from ₳1.63B to ₳1.64B. Of the ₳350M that the net change limit then allowed for the spending period from epoch 613, ₳67.8M had been drawn by the close of the window, across four withdrawals.

One request was decided. [Cardano Summit](/ga/bd91fa7ea9b4e09f76cbde3abb0b564ffc60b27e18af464ccec9bef9c718d08700/) asked for ₳14.1M to fund the Cardano presence at two conferences in one go, the Summit and a second event in Singapore, and expired at the start of epoch 630 with 10.0% of counted DRep power behind it, on 32 yes votes against 132. Five committee members voted yes, one no and one cast no ballot, five out of seven and 71.43%, above the committee's two thirds bar. Constitutional or not, the bundle did not have the DReps.

The separate pieces were already on chain before the bundle expired: [Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore](/ga/7b42570b685aa6085c8345f894a6b04df06cb6fb17f9f252cfe891ceae705ddc00/) was submitted in epoch 627, and a baseline sponsorship for the Singapore conference and a top up to it followed in epoch 628. How the DReps treated each of the three is told in the [review of epochs 633 to 635](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/).

## Forty two more DReps with a recent ballot

The turnout moved the count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs from 273 at epoch 630 and 275 at epoch 631 to 315 at epoch 632, a rise of 42 in three epochs and nearly all of it in the last one. The count holding power with no recent ballot fell from 593 to 566 over the same three. Both counts carry a reliability flag in the data and cover different populations, so they say that activity rose, not how many of the newly active had been silent before.

The power itself barely moved. DReps held ₳5.84B of delegation at epoch 630 and ₳5.85B at epoch 632. Delegation changed little while the count of DReps with a recent ballot rose.
