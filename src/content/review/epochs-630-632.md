## 1,684 ballots in one epoch

Nine treasury requests, all filed together in one transaction in epoch 626, had their voting close at the boundary out of epoch 632. That is the reason for everything else in this section.

DReps cast 300 ballots in epoch 630, 471 in epoch 631 and 1,684 in epoch 632, the most in any single epoch since 573. What that measures is work rather than turnout: a DRep going through all nine requests casts nine ballots, and across the whole window 236 distinct DReps cast a final ballot. No stake pool ballot was recorded at all, because everything in front of the voters was a treasury withdrawal and pools have no vote on those. Committee members cast 74 ballots, for the same reason in reverse: every request needs a constitutionality finding of its own.

In that last epoch the nine drew 1,033 ballots between them from DReps and committee members, out of 1,732 cast on everything. What they asked for ranges from ₳3.6M to ₳62.1M, and the subjects are mostly core work: consensus, maintenance of the existing node, upgrades, developer tooling, improvements to the smart contract language, second layer scaling, an indexing service and a capital project. As of the close of epoch 632 six of the nine stood above the 67% a withdrawal needs and three below it. Their decision is in the [review of epochs 633 to 635](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/).

## No payments, one request expired

The treasury paid nothing in these three epochs. Between the readings at epoch 630 and epoch 632 its balance rose by ₳7.5M, from ₳1.63B to ₳1.64B.

One request was decided. [Cardano Summit](/ga/bd91fa7ea9b4e09f76cbde3abb0b564ffc60b27e18af464ccec9bef9c718d08700/) asked for ₳14.1M to fund the Cardano presence at two conferences in one go, the Summit and a second event in Singapore, and expired at the start of epoch 630 with 10.0% of counted DRep power behind it, on 32 yes votes against 132. Five committee members voted yes, one no and one cast no ballot, which put the committee at 71.43% and above its two thirds bar. The committee had no objection to it. The DRep support was not there.

The separate pieces were already on chain before the bundle expired: [Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore](/ga/7b42570b685aa6085c8345f894a6b04df06cb6fb17f9f252cfe891ceae705ddc00/) was submitted in epoch 627, and a baseline sponsorship for the Singapore conference and a top up to it followed in epoch 628. How the DReps treated each of the three is told in the [review of epochs 633 to 635](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/).

## More DReps with a recent ballot

The count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs went from 273 at epoch 630 to 315 at epoch 632, and the count holding power with no recent ballot fell from 593 to 566. Both are rolling twelve epoch measures carrying a reliability flag, and neither is a count of who voted in this window. They say the recent activity level rose, and they do not identify 42 DReps who came back.

DReps held ₳5.84B of delegation at epoch 630 and ₳5.85B at epoch 632. Delegation changed little while the count with a recent ballot rose.
