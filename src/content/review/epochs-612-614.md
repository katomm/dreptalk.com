## ₳350M for the period from 613 to 713

The spending period that ended with epoch 612 had allowed ₳350M in treasury withdrawals, and forty of them had used ₳347.0M of it. ₳2.97M was left. Voting on the next limit closed at the start of epoch 612, ahead of the new period beginning in epoch 613. [Net Change Limit (Epoch 613 to Epoch 713)](/ga/dc4c679c8cf1cec49817d4d2c1c96cd802ec8a047a11dc0b0bb125b5af0a76cd00/) proposed ₳350M again, for the period specified as epochs 613 to 713, and closed with 61.33% of counted DRep power behind it, on 125 yes votes, 31 no and 10 abstain. Eleven of the twelve largest DReps on its ballot list, ranked by delegated power at epoch 614, voted yes and one voted no.

A net change limit is set by an information action, which the chain neither ratifies nor executes, so the ceiling is an agreement recorded by a vote rather than a rule the ledger enforces. Four committee members voted yes and three cast no ballot. Stake pools recorded 51 yes ballots, 1 no and 3 abstain, with the yes side at 9.92% of stake. A competing proposal for a lower limit over the same period was already on chain, submitted in epoch 611, and was still open when this window closed.

A second information action, [Name Protocol Version 11 hard fork - van Rossem](/ga/8845bfc37bb2f69e8f200fe28148b3dea3c4399b0c49ee0ed2bb4e349cab9eb700/), closed at the start of epoch 613 with 83.62% of counted DRep power behind the name, 86 pool ballots all yes, and five committee yes votes with one abstention.

## The pools carry a memory upgrade by 6.16 points

[Increase Transaction and Block Memory Units (Part 1 of 2)](/ga/c21b00f90f18fce4003edf42b0b0d455126e01c946e80cc5341a9f9750caf79500/) raised the memory a transaction and a block may use for script execution. It was ratified at the start of epoch 613 and took effect at the start of epoch 614, on 86.45% of counted DRep power from 224 yes votes, 4 no and 6 abstain, with six committee members for it and one casting no ballot.

This kind of parameter change also needs the stake pools, and here they were the closer side. 316 pools voted yes, 3 no and 75 abstained, and with every pool that cast no ballot counted as no the reading was 57.16% of stake against a 51% bar, 6.16 points clear. Across all actions, pools cast 327 of the window's 346 ballots in epoch 612, and final ballots in the window came from 295 pools.

## Six committee votes against a request with no title

[Untitled withdrawal of ₳500,000](/ga/4b10e5793208cb8f228756e02113227c91602248eac4d992681a0ee760b6c4e200/) has no readable name in the record. The address its metadata points to names it as the first withdrawal of a liquidity budget, and the file behind that address could not be retrieved when this review was written. It asked for ₳500,000 and expired at the start of epoch 614 with 63.55% of counted DRep power behind it, on 127 yes votes, 44 no and 24 abstain, short of the 67% bar. All six committee members who voted opposed it, and one cast no ballot. Among the twelve largest DReps on its ballot list, eight voted yes, one abstained and three voted no.

## Dropped, not expired

[Reduce minimum Constitutional Committee size (committeeMinSize) from 7 to 5](/ga/dfdac5921ab657241fce58583d61bef59a369e01d2ba78191d6df6632a07fdfd00/) was submitted in epoch 612 and dropped in epoch 614. It stood at 19.19% of counted DRep power against a 75% bar, on 38 yes votes, 55 no and 6 abstain, with one committee member voting yes.

A dropped action is removed by the ledger, neither voted down nor timed out. Parameter change proposals form a tree of predecessor links, and enacting one removes the competing branches and everything that hangs off them. The memory limit increase was enacted at the start of epoch 614, the epoch this proposal was dropped. The pack records the drop and its timing, not the predecessor links themselves. A later proposal for the same change to the committee minimum is covered in [a later review of that second proposal](/governance-review/epochs-642-646/).

No withdrawal was paid in the window, and the treasury balance rose by ₳8.6M from the epoch 612 reading to the epoch 614 one. The 800 ballots of the window, superseded votes included, were 441 from DReps, 355 of those in epoch 612, 13 from committee members and 346 from stake pools, with final ballots from 179 DReps and 295 pools.
