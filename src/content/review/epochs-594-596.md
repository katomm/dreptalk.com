## ₳1.69B abstained

[Constitutional Committee Compensation Epochs 581-653](/ga/d2745225498d1c56c0f01be9971074a49144d625df0e73a86c51689624fbadb000/) asked the DReps, according to its text, whether the treasury should fund a budget of one million ada to pay committee members for the period from epoch 581 to epoch 653. It cites the constitution's own line that committee costs are to be provided for in ecosystem budgets, and it argues that without pay the seats go only to those who can afford them. Voting closed at the start of epoch 596 with 21.18% of counted DRep power behind it, on 48 yes votes, 105 no and 27 abstain.

₳0.83B voted yes, ₳1.50B voted no, and ₳1.69B abstained, more than either side. Among the twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 596, [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) alone voted yes. [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) and four others voted no, and [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/), [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/), [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) and [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) abstained. The 21.18% is yes power over all counted power: abstaining power is left out, while power without a ballot and power delegated to the always no confidence option count on the no side.

Committee members cast advisory ballots on it too. Cardano Japan Council, Ace Alliance and Eastern Cardano Council voted yes, Cardano Atlantic Council and Tingvard abstained, and two members cast no ballot. With the two abstainers left out, five counted members remain, so three yes votes are 60%. Stake pools cast 2 yes ballots, 1 no and 12 abstentions, the yes stake at 0.09%.

## Three actions in their last epoch

[Loan ₳5,000,000 to Expand Cardano's Global Listings](/ga/03f671791fd97011f30e4d6b76c9a91f4f6bcfb60ee37e5399b9545bb3f2757a00/) reached its final epoch of voting, with the boundary to epoch 597 as its deadline. As of the close of epoch 596 it stood at 75.89% of counted DRep power, on 151 yes votes, 38 no and 20 abstain, with five committee members for it and one abstaining. Two information actions shared the deadline: [Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit.](/ga/3285b7fd0da16d21e0b8f8910c37f77e17a57cfff8f513df4baf69295480108800/) stood at 68.16% as of that close and [Securing  Generic Top-Level Domains for the Cardano Ecosystem](/ga/a36eafaea085b77f97cceacf07fe9450f8c6b47fec3af94da8f7d158a1fc972200/) at 73.83%. ## Fewer recent voters, more concentrated power

By epoch 596, 290 DReps had voted within the previous 12 epochs, down from 294 at epoch 594, and the count was last this low or lower in epoch 539. DReps cast 241 ballots in the three epochs, pools 37 and committee members 18, for 296 with superseded votes included. Final ballots came from 82 distinct DReps and 13 pools.

The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳5.64B to ₳5.65B, while the share held by the ten largest DReps went from 49.39% to 49.61%, its highest since epoch 522. No treasury withdrawal was enacted from epoch 579 through epoch 596. The balance grew ₳8.1M between the epoch 594 and epoch 596 readings, to ₳1.64B, and ₳78.0M of the ₳350M spending limit remained after 38 withdrawals. No proposal was submitted in the three epochs.
