## ₳120M leaves the treasury

The withdrawal for [Cardano PRIME](/ga/529dccaadaa000746c22f1682574cb3f436eeba4d19710b90791a54226dc96d700/) was paid at the boundary to epoch 650. What it funds is a twelve month programme to get decentralised finance protocols on Cardano ready for use, turn on incentives for them and build liquidity that stays, run by an outside firm under an oversight group, with Intersect holding the money and releasing it against milestones with published disbursement records and a separately auditable account. At ₳120M it is the largest single withdrawal in this record, which starts at epoch 508, ahead of the ₳96.8M paid in epoch 575, and every withdrawal enacted up to the end of this window comes to ₳804.4M together. The vote on it is in the [previous edition](/governance-review/epochs-647-649/).

The balance moved with it, from ₳1,453.7M at the start of epoch 649 to ₳1,337.4M at the start of epoch 650, the lowest reading in this record, and no further withdrawal was enacted inside the window.

One other vote closed inside it, at the boundary to epoch 651. [Name the Protocol Version 12 hard fork “von Bergen“](/ga/48bab0ca71cc46f2ee421242b14f292b8c8382bda707d03ea662644bed22b89300/) is an info action, which the ledger neither ratifies nor executes, proposing that the next protocol upgrade carry the name of a community member, stake pool operator and ambassador from the earliest days of the network who died in January 2026. DReps put 61.3% of counted power behind it on 125 yes ballots, 2 no and 23 abstain, pools 9.0% of stake on 34 yes ballots, and in the committee no member voted yes, three voted no and one abstained. An info action has no bar to clear, so those readings are the record of the vote and nothing more.

## Two votes reach the same deadline, one of them by design

Two actions were at their deadline as of the close of epoch 652, and they are not the same kind of proposal. [Update Constitutional Committee 2026](/ga/729daaf2f9f89f842a61f6e3ebf7e57d16d6fa4116e29c13114780cb3909085000/) fills the four committee seats whose terms run out with epoch 653 with the candidates elected in a public vote, each for a new term of 146 epochs ending at epoch 799, the longest the protocol parameter allows. That maximum is why the vote could only be decided at its deadline: the expiry epoch of the new members is a fixed value, the ledger measures a term backwards from the epoch in which the action takes effect, and any earlier start would have made each term longer than the limit, which the ledger does not accept. Its own text says so, and adds that two of the four seats go to members being re-elected, whose new terms begin where their current ones end. [Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada and increase Plutus Memory Limits (Part 2)](/ga/ab474223d40e2e3540555364be27e161a809c33651408f43d84acff10c0ba30600/) bundles two protocol changes.

The parameter change is the one worth unpacking, because the pools have a direct stake in half of it. The first part cuts the minimum fee every stake pool must charge from 170 ada per epoch to 75, a reduction of about 56%. That fee is taken before rewards are shared out, so it weighs most on small pools: the proposal puts the penalty it imposes on a pool producing one block per epoch at around 53% of what its delegators would otherwise earn, up from about 28% after the last reduction in 2023, and projects it reaching 100% by around epoch 758 if nothing changes. The second part is the other half of the memory increase an [earlier edition](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/) covers: the per transaction limit goes from 16,500,000 to 17,500,000 units and the per block limit from 72,000,000 to 77,500,000, completing a cumulative increase of a quarter in two steps.

As of that close both were above the DRep bar and only one was above the pool bar.

As of the close of epoch 652 the committee update stood at 72.0% of counted DRep power and the parameter change at 68.6%, four points apart. On the pool side they were twenty two points apart, 56.2% against 34.5%, with only the first above the 51% a pool vote needs. Pool stake is counted with abstaining stake left out, and stake behind a pool that cast no ballot counted against, though a pool whose reward account points at one of the predefined options votes that way automatically rather than falling silent, so a silent pool and an automatically abstaining one are not the same thing.

These percentages reflect voting power, not head counts. DReps carry different weights, eligible power that did not vote stays in the denominator, and abstaining power is left out of it, which is why the shares read far below what the yes and no counts suggest.

The committee votes on the parameter change as well, and as of the same close five members had voted yes and none against, above its two thirds bar. It does not vote on a committee update, so there the DReps and the pools are the whole of it.

Pool voting activity peaked in the final epoch. Across the three epochs stake pool operators cast 566 ballots on everything in front of them and DReps 418, superseded votes included, and 305 of those pool ballots fell in epoch 652, the last epoch in which either of these two actions could be voted on. That is every pool ballot of the epoch rather than a timeline for one action, so it shows when the pools were active and not which proposal drew them.

The four seats and the deadline line up. Seven members were active at the end of epoch 652, and four of them hold terms that run through epoch 653. The vote on both actions ends at the start of epoch 653, on 1 September, and those four terms end five days after that, at the boundary to epoch 654 on 6 September, the earliest moment the update could take effect. What became of either action is settled outside this window.

Two withdrawals submitted in epoch 649 were still running past the window, with deadlines at the boundary to epoch 656, and no share from inside the window exists for either. [Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit.](/ga/7d37220b71806410cc8adfbdafbad494e1ce3fdc674ab13c88a72b3b27de78d900/) is the same ₳103,000 request, on the same document, that ran out at the boundary to epoch 643 in the [review of epochs 642 to 646](/governance-review/epochs-642-646/), a deposit lost to a node bug on one of the first info actions plus the rewards forgone. [Governance Incentives Framework 2026](/ga/f57f8fcb4e83ad1b6f58bffd4a302aacbb5a90874c83c5f84a7f8b4bb43ce9bc00/) asks for ₳4.2M for a research programme to test how governance participants might be rewarded before any mechanism is put to a vote.

## The DReps behind those votes

The power delegated to DReps fell across the window, from ₳5.26B at epoch 650 to ₳5.13B at epoch 652, a drop of ₳122.9M. That figure leaves out the always abstain and always no confidence options, which are delegation targets rather than voters, and the always abstain option alone holds ₳9.78B, more than everything delegated to DReps.

The count of recent voters moved both ways. The number of DReps that cast at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs was 292 at the end of epoch 652, against 302 at epoch 649 and 342 at epoch 643, so the window sits below where the series stood before it. In the window itself it read 286 in epoch 650, 286 again in epoch 651 and 292 in epoch 652.
