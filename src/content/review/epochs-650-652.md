## The treasury, minus ₳120M

[Withdraw 120,000,000 ada for AlphaGrowth’s Cardano PRIME](/ga/529dccaadaa000746c22f1682574cb3f436eeba4d19710b90791a54226dc96d700/) was submitted in epoch 642 and enacted at the boundary to epoch 650, the moment epoch 650 began, and its last ballots are timed to epoch 648. On the DRep side 155 DReps voted yes, 39 no and 12 abstain, and the share of voting power behind it was 80.1% with abstaining power excluded, against a 67% bar, which is the share a body has to reach for ratification. Six committee ballots were yes and none were no. At ₳120M it is the largest single withdrawal in this record, ahead of the ₳96.8M enacted in epoch 575.

The balance moved with it. The treasury held ₳1,453.7M at the start of epoch 649 and ₳1,337.4M at the start of epoch 650. In this record, which reaches back to epoch 508, that is the lowest reading. It rose again to ₳1,341.1M and then ₳1,344.9M as the window ran out, and no second withdrawal was enacted inside it. Every withdrawal enacted up to the end of epoch 652 comes to ₳804.4M together. The only epoch of the chart below with a larger outflow is 634, where six withdrawals were enacted for ₳131.5M.

Of the 15 largest DReps by power at the end of epoch 652, 11 voted yes on the withdrawal. [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/), [Everstake](/dreps/drep1yt8p080ajks6zdnxd9z6a6q60p9sm9j5rl7tc63mfna8r6cnp4wr3/) and [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) abstained, and [Army of Spies](/dreps/drep1ygqgayvx8yzsaj9hprja3l6jy3v4px9z3u8uvecuvm3f92ce7mckx/) voted no. Blockdaemon and Everstake alone held ₳519.7M of delegated power at epoch 652.

## The vote on the committee closes, and four terms run one epoch longer

[Update Constitutional Committee 2026](/ga/729daaf2f9f89f842a61f6e3ebf7e57d16d6fa4116e29c13114780cb3909085000/) was submitted in epoch 646 and its expiry epoch is 653, so epoch 652 was the last epoch in which a ballot on it could be cast. As of the close of epoch 652, 166 DReps had voted yes, 8 no and 10 abstain, and the share of voting power behind it was 72.0% with abstaining power excluded, against a 67% bar. On the stake pool side 302 pools voted yes and 1 no, with 35 abstaining. By stake rather than by count, with pools that did not vote counted as no by default (a pool whose reward account delegates to the always-abstain option counts as abstain instead) and abstaining stake left out, the pool share was 56.2% against a 51% bar. The Constitutional Committee does not vote on a committee update, so there is no committee tally to read.

Seven seats were active at the end of epoch 652. Four of them hold terms that run through epoch 653 and end at the boundary to epoch 654, one epoch after the boundary at which both of the closing voting windows run out. The other three run to epoch 726. This edition does not state the committee minimum size in force at the end of the window, because our data holds no protocol parameter snapshot at or before it, and the minimum that applies today is not evidence for an earlier epoch.

## The parameter change, above one bar and below the other

[Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada and increase Plutus Memory Limits (Part 2)](/ga/ab474223d40e2e3540555364be27e161a809c33651408f43d84acff10c0ba30600/) is the second action whose voting window ends at that boundary. As of the close of epoch 652, 149 DReps had voted yes, 34 no and 10 abstain, and the share of voting power behind it was 68.6% with abstaining power excluded, against its 67% bar. On the stake pool side 147 pools voted yes and 59 no, with 41 abstaining. By stake, again with pools that did not vote counted as no by default (a pool whose reward account delegates to the always-abstain option counts as abstain instead) and abstaining stake left out, the pool share was 34.5% against a 51% bar. Five committee ballots were yes and none were no.

Every body with a bar has to reach it at the same epoch boundary, so no single reading settles the action on its own. For scale, one earlier parameter change sits in this review's comparison list, [Increase Transaction and Block Memory Units (Part 1 of 2)](/ga/c21b00f90f18fce4003edf42b0b0d455126e01c946e80cc5341a9f9750caf79500/), which was enacted at the boundary to epoch 614 with a DRep share of 86.5%, abstaining power excluded, and a pool share of 57.2% by stake.

The pool side also moved late. Across epochs 650 to 652 DReps cast 418 votes and stake pool operators 566, superseded votes included: a DRep or a pool that votes again replaces its earlier ballot, and both ballots count as votes cast. 305 of the pool votes were cast in epoch 652 alone, the last epoch in which either of the two closing actions could be voted on. As of that close the two pool shares differ, 56.2% on the committee update and 34.5% here.
