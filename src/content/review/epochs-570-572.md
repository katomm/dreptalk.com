## ₳1.5M to Amaru, and a first

[Amaru Treasury Withdrawal 2025](/ga/60ed6ab43c840ff888a8af30a1ed27b41e9f4a91a89822b2b63d1bfc52aeec4500/) was ratified at the start of epoch 570 and paid at the start of epoch 571. It had 74.53% of counted DRep power against a 67% bar, on 198 yes votes, 6 no and 11 abstain, and all seven interim committee members voted yes, as did all twelve of its largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 572. The ₳1.5M is the first entry in the record's list of enacted treasury withdrawals. Amaru appears again in [a later review](/governance-review/epochs-621-623/).

## 39 requests in one epoch

In epoch 570, 37 withdrawal requests were filed as the indices of a single transaction and two more on their own, 39 requests for ₳275.0M together. The spending period, epochs 532 to 612, has a ₳350M limit, and ₳348.5M of it remained at epoch 572 after the Amaru payment, since a request consumes nothing until it is paid. They ranged from ₳45,217 for core tool maintenance to ₳96.82M for core development, and every one of them was still open when the window closed, with the boundary to epoch 577 as their common deadline.

Most ballots of the window went to the 39. Across all actions, DReps cast 213 ballots in epoch 570, 2,350 in epoch 571 and 1,413 in epoch 572, and the 2,350 is the highest epoch count in the record from epoch 508 to 572. Stake pools, who do not vote on a treasury withdrawal, cast 14 ballots and committee members 5, for 3,995 in the window with superseded votes included, and none of the committee ballots was on the 39. The surviving ballots cast in epochs 570 to 572 came from 200 distinct DReps. Among the twelve largest DReps at epoch 572, [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) had voted yes on all 39 by the close, [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) on 38, [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) had voted on 38 and abstained on twelve of them, and [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) and [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) on none. The decisions are in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/) and the [review of epochs 576 to 578](/governance-review/epochs-576-578/).

## Two budget signals wait

Two information actions were open at the close, actions that record a signal, are never ratified or enacted and cannot themselves release treasury funds. [Cardano GovTool Budget - 12 months full active maintenance and development](/ga/9d213a57684d7ddf6f3350c80d042639ecbed5ccccc4a05bf54959a086593e7b00/), filed in epoch 567, asks for a year's maintenance budget for the governance tool, and [Tempo for Cardono Governance - Maintenance & Development Budget for 2025](/ga/495bd3cd44ea6bc0b4c2dbb1e4cdc7e214874c2c07490a4e2476a627ef91128000/), filed in epoch 569, for maintenance and development of the Tempo tool. The GovTool signal is followed in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/) and the Tempo signal in the [review of epochs 576 to 578](/governance-review/epochs-576-578/).

At epoch 572, 838 DReps held delegated power, up from 835 at epoch 570 and the highest count in the record from epoch 508, while their combined power fell from ₳5.31B to ₳5.26B. The count with a ballot in the last 12 epochs rose from 317 to 332. The treasury gained ₳6.8M between the epoch 570 and epoch 572 readings, the ₳1.5M payment included.
