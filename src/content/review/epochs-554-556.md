## Intersect's ₳350M limit closes above its 50% bar

[2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/) closed at the start of epoch 554 with 73.4% of DRep power behind it, on 201 yes ballots, 123 no and 13 abstain. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no. Yes ballots carried ₳3.57B of power and no ballots ₳0.92B. The action sets ₳350M as the most that treasury withdrawals may total from the start of epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604, the first such limit since on-chain governance began. By epoch 556 nothing had been withdrawn against it.

Intersect filed the action, and its document says the period was agreed with the Cardano Budget Committee. The document bases the amount on the treasury's income of the previous year, ₳336.0M over epochs 459 to 531, and sets its own bar at more than 50% of the active voting DRep stake, the figure the constitution sets. The two year alternative, ₳300M for 2025 and ₳250M for 2026, had closed short of that bar at the start of epoch 553, as the [review of epochs 548 to 553](/governance-review/epochs-548-553/) sets out. Both proposals state that a budget request made before a limit is agreed is unconstitutional.

## YUTA, SIPO and SASA vote yes, HOSKY no

[SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) voted yes, writing that the limit "is not a 'must spend' limit but a 'can't spend more than this' limit" and that the budget proposed by Intersect came in below it. AdaStat voted yes because the sum "does not actually mean all this money will definitely be taken", since DReps decide each withdrawal. The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) found ₳350M "higher than we would have preferred" and voted yes to get the budget process under way. [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) wrote that ₳350M gives the flexibility to respond to the business opportunities it expects regulatory developments to bring.

[YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the DRep with the most delegated power at epoch 556, voted yes in epoch 548 while preferring the two year alternative, because income in the current year is clearly lower than the year before. It wrote that no limit at all would be the worst outcome. [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) had opposed ₳350M in favour of the same alternative and changed its vote to yes in epoch 553, writing that after that alternative fell short a second failure "would effectively halt all budget-related actions" for the year.

HOSKY voted no, writing that a limit equal to a whole year of treasury income "sends the wrong message" and "risks framing it as a target rather than a safeguard". Cerkoryn voted no because a limit this high "risks incentivizing the community to withdraw funds up to the maximum". InputEndorsers voted no, writing that ₳350M is roughly 14,000,000 ada above the previous year's income and that a lower limit "would focus minds" in a first year when budget processes are at their weakest. [hix_coffeepool☕️](/dreps/drep1ygnh2uf4wkc8ldgfxwz7rzuga3m8jtqew9xh5g3n587mg6g3ge0sj/) abstained, writing that fewer withdrawals are better for decentralisation but that keeping unnecessary ada in the treasury is also unhealthy.

## Intersect Constitutional Council finds the 73 epoch span unconstitutional

Five committee members found the limit constitutional, for a committee share of 83.3%.
