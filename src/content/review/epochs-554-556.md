## The first ceiling

[2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/) closed at the start of epoch 554 with 73.35% of counted DRep power behind it, on 201 yes ballots, 123 no and 13 abstain, ₳3.57B of power for it against ₳0.92B. It puts a ceiling of ₳350M on what the treasury may pay out over 72 epochs, from the start of epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604, a period that was already more than twenty epochs old when the vote closed.

That backdating is not an oversight. The constitution forbids a budget request until a ceiling exists, so until this vote nothing could be asked for and nothing could be paid, and the period had to start where the budget year started. The ₳350M is not money committed to anything: it is the most that may leave the treasury before the end of epoch 604, and at this point none of it had. The figure itself was agreed with the Cardano Budget Committee and sized to what the treasury actually took in over the previous year, ₳336.0M across epochs 459 to 531, and the document names its own bar: more than 50% of the active voting DRep stake, the figure the constitution sets for a spending limit, which 73.35% clears with room to spare. Five committee members voted yes, one no and one abstained, for a share of 83.33%. The pools recorded 42 no ballots to 27 yes, which changes nothing, because a pool ballot carries no weight on an information action.

A late surge like this is a pattern the series returns to. A DRep with a large delegation can wait until the last epoch and still decide the outcome, which makes an early tally on any open proposal a weak guide to where it will end.

## Two rival ceilings, three budgets

With a limit in place, five information actions followed within two epochs, and they fall into two groups.

Two proposed different ceilings. [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/), filed in epoch 554, asks for ₳200M over exactly the period that had just been settled, from epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604, and states that where the two conflict it supersedes the earlier ceiling. Its argument is not that the ₳350M figure is wrong about income but that the first ceiling should be set low: the community had never paid core developers before, the budget and audit processes were still being worked out, and a limit for the following year could be raised once there was experience to go on. [Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563 to 635](/ga/637e3c256da9c1350847ca3211e8c44e3660a4471e8b6f68a8c537746b4aeb7300/) followed in epoch 556 with ₳300M for a period of its own that begins at epoch 563, which overlaps the one running to the end of epoch 604 rather than following it. Its reason is timing: the ceilings on the table only ran to the end of 2025, while the budgets being proposed were built on twelve month timelines that reach into 2026, and its text says it would replace any previous ceiling if agreed. Two ceilings covering the same epochs is a problem the series has to come back to.

Three asked to spend. [Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget: Amaru Node Development 2025](/ga/bd488931f792651fefa9c6fda185a2c6cec83245b51d994e33090ce36e29cc2600/) asks for ₳1.5M for the second half of 2025 to fund a second block producing node, written mainly in Rust under a not for profit association of several Cardano companies, so that the project does not depend on a single company for its funding, with the project's own maintainer committee as administrator and a smart contract to hold the money. [Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/4840e305563327358cf70dae5015b2df8f8c35cef03f74521d4f117ac17bc38400/) is for putting treasury funds to work as liquidity. [2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 7.5M ₳ for community builders](/ga/defbf15b06092718adf4befeab982e03d2966b9caeef93c19e470549ef75ea4900/) bundles the proposals from community builders that cleared ₳2.69B of support in the off chain poll, the bar its authors read the constitution as requiring, and leaves out the founding entities, on the argument that a builder who cannot put up the ₳100k deposit alone should not be shut out of the on chain vote. All five were still open at the close of epoch 556. The [review of epochs 557 to 560](/governance-review/epochs-557-560/) has the first result, and the [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/) the other four.

## Delegated power passes ₳5B

The power delegated to DReps passed ₳5B at epoch 554 and read ₳5.10B at epoch 556, the highest up to then, held by 818 DReps. The ten largest held 48.21% of it at epoch 554 and 48.1% at epoch 556. The window recorded 377 ballots, superseded votes included, 338 from DReps and 39 from pools, with final ballots from 191 DReps and 27 pools. The treasury read ₳1.75B at epoch 556, ₳8.7M above its epoch 554 reading and the highest up to then, with the whole ₳350M allowance still unspent.
