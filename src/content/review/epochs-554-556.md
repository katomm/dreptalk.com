## ₳350M, and the yes came last

[2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/) is one of the two actions the record lists as defining the ₳350M ceiling on treasury withdrawals for the period that began in epoch 532. The constitution requires such a limit before any withdrawal. An information action is never ratified or enacted, so the vote recorded support for the ceiling without itself authorising a withdrawal. It closed at the start of epoch 554 with 73.35% of counted DRep power behind it, on 201 yes votes, 123 no and 13 abstain. The final tally recorded ₳3.57B behind the yes ballots and ₳0.92B behind the no, and the share also counts power without a ballot and the always no confidence option on the no side. Five committee members voted yes, Intersect Constitutional Council voted no and Eastern Cardano Council abstained, five yes over seven active members minus one abstainer, or 83.33%. Of the twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 556, ten voted yes, one unnamed DRep voted no and [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) abstained.

The support arrived late. Yes ballots carried ₳1,702.7M of DRep power across epochs 547 to 552 and ₳1,894.0M in epoch 553, measured at each epoch and superseded ballots included. The pools, whose ballots carry no weight on an information action, recorded 27 yes, 42 no and one abstention.

## Five signals in two epochs

Five information actions were submitted in epochs 554 and 556. In epoch 554, the epoch the limit closed, [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/) was filed. According to its text it is a rival for the same period and argues for two hundred million ada instead, so that the treasury keeps growing while funding processes settle. In that epoch it received 111 yes ballots, 34 no and three abstentions across the roles. In epoch 556 four more arrived. [Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget: Amaru Node Development 2025](/ga/bd488931f792651fefa9c6fda185a2c6cec83245b51d994e33090ce36e29cc2600/) received 49 yes ballots, four no and three abstentions in that epoch, and [Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/4840e305563327358cf70dae5015b2df8f8c35cef03f74521d4f117ac17bc38400/) six yes, 23 no and six abstentions. [2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 7.5M ₳ for community builders](/ga/defbf15b06092718adf4befeab982e03d2966b9caeef93c19e470549ef75ea4900/) and [Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563 to 635](/ga/637e3c256da9c1350847ca3211e8c44e3660a4471e8b6f68a8c537746b4aeb7300/) completed the four. All five were open at the close. The rival limit is followed in the [review of epochs 557 to 560](/governance-review/epochs-557-560/), the other four in the [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/).
