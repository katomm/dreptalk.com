## ₳350M, and the yes came last

[2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/) closed at the start of epoch 554 with 73.35% of counted DRep power behind it, on 201 yes votes, 123 no and 13 abstain, and the support arrived late. Yes ballots carried ₳1,702.7M of DRep power across epochs 547 to 552 and ₳1,894.0M in epoch 553, measured at each epoch and superseded ballots included. The final tally recorded ₳3.57B behind the yes ballots and ₳0.92B behind the no.

The action is one of the two the record lists as defining the ₳350M ceiling on treasury withdrawals for the period that began in epoch 532, a period start that predates the vote. The constitution requires such a limit before any withdrawal. An information action is never ratified or enacted, so the vote recorded support for the ceiling without itself authorising a withdrawal. Five committee members voted yes, Intersect Constitutional Council voted no and Eastern Cardano Council abstained, a share of 83.33%. Of the twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 556, ten voted yes, one unnamed DRep voted no and [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) abstained. The pools, whose ballots carry no weight on an information action, recorded 27 yes, 42 no and one abstention.

## Five signals in two epochs

Five information actions were submitted in epochs 554 and 556, two of them about the ceiling and three about budgets. In epoch 554 [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/) was filed, a rival that according to its text proposes two hundred million ada for the same period, and it received 111 yes ballots, 34 no and three abstentions across the roles in that epoch. In epoch 556 [Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563 to 635](/ga/637e3c256da9c1350847ca3211e8c44e3660a4471e8b6f68a8c537746b4aeb7300/) proposed a ceiling for the period after this one. The three budgets arrived in epoch 556 as well: [Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget: Amaru Node Development 2025](/ga/bd488931f792651fefa9c6fda185a2c6cec83245b51d994e33090ce36e29cc2600/) received 49 yes ballots, four no and three abstentions in that epoch, [Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/4840e305563327358cf70dae5015b2df8f8c35cef03f74521d4f117ac17bc38400/) six yes, 23 no and six abstentions, and [2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 7.5M ₳ for community builders](/ga/defbf15b06092718adf4befeab982e03d2966b9caeef93c19e470549ef75ea4900/) was the third. All five were open at the close. The rival limit is followed in the [review of epochs 557 to 560](/governance-review/epochs-557-560/), the other four in the [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/).

## More than ₳5B

The power delegated to DReps first passed ₳5B at epoch 554, at ₳5.00B, and read ₳5.10B at epoch 556, held by 818 DReps. The ten largest DReps held 48.21% of it at epoch 554 and 48.1% at epoch 556. Epochs 554 to 556 saw 377 ballots, superseded votes included, 338 from DReps and 39 from pools and none from the committee, with final ballots from 191 DReps and 27 pools. At epoch 556 the treasury held ₳1.75B, ₳8.7M above its epoch 554 reading and the maximum for epochs 508 to 556, and the ₳350M allowance remained wholly unspent.
