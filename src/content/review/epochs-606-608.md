## Not one pool against, and it failed anyway

[Add Constitutional Committee Member - Christina](/ga/eb8ef28ca80dbd9ced6f577f013bdfc64897d1f4e326beb37a50fb6b62c38ccd00/) expired at the start of epoch 607. 97 stake pools voted yes on it, four abstained, and not a single pool voted no.

Pools do not vote by head count. What counts is the stake delegated to them, and those 97 pools controlled 17.42% of the counted pool stake against the 51% a committee change needs. Most pools simply did not vote, and a pool that does not vote does not sit outside the arithmetic: depending on what its reward account points at, its stake may count against, count as abstaining, or drop out of the denominator altogether. What the record shows here is that the stake behind the pools that did turn up was a sixth of what the vote needed.

The DRep side fell short too, at 53.84% of counted power against a 67% bar, so this is not a case of one body blocking another. Both requirements were missed, and only one of them would have been enough.

The proposal had asked for an eighth member so that the committee would not stop functioning if one of seven resigned, which is what had happened two windows earlier. That argument lost, and the committee stayed at exactly seven.

## The constitution passes on the third attempt

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.4](/ga/91a79f5c934b7c91e3027736d565080c2b6611fb8484b1156fdf16121fcfb41000/) was ratified at the start of epoch 608 with 79.85% of counted DRep power behind it, on 157 yes ballots to 24 no, and six committee members in favour with one against. It is the same revision that expired at 55.61% at epoch 581 and at 64.85% at epoch 593.

What it changes is worth repeating now that it has passed. The budget information action disappears as a separate step: a treasury withdrawal carries the requirements itself, so a project no longer needs a budget endorsed first and a payment approved second. Provisions the text calls aspirational rather than enforceable are deleted, among them codes of conduct for DReps, pool operators and the committee. Definitions arrive for terms the constitution had been using without them, such as active voting stake and net change limit. A proposal's documents must stay immutable after submission, which closes the gap a committee member had objected to earlier in the series. And the treasury audit safeguards apply to every withdrawal rather than to some. It took effect one epoch later, in the [review of epochs 609 to 611](/governance-review/epochs-609-611/).

## The strategy passes too, with a mission attached

[Cardano 2030: Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs](/ga/3a5be93bafd26d0dbb9bfe19f4acecaa38cef75e50201d19199e6cc856db660100/) closed at the start of epoch 608 with 67.8% of counted DRep power behind it, on 152 yes ballots to 26 no. The draft of the same strategy that the [review of epochs 588 to 590](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/) covers had closed far below that, with most of the delegated power abstaining. Two things changed in between: this version carries a mission, a strategy framework and measurable targets rather than a vision statement alone, and it comes from the product committee that ran the process rather than from a community member filing on its behalf. Which of the two mattered is not something the record answers.

## ₳70M out, and ₳500,000 refused

[Withdraw ₳70,000,000 for Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/f8393f1ff814d3d52336a97712361fed933d9ef9e8d0909e1d31536a549fd22f00/) was paid at the start of epoch 606, the money for stablecoin, custody, analytics, bridge and oracle integration work that the [review of epochs 603 to 605](/governance-review/epochs-603-605/) describes.

A much smaller request went the other way. [A treasury withdrawal without a title in the record](/ga/4b10e5793208cb8f228756e02113227c91602248eac4d992681a0ee760b6c4e200/) was filed in epoch 607 for ₳500,000, the first payment against the stablecoin liquidity budget endorsed at epoch 589, and all six committee members who voted found it unconstitutional. The published reason names two specific omissions: a withdrawal action has to say under what circumstances the money might be refunded to the treasury, and it has to disclose whether the recipient has taken ada from the treasury in the previous 24 months. This one did neither. The record holds no title for the action at all, and what it asks for comes from the document its anchor points at.

## Two proposals about the next upgrade

[Name Protocol Version 11 hard fork - van Rossem](/ga/8845bfc37bb2f69e8f200fe28148b3dea3c4399b0c49ee0ed2bb4e349cab9eb700/), filed in epoch 606, asks for the next hard fork to be named in memory of a community contributor, the same tradition that gave the last two upgrades their names. [Increase Transaction and Block Memory Units (Part 1 of 2)](/ga/c21b00f90f18fce4003edf42b0b0d455126e01c946e80cc5341a9f9750caf79500/) arrived in the same epoch and is the first half of a pair: it raises the memory a Plutus script may use in one transaction and in one block by a quarter, and its own text says a guardrail requires the change to be split across two linked actions. Both had deadlines at the start of epoch 613, and the [review of epochs 612 to 614](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/) has them.

## The numbers

The window recorded 717 ballots, 538 from DReps, 167 from pools and 12 from committee members, with final ballots from 146 distinct DReps and 62 pools. The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.62B at epoch 608 across 862 DReps, the ten largest holding 49.76% of it. The treasury reading rose ₳7.93M across the three epochs to ₳1.61B, having fallen by the ₳70M payment at the start of the first of them.
