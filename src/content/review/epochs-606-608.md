## 97 pools voted yes, and that was 17.42% of stake

Not one pool voted against [Add Constitutional Committee Member - Christina](/ga/eb8ef28ca80dbd9ced6f577f013bdfc64897d1f4e326beb37a50fb6b62c38ccd00/). The pools recorded 97 yes ballots and 4 abstentions, and the proposal still expired at the start of epoch 607, because yes stake reached 17.42% against a bar of 51%. The share measures stake rather than ballots, with abstaining stake left out and the stake of every pool that cast no ballot counted as no. A change to the committee's membership needs the DReps and the pools, and the committee itself has no vote on it.

DReps were short as well, at 53.84% of counted power against 67%, on 132 yes votes, 31 no and 15 abstain. They cast ₳2.88B of yes ballots and ₳1.24B of no, and the share also counts power without a ballot on the no side. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) opposed the addition and [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) abstained. The other nine of the twelve largest voters, measured by delegated power at epoch 608, supported it. The previous addition to the committee, [Add Constitutional Committee Member](/ga/4dab331457b61b824bbc6ba4b9d9be4750e25c0b5dd42207aeb63c7431a6b70400/), had taken effect at the start of epoch 602 on 79.38% of counted DRep power, which the [review of epochs 600 to 602](/governance-review/epochs-600-602/) covers.

## The strategy framework closes at 67.8%

[Cardano 2030: Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs](/ga/3a5be93bafd26d0dbb9bfe19f4acecaa38cef75e50201d19199e6cc856db660100/) is an information action seeking support for a vision, a strategy and measurable targets for the ecosystem, and it records a signal without enacting anything. It closed at the start of epoch 608 with 67.8% of counted DRep power behind it, on 152 yes votes, 26 no and 17 abstain. [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) was the only no among the twelve largest DRep voters, and the pools cast 47 yes ballots and none against, 8.39% of stake.

In the committee, Ace Alliance and Cardano Japan Council voted yes, Eastern Cardano Council and Tingvard abstained, and three members cast no ballot. Two yes votes over five members, the seven active minus the two abstainers, gave 40%. Eastern Cardano Council abstained on the strategy framework and cast the only committee no on both the withdrawal and the constitution.

DRep voting peaked in epoch 607 at 260 ballots. Across the three epochs the record counts 538 DRep ballots, 167 pool ballots and 12 committee ballots, superseded votes included, with final ballots from 146 DReps and 62 pools.

The [review of epochs 612 to 614](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/) follows three proposals filed in these epochs and still open at their end: a naming action for the next hard fork and a memory limit increase from epoch 606, and an untitled ₳500,000 withdrawal from epoch 607. DeltaDeFi and the limit proposal for the next spending period were collecting ballots as well, and the [review of epochs 609 to 611](/governance-review/epochs-609-611/) takes them up.
