## More DReps for it, less than half the power

[Net Change Limit of 300 Million ADA for Epochs 613 to 713](/ga/73a4eb2148781c37ef37c90a33a1d3d00511a8eefe9cdfaa1ea593b090f23f9600/) proposed a lower ceiling on what the treasury may pay out over the hundred epochs from 613 than the ₳350M the DReps had already agreed to, a decision the [review of epochs 612 to 614](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/) covers, and its own text says it would supersede any limit previously agreed for the period. Voting closed at the start of epoch 618. The tally was 118 yes votes, 42 no and 13 abstain, and the yes side represented 44.08% of counted DRep power.

Yes ballots carried ₳2.46B, more than the ₳1.71B behind the no ballots. But counted power also holds every delegated ada that cast no ballot at all and the always no confidence option, on the no side, while abstaining power is left out. Among the twelve largest DReps on its ballot list, ranked by delegated power at epoch 620, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) voted no, one abstained and eight voted yes. At epoch 620 those three held ₳1.28B in delegated power between them.

As an information action it recorded votes without being ratified or enacted on chain. Six committee members voted yes and one cast no ballot, a reading of 85.71%. Stake pools recorded 30 yes ballots, 2 no and 21 abstain, with the yes side at 5.51% of stake.

## DRep power reaches its highest recorded level

At epoch 618, the fourth epoch of the window, the power delegated to DReps stood at ₳5.87B, higher than at any point in the record that starts at epoch 508. It had been ₳5.83B at epoch 615 and was ₳5.83B again at epoch 620. At the peak 866 DReps held power, the same count as at epoch 615, and by epoch 620 that count had fallen to 860.

## Nothing paid, one ratification, five new requests

The treasury paid nothing in the six epochs, and its balance rose by ₳19.3M between the readings at epoch 615 and epoch 620, from ₳1.64B to ₳1.66B. Nothing had yet been drawn against the new spending period.

[Amaru](/ga/2f2fc2539dde550d3c83bea67c11b03861407976bbe8d07c5b0e124aa8a12baa00/) was ratified at the start of epoch 620. Its stored tally records 70.93% of counted DRep power, on 136 yes votes, 16 no and 8 abstain, with all twelve of the largest DReps on its ballot list voting yes. Five committee members voted yes, one abstained and one cast no ballot, a reading of 83.33%. The ₳10.1M withdrawal was ratified inside the window and had not been paid by its close.

Five proposals arrived during the window. Three sought treasury withdrawals totalling ₳57.7M: ₳800,000 for [Cardano Defi Liquidity Budget - Withdrawal 1](/ga/e5c4d7ebaad03ac127ec19a286605a188d6fea1c54e075c619ca6993b7fa9fd100/) and ₳6.9M for [Dingo: a Production-Grade Block Producer in Go by Blink Labs](/ga/f35285db3c4e085ad331843b3007737952b8a322bb3216311edc37fdf44ad3da00/) in epoch 617, then ₳50M for [Cardano x Draper Dragon: Orion Fund](/ga/8807e3cee3be5647742c329c1dea242f0c1c29b8ad050e15169e3b0302fcbfd600/) in epoch 618. The [Cardano Budget Process Framework (facilitated by Intersect)](/ga/e9693ed6b6465b2b46daaf641486a12b4b2946081634b5967b9d98897b0598fa00/) arrived in epoch 616 and [Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst](/ga/b518efa466593906ab388f978b3695449809aacdf28d2aaf4eb5ba16b3c24a1e00/) in epoch 619. All five remained open at the close.

Across the six epochs DReps cast 709 ballots, committee members 24 and pools 55, superseded votes included. The 788 ballots include final votes from 180 DReps and 21 pools.
