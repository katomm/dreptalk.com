## More DReps for it, less than half the power

[Net Change Limit of 300 Million ADA for Epochs 613 to 713](/ga/73a4eb2148781c37ef37c90a33a1d3d00511a8eefe9cdfaa1ea593b090f23f9600/) proposed a lower ceiling on what the treasury may pay out over epochs 613 to 713 than the ₳350M the DReps had already agreed to in the [window before](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/). Its own text says it would supersede any limit previously agreed for the period. Voting closed at the start of epoch 618. The tally was 118 yes votes, 42 no and 13 abstain, and the yes side represented 44.08% of counted DRep power.

Yes ballots carried ₳2.46B, more than the ₳1.71B behind the no ballots. The share is lower than either figure suggests because abstaining power leaves the denominator while delegated power that cast no ballot at all is counted against, and most delegated power did not vote. Among the twelve largest DReps on its ballot list, ranked by delegated power at epoch 620, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) voted no, one abstained and eight voted yes, and at epoch 620 those three held ₳1.28B in delegated power between them.

So does the ₳350M ceiling stand? Yes, and that is the answer the article owes the reader rather than the observation that information actions are not enacted. A ceiling has a bar of its own: the treasury guardrails require more than half of the active voting stake to agree one, and a published account of this vote names that requirement explicitly. At 44.08% the lower ceiling did not reach it, so the ₳350M agreed for epochs 613 to 713 remains the limit in force, and the six committee yes votes on this proposal change nothing about that.

## A high reading inside a narrow band

At epoch 618 the power delegated to DReps stood at ₳5.87B, the highest reading in the record up to then. It is a small movement in a flat stretch rather than an event: ₳5.83B at epoch 615, ₳5.87B at 618, ₳5.83B again at 620.

The movement inside the total was larger than the total itself suggests. The largest decline the record lists is the [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) DRep, down ₳56.7M from ₳188.0M to ₳131.3M between epochs 614 and 620. That is measured across the whole window rather than at the epoch of the high reading, so it describes the same period and not the same movement.

## Nothing paid, one ratification, five new requests

The treasury paid nothing in the six epochs, and its balance rose by ₳19.3M between the readings at epoch 615 and epoch 620, from ₳1.64B to ₳1.66B. Nothing had yet been drawn against the new spending period.

[Amaru Treasury Withdrawal 2026](/ga/2f2fc2539dde550d3c83bea67c11b03861407976bbe8d07c5b0e124aa8a12baa00/) was ratified at the start of epoch 620 with 70.93% of counted DRep power, on 136 yes ballots to 16 no, five committee members in favour and one abstaining. It asks ₳10.1M for a second year of work on Amaru, the independently built block producing node whose first ₳1.5M was the [first payment this record holds](/governance-review/epochs-570-572/). The step from ₳1.5M to ₳10.1M is the difference between funding the scopes that nothing else covered and funding a full year of the project. It was ratified inside this window and had not been paid at its close.

Five proposals arrived during the window and were still open at its close, among them three withdrawals asking for ₳57.7M together, the largest of them ₳50M for [Cardano x Draper Dragon: Orion Fund](/ga/8807e3cee3be5647742c329c1dea242f0c1c29b8ad050e15169e3b0302fcbfd600/) in epoch 618.

Across the six epochs DReps cast 709 ballots, committee members 24 and pools 55, superseded votes included. The 788 ballots include final votes from 180 DReps and 21 pools.
