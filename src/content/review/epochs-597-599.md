## Three things happened in order

The sequence matters more than any of the three figures in it.

At the boundary that opens epoch 597, [Loan ₳5,000,000 to Expand Cardano's Global Listings](/ga/03f671791fd97011f30e4d6b76c9a91f4f6bcfb60ee37e5399b9545bb3f2757a00/) was ratified, with 75.89% of counted DRep power behind it, five committee members voting yes and one abstaining. At the start of epoch 598 the ledger paid the ₳5M out. It is the first treasury request in this record structured as a loan rather than a grant, and worth being precise about: what the ledger did was pay ₳5M to an address. Being repaid with interest is a promise in the proposal, not an obligation the chain holds or enforces.

Between those two boundaries, inside epoch 597, a committee member resigned. The list of the seven serving members in the compensation proposal of the [review of epochs 594 to 596](/governance-review/epochs-594-596/) identifies the key that left as Cardano Atlantic Council.

## Why six is a problem

The loss of one member out of seven does not weaken the committee's opinion. It stops it counting. The protocol carries a parameter for the minimum committee size, set to seven, and while the number of active members is below it no governance action needing a committee ruling can be ratified at all. A treasury withdrawal, a parameter change, a constitution: all of them wait.

That gives the window a shape worth reading carefully. The loan was ratified before the resignation, so its payment at epoch 598 was already settled and went through. What had not yet cleared that boundary was now stuck. The data behind this edition flags the loan itself as decided by a committee below its minimum, which is the roster of epoch 599 applied to a check at epoch 597, and nothing here rests on that flag.

## The fix, and three more asks

[Add Constitutional Committee Member](/ga/4dab331457b61b824bbc6ba4b9d9be4750e25c0b5dd42207aeb63c7431a6b70400/) was filed in epoch 599 to put the number back to seven, with a member chosen by participating DReps in a snap election held over ten days. It asks pools as well as DReps, because a change to the committee always does, and its deadline was the start of epoch 606. The [review of epochs 600 to 602](/governance-review/epochs-600-602/) has the result.

Two budgets and an extension arrived in epoch 597. [Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/8f54d021c6e6fcdd5a4908f10a7b092fa31cd94db2e809f2e06d7ffa4d78773d00/) asks for funding for integration work, and [Withdraw ₳70,000,000 for Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/f8393f1ff814d3d52336a97712361fed933d9ef9e8d0909e1d31536a549fd22f00/) followed in epoch 599 as the payment against it. [2025 Net Change Limit Extension](/ga/d16dffbae9d86a73cb343506e6712d79c278096dc25e8ba6900eb24522726bba00/) asks to extend the spending period, which runs to the end of epoch 604 and would otherwise close with ₳73.0M of its ₳350M unused. As of epoch 599 the extension stood at 85.9% of counted DRep power, the strongest reading of any proposal in the window, and the [review of epochs 603 to 605](/governance-review/epochs-603-605/) has its result.

## Two smaller results

[Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit.](/ga/3285b7fd0da16d21e0b8f8910c37f77e17a57cfff8f513df4baf69295480108800/) closed at the start of epoch 597 with 68.16% of counted DRep power, on 158 yes ballots to 13 no. It asks the treasury to refund the deposit paid to submit one of the earliest proposals this series covered, the note of thanks the [first edition](/governance-review/epochs-508-513/) covers. [Securing  Generic Top-Level Domains for the Cardano Ecosystem](/ga/a36eafaea085b77f97cceacf07fe9450f8c6b47fec3af94da8f7d158a1fc972200/) closed at the same boundary with 73.83%, asking for internet domain endings, the part of an address after the last dot, to be secured for the ecosystem. Both are signals: neither moves money on its own.

## The numbers

The window recorded 684 ballots, 549 from DReps, 133 from pools and 2 from committee members, with final ballots from 231 distinct DReps and 107 pools. The pool figure follows the committee proposal, which pools vote on. The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.59B at epoch 599 across 855 DReps, the ten largest holding 49.40% of it, and the count with a recent ballot rose from 305 to 329. The treasury reading rose ₳2.6M to ₳1.65B, a smaller rise than usual because ₳5M left it in the same span, and ₳73.0M of the ₳350M ceiling remained.
