## One seat empty

According to the proposal filed in epoch 599 to replace it, Cardano Atlantic Council resigned from the committee in epoch 597, and at epoch 599 six members remained, three with terms running to epoch 653 and three to epoch 726. The departed member's ballots on the three decisions of this window, all cast in epoch 592, are left out of the committee tallies reported here.

Six is one short. [Add Constitutional Committee Member](/ga/4dab331457b61b824bbc6ba4b9d9be4750e25c0b5dd42207aeb63c7431a6b70400/), whose vote the [review of epochs 600 to 602](/governance-review/epochs-600-602/) covers, describes a snap election among DReps, puts the minimum size at seven and says the addition would let on-chain governance resume in full. While the committee is below that minimum, a constitution change, a parameter change, a hard fork or a treasury withdrawal cannot be ratified. A committee change and a motion of no confidence need no committee vote.

## The ₳5M loan

The loan was ratified at the boundary that opened epoch 597, before the resignation the replacement proposal describes. [Loan ₳5,000,000 to Expand Cardano's Global Listings](/ga/03f671791fd97011f30e4d6b76c9a91f4f6bcfb60ee37e5399b9545bb3f2757a00/) had 75.89% of counted DRep power against a 67% bar, from 151 yes votes, 38 no and 20 abstain. The proposal describes itself as the first loan based treasury withdrawal, money for exchange listings of a community token that is to be repaid with interest rather than granted. Five committee members voted yes and Ace Alliance abstained, a share of 100%. Among the action's twelve largest DRep voters by delegated power at epoch 599, [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) abstained, [Army of Spies](/dreps/drep1ygqgayvx8yzsaj9hprja3l6jy3v4px9z3u8uvecuvm3f92ce7mckx/) voted no and the other ten voted yes.

The ₳5M was paid at the start of epoch 598, the thirty-ninth withdrawal charged to the ₳350M limit of the current spending period. At epoch 599 the period had ₳277.0M used and ₳73.0M left. The treasury read ₳1.65B in epoch 597, ₳1.64B in epoch 598 and ₳1.65B in epoch 599, ₳2.6M more at the end than at the start.

## Two signals at the same boundary

[Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit.](/ga/3285b7fd0da16d21e0b8f8910c37f77e17a57cfff8f513df4baf69295480108800/) closed at the start of epoch 597 with 68.16% of counted DRep power behind it, on 158 yes votes, 13 no and 12 abstain. The proposal attributes a lost deposit to a bug in the node software weeks after on-chain governance began, on an early and symbolic information action, and asks that the submitter be reimbursed. Three committee members voted yes, Cardano Japan Council abstained and two cast no ballot, a share of 60%. Among the pools, 16 voted yes, 3.6% of stake.

[Securing  Generic Top-Level Domains for the Cardano Ecosystem](/ga/a36eafaea085b77f97cceacf07fe9450f8c6b47fec3af94da8f7d158a1fc972200/) closed at the same boundary with 73.83%, on 169 yes votes, 24 no and 11 abstain. The proposal text describes a request by the Cardano Foundation for support before it applies, with its own funds, to register two top level domains for the ecosystem in the next application window. Ace Alliance and Cardano Japan Council voted yes, three members abstained and one cast no ballot, a share of 66.67%. Among the pools, 15 voted yes, 3.52% of stake.

## ₳84.9M less power, 39 more voters

The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.65B at epoch 596 and ₳5.56B at epoch 597. The largest declines listed for epochs 596 to 599 are [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), from ₳645.2M to ₳617.3M, WaffleCapital, from ₳57.2M to ₳32.4M, and [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), from ₳493.2M to ₳479.9M. By epoch 599 the total had recovered to ₳5.59B.

The count of DReps with at least one ballot in the last 12 epochs went the other way, from 290 at epoch 596 to 329 at epoch 599, its highest since epoch 586. Across the window 231 DReps cast a final ballot, and DRep ballots reached 216 in epoch 599. With superseded votes included, the three roles cast 684 ballots, 549 from DReps, 133 from pools and 2 from committee members.

Two information actions were submitted in epoch 597, on a ₳70M budget for critical integrations and on an extension of the spending period, and a ₳70M withdrawal for that budget followed in epoch 599, the same epoch as the committee addition. All four were still collecting ballots at the close. The [review of epochs 603 to 605](/governance-review/epochs-603-605/) has the budget, the extension and the withdrawal.
