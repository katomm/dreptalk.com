## A question about how big a pool may get

[Should K increased?](/ga/7fd6429add8f2611ad8d48c0cc49101463093aec285faea402e8cfde78ea58d700/) arrived in epoch 514 and asked whether Cardano should raise its target number of stake pools. Dividing the total supply by that parameter gives the saturation point: above it, adding more stake no longer increases a pool's rewards, so delegators have a reason to move stake elsewhere. The proposal put the parameter at 500 at the time of writing. Raising it would lower the saturation point and encourage delegators to move stake to other pools, though not necessarily to different operators, and would also make a given pledge count for more in the reward calculation, which favours operators who can back their own pool with their own ada.

What the proposal did not do was ask for the change. Its own text says it is by no means a suggestion of what should be modified, and that the goal is to get people voting before the next hard fork and to show them which parameters governance can touch at all. An information action registers an opinion and never enacts anything, so this was a practice run by design, and the document closes by inviting readers to register as a DRep with their own wallet or to delegate to one. Across the roles it drew 59 yes ballots, 41 no and 8 abstain in its first three epochs, superseded ballots included, and the no side gained ground as it went: 5 yes to 1 no in epoch 514, then 24 yes to 22 no in epoch 516. No share is given here, because voting ran to the start of epoch 521.

The practice run was taken seriously. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) abstained in epoch 515 because the question could not be answered either way: a yes is dangerous, since a raise to 100,000 would be plainly unreasonable, and a no is dangerous, since there are cases where a raise is right and they need analysis with the other parameters. SIPO voted no in epoch 515 because the market, the regulatory outlook and energy costs already made running a pool hard, and hix_coffeepool☕️ because a parameter question should not be answered "without objective indicators". jonahkoch voted no because the parameter "should not be adjusted in isolation" from the pledge factor and the minimum pool cost. The yes votes were about saturated pools: Cerkoryn wrote in epoch 516 that the parameter sits at 500 while over 3,000 pools exist, and RABIT in epoch 514 that over 1,000 pools have minted a block, so 1,000 would be the number. The [review of epochs 517 to 522](/governance-review/epochs-517-522/) has the result.

## Most DReps still had not voted on anything

Participation was still limited. At epoch 516, 441 DReps held ₳561.1M of delegated power, up ₳73.6M from ₳487.5M at epoch 514 and the highest reading up to then, and 136 of them had a recorded ballot in the previous twelve epochs. The record lists 139 recently voting DReps in all, the most up to then and up from 122 at epoch 514, so participation was growing, from a low base and more slowly than delegation. Concentration moved the other way and then back: the ten largest DReps held 48.42% of the power at epoch 514, their smallest share up to then, and 50.59% at epoch 516.

## The naming proposal closes, and an older signal nears its end

[An information action without a title in the record](/ga/15f82a365bdee483a4b03873a40d3829cc88c048ff3703e11bd01dd9e035c91600/), the request to name the next hard fork after the HOSKY token that the [review of epochs 508 to 513](/governance-review/epochs-508-513/) covers, closed at the start of epoch 514. Its stored DRep counts were 11 yes, 93 no and 21 abstain, ₳8.8M of power for and ₳202.6M against, and pools recorded 6 yes ballots to 64 no. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no. All of its ballots fell before this window, YUTA's no among them, on the ground that a meme coin's name could lower the brand of the chain, and one pool's no on the ground that a hard fork named after a token might be read as advertising.

[Cardanoの生きがい - Ikigai -](/ga/59fd353253eb177e2104e8f23ea4c63e3d32ef95c7865d03e90d3884424dc1db00/), the note of thanks that asks for no change at all, had its last three epochs of voting here. As of the close of epoch 516 it stood at 41.42% of DRep power, on stored counts of 104 yes, 3 no and 16 abstain, with three committee yes votes and three abstentions as of that close. The last no came in epoch 516 from HeptaSean, who would not thank the founding entities and one founder unconditionally and called the text's wider ambitions "overpromising what a cryptocurrency can do". Among the committee, Input Output abstained in epoch 516 because "expressions of feeling are not a matter for constitutional adjudication", while the Cardano Japan Council voted yes in epoch 514 and added that actions which change nothing "could hinder the smooth operation of governance" and should one day be excluded. Of the 156 ballots cast in epochs 514 to 516, DReps supplied 92, pools 59 and committee members 5, with final ballots from 78 distinct DReps and 47 pools.
