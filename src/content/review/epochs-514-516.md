## A question about how big a pool may get

[Should K increased?](/ga/7fd6429add8f2611ad8d48c0cc49101463093aec285faea402e8cfde78ea58d700/) arrived in epoch 514 and asked whether Cardano should raise the parameter that decides how large a single stake pool may grow. Dividing the total supply by that parameter gives the saturation point: past it a pool earns no more rewards, so delegators have a reason to move on and stake spreads across more operators. The proposal put the parameter at 500 at the time of writing, for a saturation point of about ₳74.6M per pool. Raising it would lower that ceiling and split the same stake across more pools, and would also make a given pledge count for more in the reward calculation, which favours operators who can back their own pool with their own ada.

What the proposal did not do was ask for the change. Its own text says it is by no means a suggestion of what should be modified, and that the goal is to get people voting before the next hard fork and to show them which parameters governance can touch at all. An information action registers an opinion and never enacts anything, so this was a practice run by design. Across the roles it drew 59 yes ballots, 41 no and 8 abstain in its first three epochs, superseded ballots included, and the no side gained ground as it went: 5 yes to 1 no in epoch 514, then 24 yes to 22 no in epoch 516. Voting on it ran to the start of epoch 521, and the [review of epochs 517 to 522](/governance-review/epochs-517-522/) has the result.

## Most DReps still had not voted on anything

The practice was needed. At epoch 516, 441 DReps held ₳561.1M of delegated power, up ₳73.6M from ₳487.5M at epoch 514 and the highest reading up to then, but only 139 of them had cast a ballot in the previous twelve epochs. That count was itself the most up to then, up from 122 at epoch 514, so participation was growing, from a low base and more slowly than delegation. Concentration moved the other way and then back: the ten largest DReps held 48.42% of the power at epoch 514, their smallest share up to then, and 50.59% at epoch 516.

## The naming proposal closes, and an older signal nears its end

[An information action without a title in the record](/ga/15f82a365bdee483a4b03873a40d3829cc88c048ff3703e11bd01dd9e035c91600/), the request to name the next hard fork after the HOSKY token that the [review of epochs 508 to 513](/governance-review/epochs-508-513/) covers, closed at the start of epoch 514. Its stored DRep counts were 11 yes, 93 no and 21 abstain, ₳8.8M of power for and ₳202.6M against, and pools recorded 6 yes ballots to 64 no. All of its ballots fell before this window.

[Cardanoの生きがい - Ikigai -](/ga/59fd353253eb177e2104e8f23ea4c63e3d32ef95c7865d03e90d3884424dc1db00/), the note of thanks that asks for no change at all, had its last three epochs of voting here. As of the close of epoch 516 it stood at 41.42% of counted DRep power, on stored counts of 104 yes, 3 no and 16 abstain, with three committee yes votes and three abstentions as of that close. Of the 156 ballots cast in epochs 514 to 516, DReps supplied 92, pools 59 and committee members 5, with final ballots from 78 distinct DReps and 47 pools.
