## Atrium Lab asks whether to raise the pool target above 500

[Should K increased?](/ga/7fd6429add8f2611ad8d48c0cc49101463093aec285faea402e8cfde78ea58d700/) was put forward by Atrium Lab in epoch 514 and asked whether Cardano should raise its target number of stake pools. The parameter sets the saturation point of a pool: a pool can take more stake, but its rewards stop increasing beyond that point. The document puts that point at about ₳74.6M per pool, with the parameter at 500 when it was written, and adds that a higher value gives smaller pledges more effect on rewards. The document also says the action is by no means a suggestion of what the parameters should be or whether they should change now, and gives its goal as more participation in governance before the next hard fork. It lists no authors, referring readers instead to "our proposal thread" on Atrium Lab's X account.

In epochs 514 to 516 it drew 59 yes ballots, 41 no and 8 abstain across the roles, superseded ballots included, and its vote ran until the start of epoch 521. RABIT voted yes in epoch 514, writing that over 1,000 pools have minted at least one block, so the parameter should be raised to 1,000 to encourage delegators of saturated pools to move to smaller ones. jonahkoch voted no in epoch 515, writing that the parameter should not be "adjusted in isolation", since it works together with the pledge influence factor and the minimum pool cost to set the desired number of pools.

[YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) abstained in epoch 515, writing that the question could not be answered either way: a yes is dangerous because a raise to 100,000 would be plainly unreasonable, and a no is dangerous because there are cases where a raise is right, which need analysis together with the other parameters. SIPO voted no in epoch 515, writing that the market, the regulatory outlook and energy costs already made running a pool hard. hix_coffeepool☕️ also voted no, writing that a parameter question should not be answered "without objective indicators". Cerkoryn voted yes in epoch 516, writing that the parameter sits at 500 while over 3,000 pools exist.

## The HOSKY naming proposal closes at 1.9%

[Name the next hard fork HOSKY Hard Fork](/ga/15f82a365bdee483a4b03873a40d3829cc88c048ff3703e11bd01dd9e035c91600/), the joke proposal the [review of epochs 508 to 513](/governance-review/epochs-508-513/) describes, closed at the start of epoch 514 with 1.9% of DRep power behind it. Its stored DRep counts were 11 yes, 93 no and 21 abstain, ₳8.8M of power for it and ₳202.6M against. Pools recorded 6 yes ballots to 64 no. On the committee the Cardano Atlantic Council found it constitutional in epoch 510 and the Intersect Constitutional Council in epoch 512, while the Eastern Cardano Council abstained in epoch 513.

## Ikigai stands at 41.4% at the close

[Cardanoの生きがい - Ikigai -](/ga/59fd353253eb177e2104e8f23ea4c63e3d32ef95c7865d03e90d3884424dc1db00/), the note of thanks to Input Output under Charles Hoskinson, EMURGO and the Cardano Foundation that seeks no change on chain or off it, stood at 41.4% of DRep power as of the close of epoch 516, the last epoch of its vote, on 104 yes ballots, 3 no and 16 abstain. HeptaSean voted no in epoch 516, writing that it thanks the founding entities and Hoskinson personally without condition. On the committee the Cardano Japan Council found it constitutional in epoch 514, and the Cardano Foundation and the Intersect Constitutional Council in epoch 515. The Cardano Atlantic Council had abstained in epoch 513 and the Eastern Cardano Council abstained in epoch 515. Input Output abstained in epoch 516, writing that "expressions of feeling are not a matter for constitutional adjudication".

## Delegation to DReps rises to ₳561.1M

At epoch 516, 441 DReps held ₳561.1M of delegated power, ₳73.6M more than the ₳487.5M of epoch 514 and the highest reading since on-chain governance began. Over the same two readings the count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 122 to 139, also the most up to then. The ten largest DReps held 48.4% of the delegated power at epoch 514, their smallest share up to then, and 50.6% at epoch 516. The window recorded 156 ballots with superseded votes included, 92 from DReps, 59 from pools and 5 from committee members, and final ballots from 78 distinct DReps and 47 pools.
