## The pools vote, the DReps wait

[Hard Fork to Protocol Version 10 ("Plomin" Hard Fork)](/ga/0b19476e40bbbb5e1e8ce153523762e2b6859e7ecacbaf06eae0ee6a447e79b900/) was submitted in epoch 529 and was the only proposal open in the window. In the bootstrap phase, which the [review of epochs 535 to 537](/governance-review/epochs-535-537/) sees end, DRep votes did not count on a hard fork. The record holds no DRep ballot on any action in epochs 529 to 534, and in the epoch rows from 509 the only earlier epoch without one is 521. Across pools and committee members the hard fork received 584 yes ballots in epochs 529 to 534, with 5 no and 35 abstain, superseded ballots included, 32 of the abstentions in epoch 534 alone. All but two of the yes ballots came from stake pools, the two from committee members who voted yes in epoch 534. Voting was scheduled to end at the start of epoch 536, and the [review of epochs 535 to 537](/governance-review/epochs-535-537/) has its result.

The name it carries had been put to a signal that closed at the start of epoch 529. [Rename the Chang 2 Hard Fork to the Plomin Hard Fork](/ga/fff0df644d328a5367212f45bab59060bde3c4091dc96c723062896fd619731400/), a signal submitted in epoch 522, closed at the start of epoch 529 with 76.54% of counted DRep power behind it, on stored DRep counts of 209 yes, 9 no and 8 abstain. As an information action it recorded support without being ratified or changing anything on chain by itself. Yes ballots carried ₳1.71B of DRep power, no ballots ₳0.003B and abstentions ₳0.12B. Six committee members voted yes and Eastern Cardano Council abstained, and all 95 recorded pool ballots were yes, a yes stake share the record puts at 7.17%. [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) was the sole abstainer among its twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 534, the other eleven voted yes. According to its text, the signal asked whether the second upgrade of the Chang series should carry the name of a core developer who had died earlier that year and was known in the community as Plomin. The [review of epochs 526 to 528](/governance-review/epochs-526-528/) covers its last three epochs of voting.

## DRep power grows through six epochs without a DRep ballot

DReps held ₳2.72B at epoch 534, ₳417.0M more than the ₳2.30B of epoch 529, and 657 of them held some of it, up from 599. Both closing readings were highs up to then. The count of DReps with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs went the other way, from 198 to 180, and 479 of the 657 DReps with power at epoch 534 had cast no ballot in that span. Some recent voters no longer held delegated power, so the two counts describe different groups. The ten largest DReps held 44.73% of the power at epoch 529 and 46.47% at epoch 534, and the Gini measure of inequality among DReps holding power, 0.9173 at epoch 534, was the highest up to then.

The treasury gained ₳22.4M between the epoch 529 and epoch 534 readings, reaching ₳1.65B, and the record lists no enacted withdrawal. The window's 624 ballots, superseded votes included, were 622 from pools and 2 from committee members, with final ballots from 619 distinct pools and none from a DRep.
