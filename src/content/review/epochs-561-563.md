## One majority in five

Five information actions closed in these three epochs, and only one drew more than half of counted DRep power. [The Amaru budget](/ga/bd488931f792651fefa9c6fda185a2c6cec83245b51d994e33090ce36e29cc2600/) closed at the start of epoch 563 with 80.37%, on 232 yes votes, 14 no and 10 abstain. Yes ballots amounted to ₳4.32B and no ballots to ₳0.03B. Six committee members voted yes and one abstained, and 32 pools voted yes with none against. The withdrawal request that followed is in the [review of epochs 564 to 569](/governance-review/epochs-564-569/) and its payment in the [review of epochs 570 to 572](/governance-review/epochs-570-572/).

Two of the other four were alternatives to the existing ₳350M ceiling on treasury withdrawals. [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/), which according to its text asked for two hundred million ada for the current period, closed at the start of epoch 561 with 43.88% of counted DRep power, on 213 yes votes, 85 no and 11 abstain, with yes ballots amounting to ₳2.35B and no ballots to ₳2.17B. [Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563 to 635](/ga/637e3c256da9c1350847ca3211e8c44e3660a4471e8b6f68a8c537746b4aeb7300/), three hundred million ada for a period beginning in epoch 563, closed at the start of epoch 563 with 37.26%, on 109 yes votes, 87 no and 19 abstain. The committee found both constitutional, six yes votes and one abstention each.

## Committee opposition to two budgets

[The community builders budget](/ga/defbf15b06092718adf4befeab982e03d2966b9caeef93c19e470549ef75ea4900/) closed at the start of epoch 563 with 34.49% of counted DRep power, on 103 yes votes, 66 no and 25 abstain, and all seven committee members voted no. In its last epoch of voting, 562, the no ballots amounted to ₳1.01B of DRep power against ₳0.16B of yes. [Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/4840e305563327358cf70dae5015b2df8f8c35cef03f74521d4f117ac17bc38400/) closed at the same boundary with 4.05%, on 10 yes votes, 135 no and 28 abstain, with four committee members voting no, one abstaining and two casting no ballot. Its text describes a fund of fifty million ada for stablecoin liquidity in decentralised finance, an idea that returned in a later window as [a new budget signal](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/). 

The committee votes on whether an action is constitutional, and on an information action its finding is recorded and nothing more.

## The aggregate budget in its last epoch

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/), which its text calls the aggregate budget for the first spending round, was scheduled to end its voting at the start of epoch 564 and was still open as of the close of epoch 563. The [review of epochs 564 to 569](/governance-review/epochs-564-569/) has its result. Among the budget signals decided here, Amaru drew majority support while the community builders and liquidity budgets drew both lower DRep support and committee opposition.

No treasury withdrawal had been paid by epoch 563, and the period's ₳350M ceiling, set by two earlier actions and not by the proposals closing here, remained unused. The treasury read ₳1.78B at epoch 563, ₳8.5M above epoch 561. DReps cast 374 of the window's 449 ballots, committee members 32 and pools 43, superseded votes included, and the 194 DRep ballots of epoch 562 were the most in a single epoch up to then, a count of ballots rather than of distinct voters. Final ballots came from 151 DReps and 17 pools. The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳5.32B to ₳5.37B, with 826 DReps holding some of it.
