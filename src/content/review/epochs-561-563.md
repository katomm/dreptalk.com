## One majority in five

Five information actions closed in these three epochs, and only one drew more than half of counted DRep power. [The Amaru budget](/ga/bd488931f792651fefa9c6fda185a2c6cec83245b51d994e33090ce36e29cc2600/) closed at the start of epoch 563 with 80.37%, on 232 yes votes, 14 no and 10 abstain. Yes ballots amounted to ₳4.32B and no ballots to ₳0.03B, and the counted share also puts power without a ballot on the no side. All twelve of its largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 563, voted yes, six committee members voted yes and one abstained, and 32 pools voted yes with none against. The [review of epochs 570 to 572](/governance-review/epochs-570-572/) picks the Amaru thread up again.

The other four fell well short of half. Two of them proposed a net change limit, the ceiling the constitution requires before any treasury withdrawal. [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/) closed at the start of epoch 561 with 43.88% of counted DRep power, on 213 yes votes, 85 no and 11 abstain, with yes ballots amounting to ₳2.35B and no ballots to ₳2.17B. Among its twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 563, [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) and [CryptoCrow](/dreps/drep1ytfwpmt2fvdnyvlswyjyggpf8alwkpm7ha6cua9nsak525ssmvm57/) voted yes, [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/), [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) and [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted no. [Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563 to 635](/ga/637e3c256da9c1350847ca3211e8c44e3660a4471e8b6f68a8c537746b4aeb7300/), a second limit proposal for a period beginning in epoch 563, closed at the start of epoch 563 with 37.26%, on 109 yes votes, 87 no and 19 abstain. The committee found both constitutional, six yes votes and one abstention each.

## Committee opposition to two budgets

On two of the five the committee said no. [The community builders budget](/ga/defbf15b06092718adf4befeab982e03d2966b9caeef93c19e470549ef75ea4900/) closed at the start of epoch 563 with 34.49% of counted DRep power, on 103 yes votes, 66 no and 25 abstain, and all seven committee members voted no. In its last epoch of voting, 562, the no ballots amounted to ₳1.01B of DRep power against ₳0.16B of yes. [Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/4840e305563327358cf70dae5015b2df8f8c35cef03f74521d4f117ac17bc38400/) closed at the same boundary with 4.05%, on 10 yes votes, 135 no and 28 abstain, with four committee members voting no, one abstaining and two casting no ballot. Its text describes a fund of fifty million ada for stablecoin liquidity in decentralised finance, an idea that returned in a later window as [a new budget signal](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/). Among its twelve largest DRep voters at epoch 563, [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) and [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted no and [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) voted yes.

The committee votes on whether an action is constitutional. An information action is never ratified, so the finding is recorded and nothing more, and among the five closed actions only the community builders and liquidity budgets carry committee no votes.

## The aggregate budget in its last epoch

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/), which its text calls the aggregate budget for the first spending round, had the boundary to epoch 564 as its deadline. At the boundary to epoch 564 the tally as of that close showed 65.4% of counted DRep power in favour, six committee yes votes and one abstention. The [review of epochs 564 to 569](/governance-review/epochs-564-569/) continues with it.

The record shows no enacted treasury withdrawal by epoch 563 and none of the ₳350M limit for epochs 532 to 612 consumed, a limit set by two earlier actions and not by the proposals closing here. The treasury reading at epoch 563 was ₳1.78B, ₳8.5M above epoch 561 and the maximum in the record covering epochs 508 to 563. DReps cast 374 of the window's 449 ballots, committee members 32 and pools 43, superseded votes included, with 194 DRep ballots in epoch 562 alone, the highest epoch count in the record up to then. Final ballots came from 151 DReps and 17 pools. The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳5.32B to ₳5.37B, also a maximum for epochs 508 to 563, with 826 DReps holding some of it.
