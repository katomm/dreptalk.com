## 261 DReps, ₳134M

The epoch statistics of the record begin at epoch 508. According to the governance design, DRep registration, delegation and voting had begun one epoch earlier, and the record also holds an information action and ballots from epoch 507. At epoch 508, 261 DReps held ₳134.3M of delegated power between them, and the count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs stood at 54. By epoch 513, the last of the six epochs covered here, 419 DReps held ₳457.3M, ₳323.0M more, and the recent voter count read 115. The delegated power, the number of DReps holding it and the recent voter count rose at every reading from epoch 508 to 513. The ten largest DReps held 53.36% of the power at epoch 508, 54.24% at epoch 510, the highest share up to then, and 50.26% at epoch 513.

## No ballots outnumber yes on the older signal as the ikigai signal opens

[An information action without a title in the record](/ga/15f82a365bdee483a4b03873a40d3829cc88c048ff3703e11bd01dd9e035c91600/), submitted in epoch 507, collected more no than yes ballots in every epoch of the window: 4 yes, 24 no and 6 abstain in epoch 508, 3 yes, 44 no and 4 abstain in epoch 509, and 9 yes to 102 no over the six epochs, ballots cast across all three roles with superseded ballots included. Cardano Atlantic Council voted yes in epoch 510, Intersect Constitutional Council yes in epoch 512 and Eastern Cardano Council abstained in epoch 513. Its closing boundary is covered in the [review of epochs 514 to 516](/governance-review/epochs-514-516/).
