## Delegation more than triples

The epoch statistics of the record begin at epoch 508, one epoch after the upgrade that let stake holders register as DReps, delegate their voting power to one and vote. At that first reading 261 DReps held ₳134.3M of delegated power between them. Six epochs later, at epoch 513, there were 419 of them holding ₳457.3M, ₳323.0M more, and both figures had risen at every reading in between. The single largest step was between epochs 509 and 510, when the delegated total gained ₳134.1M, as much again as the whole of epoch 508.

Participation grew with it, though more slowly. The count of DReps with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs went from 54 at epoch 508 to 115 at epoch 513, so most of the new DReps had not voted on anything yet. Concentration eased a little as the pool of DReps widened: the ten largest held 53.36% of the delegated power at epoch 508 and 54.24% at epoch 510, the highest share up to then, then 50.26% at epoch 513.

## Two proposals, neither of which changes anything

Both proposals open in this window were information actions. An information action registers an opinion and is never ratified, and in this phase of governance it was one of only three kinds of proposal that could be submitted at all, alongside parameter changes and hard fork initiations. Everything the reader might expect from governance at this point, treasury payments, committee changes, a constitution, was not yet possible.

[An information action without a title in the record](/ga/15f82a365bdee483a4b03873a40d3829cc88c048ff3703e11bd01dd9e035c91600/), submitted in epoch 507, asked for the next hard fork to be named after the HOSKY token, which it calls the most widely recognised and held asset in the ecosystem, on the argument that numbering releases around the name Chang confuses users and the media. The record stores no title for it, because the document its anchor points at does not match the hash the proposal registered on chain, and what it asked for is read from that document rather than from the snapshot. DReps did not take to it. As of the close of epoch 513 its stored counts were 11 yes, 93 no and 21 abstain, and its 1.89% of counted DRep power reflects the power behind those ballots rather than their number. No ballots outnumbered yes in every epoch of its run, 44 to 3 in epoch 509 at the widest. Its voting was scheduled to end at the start of epoch 514, and the [review of epochs 514 to 516](/governance-review/epochs-514-516/) covers that boundary.

[Cardanoの生きがい - Ikigai -](/ga/59fd353253eb177e2104e8f23ea4c63e3d32ef95c7865d03e90d3884424dc1db00/), submitted in epoch 510, is the opposite kind of proposal. It thanks the organisations that brought Cardano into the world, restates what the community set out to do, and says in as many words that it seeks no change on chain or off it, asking DReps and pools to vote yes if they share the sentiment and no if they do not. It drew 50 yes ballots and 3 abstentions across the roles in the epoch it arrived, more ballots than any other epoch of this window saw on anything, then 44 yes, 3 no and 10 abstain over epochs 511 to 513. Its voting ran to the start of epoch 517, past this window, and the [review of epochs 514 to 516](/governance-review/epochs-514-516/) follows it.

## The window in ballots

DReps cast 49 recorded ballots in epoch 510, the epoch the note of thanks arrived, and 21, 20, 24, 16 and 16 in the other five. Across the six epochs that is 146 DRep ballots, 89 from pools and 4 from committee members, 239 in all with superseded votes included, and the final ballots came from 101 distinct DReps and 67 pools. The treasury held ₳1.56B at epoch 513, ₳22.9M more than at epoch 508, and no withdrawal had been paid out of it, because none could yet be proposed.
