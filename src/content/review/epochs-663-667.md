## EMURGO and Yoroi back PFMS and abstain on other treasury requests

[Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), the second largest DRep, and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/), the eighth largest, wrote in epoch 664 that they had announced their deregistration as DReps for 30 November.

Yoroi wrote that it finds it inappropriate to encumber the treasury "with discretionary commitments that outlive our active term", and that for the rest of its mandate it will abstain on treasury requests unless an action demonstrates "an extraordinary, immediate strategic necessity". EMURGO wrote that its policy is "to abstain on all discretionary funding actions" and to reserve a yes for rare initiatives that meet the same test. On those grounds both abstained on AURORA in epoch 664.

In the same epoch both voted yes on [PFMS](/ga/72491608dc1a500d38613214069a2bd3b7e9a0e670d5abd7d6374e6bb54fc0e800/), which asks ₳9.6M for Trivolve Tech to build an evidence management system for police forces, with the Cardano Foundation as administrator. EMURGO wrote that it sees the request as "a high-impact strategic imperative" for Cardano's institutional footprint. Yoroi wrote that "the strategic scale, proven operational track record, and structured treasury return warrant our support". Neither rationale mentions the deregistration.

TokenPost reported on 28 October that EMURGO asked delegators of both DReps to delegate to another DRep before 30 November, and to look for consistent voting records and public explanations when choosing one. TokenPost added that the date could be extended in special circumstances.

Rewards from staking keep accruing. They can only be withdrawn once a stake credential has delegated its vote, to a DRep or to the always abstain or always no confidence option.

## Yes votes on the copper reserve held 0.5% of DRep power at close

[The copper reserve](/ga/6eddf4f1dbad06f329aa69743559c4930b7b689f16c073061d228d9c6613d19c00/) asks ₳22M for Toto Finance, including ₳14,724,409 to buy copper, which it would tokenize as CuTT. The document names Toto Finance as both recipient and administrator and says an independent oversight provider is still to be appointed. Voting on it ran until the start of epoch 668, so the tally below is as of the close of epoch 667.

It had 5 yes votes, 126 no and 8 abstain, and 0.5% of DRep power, against the 67% a treasury withdrawal needs. Yes votes carried ₳19.2M and no votes ₳2.86B.

The [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted no, writing that the proposal "places 22,000,000 ada under the recipient's sole control" and that the money would leave before any safeguard is in place.

[SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) voted no and wrote that it had backed treasury investments designed to return capital where the funds sat under independent controls, while here "Fund control rests with the recipient alone." [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) voted no, writing that the proposal gives insufficient evidence of prospective users, demand and plans for token liquidity. Yoroi and EMURGO abstained in epoch 664, citing their deregistration.

Three committee members, Tingvard, Ace Alliance and Leandros BSP, found it unconstitutional, and the other four had not voted by the close of epoch 667. Leandros BSP wrote that the proposal's document sits on an ordinary web server and is not content-addressed, so it is not immutable as the constitution requires.
