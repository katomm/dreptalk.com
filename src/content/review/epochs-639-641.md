## ₳51.3M for the plumbing

Three withdrawals were paid across these three epochs, ₳51.3M together. [Cardano Critical Integrations V2](/ga/c05eed09c8706453c97c88d403c4ff43b7786a552fe8711aa07d3e6529ffd8ba00/) took ₳23M at the boundary to epoch 639, [Tweag](/ga/17e43ffe4b2e0df1787d54f21ac66643c74abb131a326272fceecbdbbdd0d3fc00/) ₳18.3M at the boundary to epoch 641 for core infrastructure, and [5am.earth Trust Layer Targeting Vision 2030 KPIs](/ga/aaa6d9ccb72639c77db46b73bec2eaff9e5fcdfa04909cf1a0f7855a4d31485a00/) ₳10M at the boundary to epoch 640.

None of them passed comfortably. The DRep bar for a withdrawal is 67% of counted power, and the three came in at 69.2%, 70.1% and 70.6%. The committee was steadier: seven of its members voted yes on the 5am.earth withdrawal, five yes with two abstaining on the critical integrations one, and five yes with two casting no ballot at all on Tweag. No committee member voted no on any of the three.

The Tweag payment was a second attempt. A first request from the same team, for more than twice the amount over two years instead of one, had expired in epoch 635 with almost nothing behind it, which is told in the [review of epochs 633 to 635](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/). The second came in at less than half the sum and cleared the bar.

The one request that did not make it was smaller than any of them. [Rare Evo](/ga/3f4b022c9913d52f9f39a8c82a1b9ebe5143fb745f3531f9366c7268bf9b9ce400/) asked for ₳2.75M to sponsor a conference and reached 36.3% of counted DRep power. The committee split evenly on it, three yes against three no, the only even split of the window.

## The ballots go the other way

DReps cast 420 ballots in epoch 639, 549 in epoch 640 and 636 in epoch 641, the busiest of the three, and 156 DReps cast a final ballot across the window. Across all three bodies together, 1,925 ballots were cast, superseded votes included.

Of everything still being voted on when epoch 641 closed, two had their deadline right there, at the boundary to epoch 642. [Hydra](/ga/4f6e5df9a6e14bd49e406e2bbb0d108b3be2be64b9569b930053a0c1eebe0d4700/) stood at 74.3% of counted DRep power as of that close, and [Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5](/ga/c75bb221606687aa858ec89c7a15c88e9c17054f2e045ae31ecc8a9687cd206e00/) at 80.1% against its own 75% bar.

## ₳211M leaves the DReps

While that was being decided, delegation drained. The power behind DReps fell from ₳5.56B at epoch 639 to ₳5.35B at epoch 641, a fall of ₳211M in three epochs, and the count of DReps holding any power fell from 892 to 883.

The steepest individual falls the record ranks are measured across a slightly wider span, from epoch 638 to epoch 641. Over it [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) went from ₳687.3M to ₳592.3M and [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) from ₳477.8M to ₳428.5M. Two more left the list entirely: P, a DRep that had held ₳66.0M, ended the span with no delegation at all, and an unnamed DRep with ₳38.4M deregistered.

[Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) are still the two largest DReps at the end of the window, at ₳592.3M and ₳428.5M. The top of the list got smaller without changing hands.
