## ₳51.3M paid, and what it buys

Three withdrawals were paid across these three epochs, ₳51.3M together, and they fund three quite different things.

[Tweag Core Cardano Infrastructure: Treasury Withdrawal 2026 to 2027](/ga/17e43ffe4b2e0df1787d54f21ac66643c74abb131a326272fceecbdbbdd0d3fc00/) took ₳18.3M at the boundary to epoch 641, and it is the one closest to the protocol itself. Its main deliverable is bringing a faster finality mechanism to the main network, cutting the time before a transaction can be treated as settled from roughly twelve minutes to about two, alongside work on letting a node run without the whole chain history, which lowers what a stake pool has to store, and on conformance testing for that and for the parallel block production work.

[Cardano Critical Integrations V2](/ga/c05eed09c8706453c97c88d403c4ff43b7786a552fe8711aa07d3e6529ffd8ba00/) took ₳23M at the boundary to epoch 639, the second tranche of the integration fund for stablecoins, custody, analytics, bridges and oracles that an [earlier edition](/governance-review/epochs-603-605/) describes.

[5am.earth Trust Layer Targeting Vision 2030 KPIs](/ga/aaa6d9ccb72639c77db46b73bec2eaff9e5fcdfa04909cf1a0f7855a4d31485a00/) took ₳10M at the boundary to epoch 640, and is the one that needs explaining rather than summarising. It would build a record on Cardano that agricultural supply chains can be checked against: identity credentials that farmers and local agricultural entrepreneurs control themselves, plus satellite data about farms and crops written on chain as a verifiable source. Its text describes an eighteen month programme across three countries with three named application paths, certification, traceability and credit, and a target of half a million registered farmers.

None of the three passed comfortably. A withdrawal needs 67% of counted DRep power, and these came in at 69.2%, 70.1% and 70.6%. No committee member voted no on any of them.

The Tweag payment was a second attempt. A first request from the same team, over two years rather than one and for more than twice the amount, had expired in epoch 635 with almost nothing behind it, as the [review of epochs 633 to 635](/governance-review/epochs-633-635/) records. The second asks for one year and less than half the sum, and it cleared the bar. Whether the shorter scope is what changed minds is not something any voter published.

The one request that did not make it was smaller than any of them. [Rare Evo](/ga/3f4b022c9913d52f9f39a8c82a1b9ebe5143fb745f3531f9366c7268bf9b9ce400/) asked for ₳2.75M to sponsor a conference and reached 36.3% of counted DRep power. The committee split three yes against three no, which did not clear its two thirds threshold.

## More ballots, less delegation

DReps cast 420 ballots in epoch 639, 549 in epoch 640 and 636 in epoch 641, the busiest of the three, and 156 distinct DReps cast a final ballot across the window, out of 883 holding power at its close. Across all three bodies 1,925 ballots were cast, superseded votes included. A rising ballot count alongside falling delegation is two measures of different things moving apart, not evidence that participation broadened: the ballot count follows how many requests were open, and eighteen were.

Of everything still being voted on when epoch 641 closed, two had their deadline right there, at the boundary to epoch 642. [Hydra](/ga/4f6e5df9a6e14bd49e406e2bbb0d108b3be2be64b9569b930053a0c1eebe0d4700/) stood at 74.3% of counted DRep power as of that close, and [Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5](/ga/c75bb221606687aa858ec89c7a15c88e9c17054f2e045ae31ecc8a9687cd206e00/) at 80.1% against its own 75% bar.

## Delegation to DReps falls ₳211M

The power delegated to DReps fell from ₳5.56B at epoch 639 to ₳5.35B at epoch 641, ₳211M across the three epochs, and the count of DReps holding any of it fell from 892 to 883. This is a reading of where stake is pointed. It is not a record of ada being sold, of money leaving Cardano or of anyone protesting: stake that stops pointing at a DRep has moved to another DRep, to one of the predefined options, or to nothing at all, and the totals do not say which.

The steepest falls the record ranks for single DReps are measured across a wider span, epochs 638 to 641, so they describe the same stretch as the fall in the total and not the same movement. Over that span [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) went from ₳687.3M to ₳592.3M and [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) from ₳477.8M to ₳428.5M. Two more dropped off the ranking for different reasons: one that had held ₳66.0M ended the span with no delegation while remaining registered, and another with ₳38.4M deregistered as a DRep. Those are not the same thing, and only the second is someone giving up the role.

The two largest DReps at the end of the window are still the same two, at ₳592.3M and ₳428.5M. The top of the list shrank without reordering.
