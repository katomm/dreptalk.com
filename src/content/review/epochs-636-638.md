## The most uneven the record had seen

By the end of epoch 638 the power delegated to DReps was spread more unevenly than at any point in the record up to then, which starts at epoch 508. The Gini coefficient, a single number for how far a distribution sits from being equal, reached 0.9396 there, its highest value in the series to that point. The ten largest DReps held 47.9% of delegated power at epoch 636 and 49.2% at epoch 638, a rise of 1.3 points in three epochs.

Two other measures moved in the opposite direction over the same three epochs. The count of DReps holding power with no ballot in the previous twelve epochs fell from 566 to 558, and the count with a ballot in that span rose from 326 to 337. These are measurements of different things: the Gini figure describes how delegated weight is spread, the other two describe how many DReps have voted recently, and neither tells you how the DReps at any size of holding voted. Any reading of the three together would take the ballots broken down by DRep weight, which this edition does not do.

DReps cast 1,236 ballots across the window, 366 in epoch 636, 377 in epoch 637 and 493 in epoch 638. The committee added 38 and the stake pools 144, for 1,418 in all, superseded votes included.

## One withdrawal paid, four sent away

The treasury paid out once. [Cardano Vision](/ga/5ad10ad39f49a0f93ac91e70482f114fdbcbc05b20ac774dbe23a82efbcb820a00/) drew ₳32.9M for a research programme on scaling and post quantum work, with 74.96% of counted DRep power behind it on 167 yes votes, 29 no and 15 abstain. It was ratified in epoch 636 and paid at the start of epoch 637. Between the treasury readings for epoch 636 and epoch 638 the balance fell by ₳25.5M, from ₳1.52B to ₳1.49B.

[Cardano Critical Integrations](/ga/c05eed09c8706453c97c88d403c4ff43b7786a552fe8711aa07d3e6529ffd8ba00/) was ratified in epoch 638 on 69.2% of counted power, ₳23M whose payment is due one epoch later, outside this window.

[Eternl](/ga/9a020ea7a6a0d813ff08c92bd8300b26077ae69a4b827c4d432ac665120325d700/) came closest at 56.8% of counted power for ₳1.68M. [The first node in the browser](/ga/4705a3e4e2b3837c370774c357e31fb30aa02279ff8d34a770d855f905e502dc00/) asked ₳4.6M to put a Cardano node inside a web browser and finished at 50.05%, on 89 yes votes, 60 no and 33 abstain. [Scalus](/ga/e0900fddf52f1f2f6439fa8a3aaa8c224ab59b6074d0e2db3b27c8f06b0e5d3300/) asked ₳8.5M for an application platform and reached 12.6% on 29 yes against 98. [Cardano dOSPO](/ga/0b3c83b5e62a63603948c247b47cfd012279756fa38cf73b0c6c19ce63f0868c00/) asked ₳12M and finished at 2.0%, on 14 yes against 118.

The Constitutional Committee, which votes only on whether an action is constitutional, found no fault with the browser node, seven votes to none, or with Scalus, five yes and one abstention with the seventh member casting no ballot at all, which is why its committee reading is 83.33% rather than a clean sweep. On the other two it went the other way: four no votes against three on Eternl, and a three to three split with one abstention on dOSPO, leaving both under the two thirds a withdrawal needs there.

## The cost model change that cleared by 1.57 points

[Update Plutus Cost Models](/ga/c82f3834898e4d70d3605fa0d92ffe31345701075b107a54309c1525f9581f6200/) was the other thing enacted in this window. Plutus cost models assign a computation and memory cost to every operation a script can perform. Those costs determine how many execution units a script consumes, and so what a transaction pays in fees. Repricing them changes what is affordable to build, in either direction: this change reprices some operations that already existed and its own text does not claim the effect is uniformly cheaper.

What it does, specifically, is three things. It prices the new operations that the coming hard fork will make available, so that they can be used the moment the protocol version rises. It enables the full set of operations in the two older versions of Plutus as well as the current one, where before some were reachable only in the newest. And it changes the settings for some operations already in use. The first part waits for the hard fork, the third takes effect on enactment.

It was ratified in epoch 637 and took effect in epoch 638, on 68.57% of counted DRep power from 120 yes votes, 4 no and 7 abstain, with five committee members finding it constitutional and two casting no ballot at all, a committee reading of 71.43%.

The four opposing DRep ballots carried ₳1.86M between them, nowhere near enough to hold a share down to 1.57 points above the bar. The rest of the counted power outside the yes side is power that voted on nothing, together with power parked on the always no confidence option, both of which count against. Which of the two dominates is not something this edition measures.

## Eternl comes back the same epoch

[Eternl: Path to Sustainability (2026-2027)](/ga/9a020ea7a6a0d813ff08c92bd8300b26077ae69a4b827c4d432ac665120325d700/) did not wait to expire before its successor arrived. [Eternl: Path to Sustainability - v2](/ga/fbb8d1a4a8d6b62f8cd706944a0582b884c2b90187b8fada7953d5c6a33eb5a700/) was submitted in epoch 637 while the first was still being voted on, and the first ran out at the start of epoch 638 at 56.8%.

The second version asks for more, not less: ₳2.35M against ₳1.68M, for the same twelve months of running a wallet that people use for payments, staking, governance and interacting with applications, a cost the proposal puts at about 420,000 dollars a year. A request denominated in work and quoted in ada moves with the ada price, and a resubmission at a higher figure in ada is not necessarily a larger ask in what it buys. Whether anything else in the scope changed is not something the record shows.

[5am.earth Trust Layer Targeting Vision 2030 KPIs](/ga/aaa6d9ccb72639c77db46b73bec2eaff9e5fcdfa04909cf1a0f7855a4d31485a00/) asked for ₳10M and its voting ran to the boundary out of epoch 638, standing at 70.63% of counted DRep power as of that close.
