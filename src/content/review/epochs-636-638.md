## The committee said yes, and it made no difference

[The first node in the browser](/ga/4705a3e4e2b3837c370774c357e31fb30aa02279ff8d34a770d855f905e502dc00/) asked the treasury for ₳4.6M to put a Cardano node inside a web browser. Every member of the Constitutional Committee who cast a ballot on it voted yes, seven of them, with nothing against and no abstentions. When the voting closed at the start of epoch 636 the DReps had put it at 50.05% of counted power, on 89 yes votes, 60 no and 33 abstain. The bar for a withdrawal is 67%. It expired at the start of epoch 636.

[Scalus](/ga/e0900fddf52f1f2f6439fa8a3aaa8c224ab59b6074d0e2db3b27c8f06b0e5d3300/) went the same way with a wider gap. It asked for ₳8.5M to build an application platform, collected five committee yes votes and one abstention with nothing against, and finished at 12.6% of counted DRep power on 29 yes votes against 98. One committee member cast no ballot at all on it, which is why its committee reading is 83.33% rather than a clean sweep: a member who stays silent is not an abstainer, and lowers the share.

The two bodies are not answering the same question. The committee checks an action against the Constitution. The DReps decide whether the thing is worth doing, whether the price is right and whether the treasury should be paying for it now. Those tests overlap wherever the Constitution sets requirements on treasury spending, but a committee yes is not a recommendation to fund, and a tally says nothing about why the DReps refused.

The committee is perfectly capable of saying no on its own. [Eternl](/ga/9a020ea7a6a0d813ff08c92bd8300b26077ae69a4b827c4d432ac665120325d700/) asked for ₳1.68M and drew four committee no votes against three yes, the only request decided in this window with more committee opposition than support. The DReps put it at 56.8%, the closest any of the four failures came to the bar. Eternl had already submitted a second version in epoch 637, before the first one ran out at the start of epoch 638.

[Cardano dOSPO](/ga/0b3c83b5e62a63603948c247b47cfd012279756fa38cf73b0c6c19ce63f0868c00/) drew the lowest DRep support of the window. It asked for ₳12M and finished at 2.0% of counted power on 14 yes votes against 118, with the committee split three yes against three no and one abstention, which left it at 50% and short of its own two thirds bar.

## One payment, ₳32.9M

The treasury paid out once in these three epochs. [Cardano Vision](/ga/5ad10ad39f49a0f93ac91e70482f114fdbcbc05b20ac774dbe23a82efbcb820a00/) drew ₳32.9M for a research programme on scaling and post quantum work, with 74.96% of counted DRep power behind it on 167 yes votes, 29 no and 15 abstain, and a unanimous seven yes from the committee. It was ratified in epoch 636 and paid at the start of epoch 637. Between the treasury readings for epoch 636 and epoch 638 the balance fell by ₳25.5M, from ₳1.52B to ₳1.49B.

Two more were still in motion when the window closed. [Cardano Critical Integrations](/ga/c05eed09c8706453c97c88d403c4ff43b7786a552fe8711aa07d3e6529ffd8ba00/) was ratified in epoch 638 on 69.2% of counted power, ₳23M whose payment is due one epoch later, outside this window.

[5am.earth Trust Layer Targeting Vision 2030 KPIs](/ga/aaa6d9ccb72639c77db46b73bec2eaff9e5fcdfa04909cf1a0f7855a4d31485a00/) asked for ₳10M and its voting closed exactly at the boundary out of epoch 638, standing at 70.63% of counted DRep power as of that close.

## The cost model change that cleared by 1.57 points

Between the arguments about money sat [Update Plutus Cost Models](/ga/c82f3834898e4d70d3605fa0d92ffe31345701075b107a54309c1525f9581f6200/). Plutus cost models assign a computation and memory cost to every operation a script can perform. Those costs determine how many execution units a script consumes, and so what a transaction pays in fees. Repricing them changes what is affordable to build.

It was ratified in epoch 637 and took effect in epoch 638, on 68.57% of counted DRep power from 120 yes votes, 4 no and 7 abstain, with five committee members in favour and two casting no ballot at all, a committee reading of 71.43%.

The four opposing DRep ballots carried ₳1.86M between them, nowhere near enough to hold a share down to 1.57 points above the bar. Almost all the counted power outside the yes side was power that cast no ballot at all, together with power delegated to the always no confidence option. Abstaining power is left out of the sum entirely.

## Power gathers while more DReps vote

By the end of epoch 638 the power delegated to DReps was spread more unevenly than at any point in the record that starts at epoch 508. The Gini coefficient, a single number for how far a distribution sits from being equal, reached 0.9396 there, its highest value in the whole series. The ten largest DReps held 47.9% of delegated power at epoch 636 and 49.2% at epoch 638, a rise of 1.3 points in three epochs.

Concentration is usually read as the small DReps going quiet, and these three epochs do not support that reading. The count of DReps holding power with no ballot in the previous twelve epochs fell from 566 to 558, while the count that had voted in that span rose from 326 to 337. Both come from a measure the data pack marks as flagged rather than exact. Taken as they stand, more DReps had voted recently at the same time as power was gathering at the top.

DReps cast 1,236 ballots across the window, 366 in epoch 636, 377 in epoch 637 and 493 in epoch 638. The committee added 38 and the stake pools 144, for 1,418 in all, superseded votes included.
