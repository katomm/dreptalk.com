## The tax stays where it was

Every epoch, a protocol parameter sets the share of the reward pot that goes to the treasury before the rest is paid out as staking rewards. [Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%](/ga/941502b0aa104c850d197923259444d2b57cab7af18b63143775465aaacc84f500/) proposed to halve it, arguing in its text that the rate had been set at launch and never revisited and that a lower one would improve staking rewards. It was the only parameter change to reach a decision in this window, and it expired at the start of epoch 546 with 23.22% of counted DRep power behind it, 43.78 points short of the 67% bar, on 140 yes votes, 223 no and 11 abstain. Yes ballots amounted to ₳0.91B of DRep power and no ballots to ₳2.02B.

A change to this economic parameter needs the DReps and the committee, while stake pool approval is needed only for security relevant parameters, and the committee was against as well: Cardano Atlantic Council and one other member voted yes, Cardano Foundation, Eastern Cardano Council, Intersect Constitutional Council, Emurgo and a fifth member voted no, two yes over seven active members, or 28.57%. Of the twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 547, seven voted no, three yes and two abstained. The no voters were [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), [BTBF_XSP](/dreps/drep1y2vyrqh0fa4qh4tmy7322va6ryk78uh6szppezaft0watyqp5hdwx/), [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/), [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/), [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) and [Hermes Stakepool Germany (HRMS)](/dreps/drep1ygs5hngva3hymv7j4v0aewxpsjqskj9dnmy7j3jz5ven5zqvulq2t/). [CryptoCrow](/dreps/drep1ytfwpmt2fvdnyvlswyjyggpf8alwkpm7ha6cua9nsak525ssmvm57/), [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) and [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) voted yes, [Blockdaemon](/dreps/drep1y2nvdau7g7xt6dlh9wydy9c4fvve24rr67twf5w63axu56q4rypfz/) and [Army of Spies](/dreps/drep1ygqgayvx8yzsaj9hprja3l6jy3v4px9z3u8uvecuvm3f92ce7mckx/) abstained. Voting peaked in epoch 540, when 69 no ballots carried ₳813M of DRep power against ₳312M behind 49 yes ballots. In epoch 545, its last epoch of voting, it drew 8 yes ballots, 33 no and 4 abstentions.

## Two limit proposals

The constitution requires a net change limit before any treasury withdrawal, and no proposal for one had yet been decided. In epoch 546 [Set 2025 Net Change Limit of 300M ADA, 2026 Net Change Limit of 250M ADA](/ga/7f320409d9998712ff3a3cdf0c9439e1543f236a3d746766f78f1fdbe1e06bf800/) was filed, proposing limits for two years at once, and in epoch 547 [2025 Net Change Limit](/ga/9b62b3c632f329016a968ac25211825bb4f84b12461121c7da3aa11df92370f900/) followed, which the record lists among the actions that define the ₳350M limit of the period beginning in epoch 532. Both were information actions, which are never ratified or enacted and authorise no withdrawal by themselves. Across the roles, the two year proposal drew 19 yes ballots, 14 no and one abstention in epoch 546 and 25 yes, 17 no and two abstentions in epoch 547, and the ₳350M proposal 8 yes and 4 no in epoch 547. The [review of epochs 548 to 553](/governance-review/epochs-548-553/) follows both, together with [Defining the Cardano Vision and Roadmap for 2025 and beyond](/ga/56f39054758f1a3cedc1de9225d66bf270b62dfdbfbc5399f1d6d43aceffc63600/), which remained open at the close with its voting scheduled to end at the start of epoch 549. The [review of epochs 554 to 556](/governance-review/epochs-554-556/) has the closing of the ₳350M proposal.

## 400 recent voters, and a smaller top ten share

The count of DReps with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 391 at epoch 545 to 400 at epoch 547, the highest up to then. The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳3.89B to ₳4.10B and 807 DReps held some of it at epoch 547, both highs up to then, and the ten largest DReps held 41.43% of it at epoch 546, the lowest share up to then. The smaller top ten share did not mean a flatter distribution overall: the Gini measure of the whole spread reached 0.9203 at epoch 547, its highest up to then. The window recorded 247 ballots, superseded votes included, 216 from DReps, 25 from pools and 6 from committee members, and final ballots came from 155 DReps and 14 pools. The treasury read ₳1.71B at epoch 547, ₳8.6M above its epoch 545 reading.
