## The treasury pays ₳44.2M, and seven requests run out

The treasury paid twelve withdrawals across these five epochs, ₳44.2M together, and seven others reached the end of their voting windows with nothing. The bar for a withdrawal is 67% of counted DRep power, and the field arrived at it with a gap in the middle: the closest of the seven that ran out stood at 63.0%, and the lowest share behind a payment was 68.4%.

Half of the twelve say maintenance in their own titles, four of them for TxPipe tooling at ₳540,750 each, one for hardware wallets and one for MLabs core tools. The rest are named for what they build or run. [Withdraw 3,810,423 ada for Mithril Protocol](/ga/b3d452bff7769d7f557ec6b8974760ee6c5e496c276652b654032966621e0ccf05/) went through at 79.3%, [IO: Hydra](/ga/4f6e5df9a6e14bd49e406e2bbb0d108b3be2be64b9569b930053a0c1eebe0d4700/) at 74.3%, and the largest single payment, ₳25.4M for governance coordination and technical work, at 75.7%.

The seven that ran out have less in common than a budget round suggests. The largest was [Cardano Builder DAO](/ga/4b7abfa70d98858750c50cd651d54c2955303b5410b34f40eb259f2fffc7c93900/), ₳20M at 11.0% of counted power, and next to it [Alchemy](/ga/0f75dd11be0b7a6cb4b305a175b17cf4b60cd307c052fccabf9f572950e7058300/) at ₳10M and 5.3%. [Blockfrost](/ga/5439b6141625436ccf600f910bb0b3301b6288933a2cdf7939758848ae8b999700/) asked for ₳9.8M to turn itself into a not-for-profit and reached 43.3%. The one that came closest to the bar was a request for ₳4.0M to bring real world payments to the chain, at 54.4%, on 68 yes votes against 61. [Strike Finance Liquidity Deployment](/ga/8721696358acdd43e34e5ed9ef1b3e2a1d2af9c1aa1972e017b9b9271b7ddc7000/) had the tightest head count of the whole window, 65 yes against 64 no, and still reads as 20.5% of power. A head count that close next to a share that far from the bar is the DRep denominator at work: each ballot carries its delegated power, and everything that stayed silent counts as no.

The smallest of the seven was a ₳103,000 reimbursement of a governance action deposit, and at 63.0% it is the one that came nearest to passing.

## A lower minimum for the committee

The minimum size of the Constitutional Committee fell from seven members to five at the start of epoch 643. [Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5](/ga/c75bb221606687aa858ec89c7a15c88e9c17054f2e045ae31ecc8a9687cd206e00/) had been ratified one epoch earlier with 80.1% of counted DRep power behind it, on 137 yes votes, 22 no and 17 abstain, against a 75% bar, and six committee members voted for it with none against.

The minimum decides when the committee stops being able to hold up its end. With fewer seats filled than the minimum, an action that needs a committee vote cannot be ratified at all, whatever the DReps and the pools do. A motion of no confidence and a change to the committee itself stay possible, because those are the two the committee does not vote on. Seven members were active at the end of epoch 646, exactly the old minimum and two above the new one. What changed is how many seats can fall vacant before the rest of the agenda stops.

## The hard fork clears the pool bar by 1.4 points

[Hard Fork to Protocol Version 11 ('van Rossem' Hard Fork)](/ga/fdd468da5cc4ac8431dcd7e2b3211666c73bc229f85879469f67f1d9d51d344d00/) was ratified at the boundary to epoch 643 and took effect one epoch later. On the DRep side it was as close to unanimous as this record gets, 180 yes votes against a single no and 4 abstain, 77.6% of counted power against a 60% bar.

The pool side was the tight one. 383 pools voted yes, none voted no, 48 abstained, and the reading was still 52.4% of stake against a 51% bar. On a hard fork every pool that does not vote counts as no, and unlike other action types nothing a pool's reward account delegates can change that. The upgrade went through with 1.4 points to spare.

## The count of voting DReps keeps falling

The count of DReps that had cast a ballot in the last 12 epochs fell from 341 at epoch 642 to 318 at epoch 646, and the power delegated to DReps as a whole, whether they voted or not, fell by ₳115.7M over the same stretch, a figure that leaves out the always abstain and always no confidence options.

The window was busy all the same. Across the five epochs 2,553 ballots were cast across DReps, stake pool operators and the committee together, superseded votes included, and 229 DReps cast a final ballot in it, each counted once. A budget round of this size pulls in voters that a quiet window does not, and the twelve payments came out of exactly that.
