## AdaStat, EMURGO and Yoroi vote yes on Intersect's budget

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/) closed at the start of epoch 564 with 65.4% of DRep power behind it, on 193 yes ballots to 63 no and 13 abstain, ₳3.51B of power for it against ₳0.71B. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no. The budget, proposed on behalf of Intersect and the Cardano Budget Committee, lists 39 proposals worth ₳275,269,340 that all name Intersect as administrator, and each of them still needs a treasury withdrawal action of its own. Six committee members found the budget constitutional, and Input Output noted that its affiliate Input Output Engineering has a proposal in it. The Cardano Japan Council abstained, writing that Intersect is "both the recipient and the overseer of a significant portion" of the budget and that this limits oversight of the funds Intersect keeps.

AdaStat wrote that the step "mostly adds delays and confusion" and voted yes "to let projects move forward". [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) called it "a constitutionally required checkpoint" whose passage is a prerequisite for any withdrawal, and [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) pointed to the multisig wallets, milestone tracking and due diligence under Intersect as administrator. The [review of epochs 557 to 560](/governance-review/epochs-557-560/) quotes the earlier ballots of YUTA, the Eternl DRep Committee, CardanoYoda and SASA.

## Dave, Cerkoryn and hix_coffeepool vote no

Dave voted no, writing that the signalling vote was "not conducted fully on-chain" and that bundling 39 proposals "forces DReps into a binary, all-or-nothing choice". Cerkoryn found ₳275M "large for a first cycle", and hix_coffeepool☕️ wrote that large withdrawals in a short time could harm the value of ada.

## Amaru asks for ₳1.5M under its node budget

[Amaru Treasury Withdrawal 2025](/ga/60ed6ab43c840ff888a8af30a1ed27b41e9f4a91a89822b2b63d1bfc52aeec4500/) was filed in epoch 566 for ₳1.5M, the first treasury withdrawal request since on-chain governance began. It draws on the ₳1.5M budget for Amaru that closed at the start of epoch 563, covered in the [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/), and not on Intersect's budget, which excludes every proposal that went on chain on its own. Amaru is a block producing node for Cardano under PRAGMA, and the request lists four members of its maintainer committee as authors, Matthias Benkort, Arnaud Bailly, Pi Lanningham and Damien Czapla.

It splits the sum across five contract addresses: ₳300,000 for the ledger and overall architecture, ₳300,000 for consensus and simulation testing, ₳500,000 for ad hoc work and middleware integrations, ₳100,000 for project management, public relations and marketing, and ₳300,000 as a contingency reserve, a quarter of the other four together. Its text says the scope owners themselves are paid from outside the withdrawal. Since contributor payroll would run out within weeks and the project's own treasury contract was still being audited, the funds would first be held in a contract Sundae Labs developed, audited by MLabs and TxPipe.

[YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) voted yes in epoch 566, writing that the request "directly corresponds to the DRep-approved roadmap" and that its cost breakdown "appears efficient". EMURGO voted yes in epoch 567, calling it "a groundbreaking milestone for funding an alternative node implementation", and Yoroi in epoch 568, writing that an alternative node reduces reliance on a single Haskell-based node. TriangleForces voted no in epoch 567, finding pay of 200,000 dollars a year per full time equivalent "disproportionate when benchmarked against industry standards".

[Tempo for Cardono Governance - Maintenance & Development Budget for 2025](/ga/495bd3cd44ea6bc0b4c2dbb1e4cdc7e214874c2c07490a4e2476a627ef91128000/), filed in epoch 569, asks for ₳380,000 over six months for Tempo.Vote, a voting app for phones that runs its own node and indexer, to be administered by Selfdriven. Voting ends at the start of epoch 574 on the first and at the start of epoch 576 on the second.

## Delegation to DReps falls ₳279.5M in epoch 565

The power delegated to DReps reached ₳5.47B at epoch 564, the highest since on-chain governance began, and read ₳5.19B at epoch 565, ₳279.5M less. Across epochs 563 to 569 the total fell ₳104.9M, while the power delegated to YUTA fell ₳311.1M, from ₳714.4M to ₳403.3M, the largest decline for any DRep over those epochs. The readings give YUTA's power only at those two epochs and do not show which delegations moved or where to. The ten largest DReps held 47.1% of the power at epoch 564 and 44.3% at epoch 565.

## The treasury reaches ₳1.80B with no withdrawal paid

No action was open in epochs 564 and 565, and no ballot was cast in either. Across the window DReps cast 316 ballots, pools 10 and committee members 8, 334 in all with superseded votes included, and 194 distinct DReps cast a final ballot. The treasury reading rose ₳21.1M to ₳1.80B by epoch 569, the highest since on-chain governance began, and none of the ₳350M the ceiling allows through the end of epoch 604 had been paid out.
