## The budget, then the silence

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/) bundles, according to its text, dozens of proposals for maintenance, development, marketing, innovation and governance work over a one year term. The withdrawals that draw on it had still to be filed as separate requests, which the [review of epochs 570 to 572](/governance-review/epochs-570-572/) covers. Voting closed at the start of epoch 564 with 65.4% of counted DRep power behind it, on 193 yes votes, 63 no and 13 abstain. Yes ballots carried ₳3.51B and no ballots ₳0.71B, and the share also counts power without a ballot on the no side. Six committee members voted yes and one abstained. The information action itself authorised no treasury payment. Among the twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by power at epoch 569, only [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) voted no.

Then, for two epochs, nothing. No proposal was open for a DRep to vote on in epochs 564 and 565, and the record shows no DRep ballot in either. Voting resumed in epoch 566 with 56 ballots, and the window's 334 ballots, superseded votes included, were 316 from DReps, 10 from pools and 8 from committee members, with final ballots from 194 DReps.

## ₳279.5M leaves in an epoch

The power delegated to DReps stood at ₳5.47B at epoch 564, the highest reading up to then, and at ₳5.19B at epoch 565. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) had the largest listed decline between epochs 563 and 569, from ₳714.4M to ₳403.3M. Between epochs 564 and 565 the share of the ten largest DReps fell from 47.14% to 44.3%, while the fewest DReps needed to hold half of the power rose from twelve to fourteen. Over the same epoch the power delegated to the always abstain option rose from ₳6.40B to ₳6.82B, and the readings do not show whether power moved between YUTA, other DReps and the default option. By epoch 569 the total had recovered, unevenly, to ₳5.26B.

The count of DReps with a ballot in the preceding twelve epochs fell from 367 at epoch 564 to 308 at epoch 568, then read 310 at epoch 569.

## A ₳1.5M request

The spending period covering epochs 532 to 612 had consumed none of its ₳350M limit by epoch 569, and the record lists no enacted withdrawal. In epoch 566 [Amaru Treasury Withdrawal 2025](/ga/60ed6ab43c840ff888a8af30a1ed27b41e9f4a91a89822b2b63d1bfc52aeec4500/) was submitted, a request for ₳1.5M, and in that epoch it received 53 DRep yes ballots, 1 no and 2 abstain, with all seven committee members voting yes on it by epoch 569. Two budget signals for governance tools followed, [Cardano GovTool Budget - 12 months full active maintenance and development](/ga/9d213a57684d7ddf6f3350c80d042639ecbed5ccccc4a05bf54959a086593e7b00/) in epoch 567 and [Tempo for Cardono Governance - Maintenance & Development Budget for 2025](/ga/495bd3cd44ea6bc0b4c2dbb1e4cdc7e214874c2c07490a4e2476a627ef91128000/) in epoch 569. The request is followed in the [review of epochs 570 to 572](/governance-review/epochs-570-572/), the governance tool signal in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/) and the second tool signal in the [review of epochs 576 to 578](/governance-review/epochs-576-578/).

Between the epoch 564 and epoch 569 readings the treasury grew ₳21.1M to ₳1.80B, its highest reading up to then.
