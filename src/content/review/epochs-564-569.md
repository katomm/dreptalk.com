## A budget is not a payment

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/) closed at the start of epoch 564 with 65.4% of counted DRep power behind it, on 193 yes ballots, 63 no and 13 abstain, ₳3.51B of power for it against ₳0.71B, with six committee members voting yes and one abstaining. Not one ada moved as a result.

That is the design, not a delay. A budget names what may be funded and by whom it will be administered, and being paid is a second, separate act: a treasury withdrawal action for a specific amount to a specific address, which DReps and the committee vote on in its own right. Nothing can be withdrawn that the budget did not cover, and nothing the budget covers is withdrawn until someone asks.

## The first request

[Amaru Treasury Withdrawal 2025](/ga/60ed6ab43c840ff888a8af30a1ed27b41e9f4a91a89822b2b63d1bfc52aeec4500/) was filed in epoch 566 for ₳1.5M, the first withdrawal request this record holds. What it buys is set out scope by scope, each with an owner and its own address: ₳300,000 for the ledger and the overall architecture, ₳300,000 for consensus and simulation testing, ₳500,000 for ad hoc work and middleware integrations, ₳100,000 for project management and marketing, and ₳300,000 held back as a contingency reserve, a quarter of the sum of the rest. The people running those scopes are paid from elsewhere, so the request covers work that nothing else funds.

In its first epoch it drew 53 DRep yes ballots, 1 no and 2 abstain, and by epoch 569 all seven committee members had voted yes on it. The [review of epochs 570 to 572](/governance-review/epochs-570-572/) has the decision and the payment.

## Two epochs with nothing to vote on

Epochs 564 and 565 record no DRep ballot at all, which reads worse than it is: the budget had closed and the first request had not yet been filed, so there was nothing open for a DRep to vote on. Voting resumed in epoch 566 with 56 ballots. Over the whole window DReps cast 316 ballots, pools 10 and committee members 8, 334 in all with superseded votes included, and 194 distinct DReps cast a final ballot.

## Delegation drops, and the treasury does not

The power delegated to DReps reached ₳5.47B at epoch 564, the highest reading up to then, and read ₳5.19B one epoch later, ₳279.5M less. Nothing left the treasury and no ada was destroyed: delegated power is stake that someone has pointed at a DRep, so a fall in it means stake was pointed somewhere else, or nowhere.

Where it went is not something the readings answer, and the arithmetic is worth doing carefully. Across epochs 563 to 569 the total fell ₳104.9M, while over exactly the same stretch the largest single decline the record lists for one DRep is ₳311.1M, from ₳714.4M to ₳403.3M. So while one large delegation shrank by three times the net fall, other DReps gained enough to absorb most of it. Over the epoch of the drop itself the power parked on the always abstain option rose from ₳6.40B to ₳6.82B, which is consistent with stake moving to a default rather than to another DRep, and also consistent with unrelated stake arriving there. The record does not distinguish the two.

Concentration eased in the same step, the ten largest holding 47.14% of the power at epoch 564 and 44.3% at epoch 565, and the smallest number of DReps that together held half of it rose from twelve to fourteen. The count with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs fell from 367 at epoch 564 to 308 at epoch 568 and read 310 at epoch 569.

## Two governance tools ask to be paid

Two more budget signals arrived, both for the software people use to take part in governance rather than for the protocol itself. [Cardano GovTool Budget - 12 months full active maintenance and development](/ga/9d213a57684d7ddf6f3350c80d042639ecbed5ccccc4a05bf54959a086593e7b00/), filed in epoch 567, is for the open source applications that most DReps register, delegate, propose and vote through: its existing funding was running out, and the consortium of builders behind it asked ₳1.15M for a year, roughly half an earlier version of the same request. [Tempo for Cardono Governance - Maintenance & Development Budget for 2025](/ga/495bd3cd44ea6bc0b4c2dbb1e4cdc7e214874c2c07490a4e2476a627ef91128000/) followed in epoch 569 for a separate, independent voting application built for phones, which runs its own node rather than depending on somebody else's, and asks ₳380,000 for six months. The [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/) has the first result and the [review of epochs 576 to 578](/governance-review/epochs-576-578/) the second.

The treasury reading rose ₳21.1M to ₳1.80B by epoch 569, the highest up to then, and none of the ₳350M allowed for the period ending at epoch 604 had been drawn.
