## A fund for stablecoin liquidity

According to its proposal text, the fund that [Stablecoin DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/e5643c33f608642e329228a968770e5b19ef5f48ff1f698712e2ce864a49e3f000/) asked for would hold fifty million ada in ada and fiat backed stablecoins and supply exchanges and lending protocols, with a nine person interim committee managing it under a treasury organisation whose membership includes DReps. The proposal sought endorsement of that budget through an information action, which cannot be ratified or release treasury funds, and said the withdrawals would follow separately. Voting closed at the start of epoch 589 with 73.73% of counted DRep power behind it, on 199 yes votes, 23 no and 12 abstain, with ₳3.91B of yes power. All seven committee members voted yes, and the pools cast three yes ballots and one abstention, the yes stake at 0.21%. Ten of the action's twelve largest DRep voters voted yes.

## More power abstained than voted yes and no combined

[Defining the Cardano 2030 Vision & Strategy](/ga/996edfe370b8e51be73541e75499a818461305e37c7fa0c1b193f2c587167cc700/) was submitted, according to its text, by a community member rather than by the committee that drafted the strategy, to gather feedback on chain where the drafters had so far collected it off chain. It closed at the start of epoch 590 with 26.22% of counted DRep power behind it, on 55 yes votes, 39 no and 43 abstain. The final tally recorded ₳1.00B of yes, ₳0.37B of no and ₳1.82B abstaining, more than the ₳1.36B of yes and no combined. The abstention ballots cast in epoch 589, the last epoch of voting, carried ₳1.18B of DRep power on their own.

Six abstainers held ₳1.56B at epoch 590 and half the places among the action's twelve largest DRep voters: [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/), [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/), [JAZZ](/dreps/drep1yt77crnmjuqkj9gcwjjsurez7s07jhwhyt4suxs3xeqftcsfrspun/) and [hix_coffeepool☕️](/dreps/drep1ygnh2uf4wkc8ldgfxwz7rzuga3m8jtqew9xh5g3n587mg6g3ge0sj/). The first four had voted yes on the liquidity budget. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) led the four who voted yes and [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) the two who voted no. Cardano Japan Council and Ace Alliance voted yes in the committee, KtorZ voted no, three members abstained and one cast no ballot. With the three abstainers left out, four counted members remain, so the two yes votes give 50%. A second, fuller version of the strategy reached the DReps in epoch 601, and its vote is in the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/).

## 300 ballots in one epoch

DReps cast 146 ballots in epoch 588, 42 in epoch 589 and 300 in epoch 590, the most in a single epoch since epoch 576. Voting on [Withdraw ₳1,150,000 for GovTool 12 months active maintenance and development](/ga/d2db60c5307cb517c735e2d0138d2b6f10fc5b221d610fa187719bdc82af9a0300/) ended at the boundary to epoch 591. As of that close it stood at 55.96% of counted DRep power, and the [review of epochs 591 to 593](/governance-review/epochs-591-593/) has the outcome. Epoch 590 was also the epoch three proposals were filed: [Loan ₳5,000,000 to Expand Cardano's Global Listings](/ga/03f671791fd97011f30e4d6b76c9a91f4f6bcfb60ee37e5399b9545bb3f2757a00/), a signal on reimbursing the deposit of an early information action and a signal on registering top level domains. A fourth, [Constitutional Committee Compensation Epochs 581-653](/ga/d2745225498d1c56c0f01be9971074a49144d625df0e73a86c51689624fbadb000/), had arrived in epoch 589, and the constitution revision submitted in epoch 586 was collecting ballots, with voting due to close at the start of epoch 593.
