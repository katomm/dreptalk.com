## A fund rather than a payment

[Stablecoin DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/e5643c33f608642e329228a968770e5b19ef5f48ff1f698712e2ce864a49e3f000/) closed at the start of epoch 589 with 73.7% of DRep power behind it, on 199 yes ballots to 23 no and 12 abstain, ₳3.91B of power for it against ₳0.17B, and all seven committee members voting yes. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no.

The proposal would deploy treasury capital as liquidity rather than pay anyone for work, with the treasury keeping a claim on the assets. ₳50M would go into a fund of ada and fiat backed stablecoins, and its text puts 99% of that into decentralised exchanges and lending protocols as liquidity, which the treasury keeps a claim on rather than gives away. A nine person interim committee of publicly identified individuals would run it from a smart contract, with oversight from a new treasury organisation, and revenue for the treasury is named as a secondary goal. As with every budget, endorsing it moves nothing: each payment into the fund still needs a withdrawal action of its own.

The support came with reservations attached. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) wrote that deeper stablecoin liquidity would "strengthen the reliability" of the protocols built on Cardano and that the oversight and audits give the community "clear visibility and control". [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) wrote as a user: there had been many situations where it would have transacted on chain "if liquidity had been higher". The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) disclosed that its yes "only narrowly" survived an internal discussion, over doubts that "this small amount of liquidity" would move activity, over how much work the board members would put in, and over a five of nine multisig it called "not sufficiently secure" for the sum. [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) abstained as "not the best expert on this topic" and asked whom the fund is for, large entities chasing yield or everyday holders doing nothing with their ada.

## The abstainers were waiting for the official version

[Defining the Cardano 2030 Vision & Strategy](/ga/996edfe370b8e51be73541e75499a818461305e37c7fa0c1b193f2c587167cc700/) closed at the start of epoch 590 with 26.2% of DRep power behind it, on 55 yes ballots, 39 no and 43 abstain. The ballot counts understate what happened. ₳1.00B of delegated power voted yes and ₳0.37B voted no, while ₳1.82B abstained, more than both sides together.

An abstention is a position, not an absence, and this time the abstainers said what it meant. The content came from the product committee of Intersect, but the action was filed by a community member acting alone, to move feedback on chain that the committee had been collecting off chain. Yoroi wrote that "an official governance action is already planned for the end of October" and that voting now "could lead to voter fatigue". [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) wrote the same, citing the product committee, and the [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) called its abstention "a commentary on the submission process, not the content". The Eternl DRep Committee abstained on the content instead: the document had "a whole lot of repetition", and its call for an actively managed treasury "depends heavily on trust in whoever manages those funds".

The no votes and the yes votes read the same document differently. [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) voted no because targets such as 200,000,000 dollars of base layer revenue lacked a calculation method, because the strategy "reads more like a comprehensive wishlist", and because a managed treasury is "a significant strategic shift" that needs a governance and risk framework first. [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) liked the document and voted no because approving the vision separately from the strategy "is a mistake". YUTA voted yes for a provisional version and promised to vote for a better one when it came. On the committee, KtorZ voted no, writing that the document "is too ambiguous in its intent", halfway between a constitution and a budget proposal, and could later be "weaponized to justify budgets" that skip due diligence, and three members abstained as not a constitutional matter. The official version those abstainers were waiting for is in the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/).

## Four new proposals, two carried over

Four proposals arrived in epochs 589 and 590, and three of them pick up threads from earlier windows.

[Constitutional Committee Compensation Epochs 581-653](/ga/d2745225498d1c56c0f01be9971074a49144d625df0e73a86c51689624fbadb000/) is a budget of ₳1M to pay the committee that took office at the start of epoch 581, which the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) covers. Its document says the action was drawn up among all seven members but provides for five of them, each able to request up to ₳200,000 through one later withdrawal, paid out from a separate smart contract per member in proportion to time served over the 73 epoch term. The two members who sit as individuals rather than as organisations are not seeking pay.
