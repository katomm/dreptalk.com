## A fund rather than a payment

[Stablecoin DeFi Liquidity Budget](/ga/e5643c33f608642e329228a968770e5b19ef5f48ff1f698712e2ce864a49e3f000/) closed at the start of epoch 589 with 73.73% of counted DRep power behind it, on 199 yes ballots to 23 no and 12 abstain, ₳3.91B of power for it against ₳0.17B, and all seven committee members voting yes.

It is the first budget in this series that does not hand money to anyone. ₳50M would go into a fund of ada and fiat backed stablecoins, and its text puts 99% of that into decentralised exchanges and lending protocols as liquidity, which the treasury keeps a claim on rather than gives away. A nine person interim committee of publicly identified individuals would run it from a smart contract, with oversight from a new treasury organisation, and revenue for the treasury is named as a secondary goal. As with every budget, endorsing it moves nothing: each payment into the fund still needs a withdrawal action of its own.

## More power abstained than voted

[Defining the Cardano 2030 Vision & Strategy](/ga/996edfe370b8e51be73541e75499a818461305e37c7fa0c1b193f2c587167cc700/) closed at the start of epoch 590 with 26.22% of counted DRep power behind it, on 55 yes ballots, 39 no and 43 abstain. The ballot counts understate what happened. ₳1.00B of delegated power voted yes and ₳0.37B voted no, while ₳1.82B abstained, more than both sides together.

An abstention is a position, not an absence. It says the DRep turned up and declined to take a side, and mechanically it removes that weight from the denominator rather than adding it to the no side, which is why a proposal can be abstained into a low percentage without anyone opposing it much. What the record does not say is why. None of the abstaining DReps published a reason.

There is one fact in the proposal itself that bears on it. The content came from the product committee, but the action was filed by a community member acting alone, to move feedback on chain that the committee had been collecting off chain. A DRep who supports the strategy but thinks a draft should be put by the people who wrote it has a reason to abstain that says nothing about the strategy. That reading fits the numbers, and it is a reading rather than a finding.

## Four new proposals, one carried over

Four proposals arrived in epochs 589 and 590. [Constitutional Committee Compensation Epochs 581-653](/ga/d2745225498d1c56c0f01be9971074a49144d625df0e73a86c51689624fbadb000/) asks whether the committee should be paid for its term. [Loan ₳5,000,000 to Expand Cardano's Global Listings](/ga/03f671791fd97011f30e4d6b76c9a91f4f6bcfb60ee37e5399b9545bb3f2757a00/) is the withdrawal that follows the loan budget endorsed in the [review of epochs 585 to 587](/governance-review/epochs-585-587/), now with a budget behind it. [Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit.](/ga/3285b7fd0da16d21e0b8f8910c37f77e17a57cfff8f513df4baf69295480108800/) asks the treasury to refund the deposit paid for a proposal, and [Securing  Generic Top-Level Domains for the Cardano Ecosystem](/ga/a36eafaea085b77f97cceacf07fe9450f8c6b47fec3af94da8f7d158a1fc972200/) asks for domain names to be secured for the ecosystem. All four had deadlines at the start of epochs 596 and 597.

[Withdraw ₳1,150,000 for GovTool 12 months active maintenance and development](/ga/d2db60c5307cb517c735e2d0138d2b6f10fc5b221d610fa187719bdc82af9a0300/) reached its last epoch of voting: as of the close of epoch 590 it stood at 55.96% of counted DRep power, with five committee members for it and one against. [CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.3](/ga/c882f194684d672316212f01efc6d28177e8965b7cd6956981fe37cc6715961e00/) was also still open, with a deadline at the start of epoch 593. The [review of epochs 591 to 593](/governance-review/epochs-591-593/) has both.

## The numbers

The window recorded 509 ballots, 488 from DReps, 5 from pools and 16 from committee members, with final ballots from 167 distinct DReps. Epoch 590 alone carried 300 DRep ballots, the four new proposals arriving at once. The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.62B at epoch 590 across 859 DReps, the ten largest holding 49.14% of it. The treasury reading rose ₳8.2M to ₳1.62B, and nothing had been paid out of it since epoch 578, leaving ₳78.0M of the ₳350M ceiling.
