## ₳190.1M in one epoch

The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.62B at epoch 608 and ₳5.81B at epoch 609. The difference, ₳190.1M, came within a single epoch, in a series that had moved between ₳5.56B and ₳5.65B over the twenty epochs before, and by epoch 611 the total stood at ₳5.82B, the highest reading in the record up to then. The count of DReps holding power went from 862 to 861.

The ten largest DReps held 49.8% of all delegated power at epoch 608 and 47.5% at epoch 609, so their combined share fell as the total rose, and the smallest group of DReps that together hold half of the total grew from eleven to twelve. [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) lost ₳47.4M between epochs 608 and 611, from ₳234.6M to ₳187.2M, the largest decline listed for that interval.

The rolling count of DReps with at least one ballot in the last 12 epochs told a different story over the same epochs. It read 320 at epoch 608 and 273 at epoch 611, 47 fewer, and it had last been at or below 273 in epoch 538. The count holding delegated power without a ballot in those twelve epochs read 592. Both rolling measures carry a reliability flag, and the difference does not identify which DReps stopped voting.

## Eight of the twelve largest voters on DeltaDeFi opposed it

[DeltaDeFi: Hydra Trading Infrastructure Budget (₳1,500,000)](/ga/a633504ddc47a852c4ae033c750597d011a3daf00a0f6bbd48de4c52ef1022dc00/) sought support for a ₳1.5M budget as an information action, which can record that support but cannot itself authorize a treasury payment. Voting closed at the start of epoch 610 with 11.97% of counted DRep power behind it, on 37 yes votes, 114 no and 18 abstain. In ada, ₳0.66B voted yes and ₳3.14B voted no, and power that cast no ballot sits on the no side of that share as well.

Ranked by delegated power at epoch 611, eight of its twelve largest DRep voters had voted no, among them [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/), and those eight held ₳2.18B in delegated power at epoch 611. Three of the twelve voted yes, [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) and [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) among them, and [Everstake](/dreps/drep1yt8p080ajks6zdnxd9z6a6q60p9sm9j5rl7tc63mfna8r6cnp4wr3/) abstained. ## The revised constitution in force

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.4](/ga/91a79f5c934b7c91e3027736d565080c2b6611fb8484b1156fdf16121fcfb41000/) took effect at the start of epoch 609, one epoch after its ratification on 79.85% of counted DRep power against a 75% bar, with six committee votes for it and one against. ## Two limit proposals for one period

[Net Change Limit (Epoch 613 to Epoch 713)](/ga/dc4c679c8cf1cec49817d4d2c1c96cd802ec8a047a11dc0b0bb125b5af0a76cd00/) proposes a ceiling on treasury withdrawals for the spending period that begins in epoch 613. Its text puts that ceiling at ₳350M, the same figure as the period ending with epoch 612. Voting on it runs to the start of epoch 612, the boundary that closes this window. As of that close it stood at 61.33% of counted DRep power, on 125 yes votes, 31 no and 10 abstain, with four committee members for it and none against.

In epoch 611 a rival arrived. [Net Change Limit of 300 Million ADA for Epochs 613 to 713](/ga/73a4eb2148781c37ef37c90a33a1d3d00511a8eefe9cdfaa1ea593b090f23f9600/) asks for the lower ceiling its title names, for the same period. As of the close of epoch 611 it had received 15 yes ballots, 7 no and 1 abstain across the roles, all of them cast in that epoch, and it remained open. The vote on the lower limit is in the [review of epochs 615 to 620](/governance-review/epochs-615-620/), the vote on the ₳350M proposal in the [review of epochs 612 to 614](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/).

No withdrawal was enacted in the window, and the treasury balance rose from ₳1.62B at epoch 609 to ₳1.63B at epoch 611. DReps cast 305 of the window's 446 ballots, pools 130 and committee members 11, superseded votes included.
