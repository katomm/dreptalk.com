## One epoch, ₳190.1M

Three epochs with no decision that changes the rules is a good moment to look at the numbers properly, and one of them moved unusually.

Between the epoch 608 and epoch 609 readings the power delegated to DReps rose ₳190.1M, from ₳5.62B to ₳5.81B. Nothing was minted and nothing came out of the treasury: delegated power is stake that somebody has pointed at a DRep, so the increase is stake arriving from holders who had it pointed somewhere else or nowhere.

At the same reading the share held by the ten largest DReps fell from 49.76% to 47.52%, and the smallest number of DReps that together held half the delegated power rose from 11 to 12. Those are three ways of saying that the new weight did not arrive at the top of the list. The Gini figure for the whole spread fell too, from 0.9367 to 0.9344, which is a fourth.

What none of them says is whose. The largest single decline the record lists for one DRep over the stretch from epoch 608 to 611 is measured across four epochs while the jump happened in one, so putting the two together would be arithmetic dressed as a trail. A snapshot of totals cannot follow a delegation from one DRep to another, and the predefined options are excluded from the totals entirely, so stake moving between a DRep and always abstain changes the figure without leaving a destination anywhere in it.

## Participation does not follow

The count of DReps with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs fell from 320 at epoch 608 to 273 at epoch 611, and 864 DReps held power at epoch 611. Neither figure is a measure of current turnout. The rolling count looks back twelve epochs, so it falls whenever a busy stretch ages out of the window, and the epochs with hundreds of ballots on the first spending round were now well behind. Inside this window 109 distinct DReps cast a final ballot across the five proposals that were open.

## The constitution takes effect, and a budget fails

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.4](/ga/91a79f5c934b7c91e3027736d565080c2b6611fb8484b1156fdf16121fcfb41000/) took effect at the start of epoch 609, one epoch after the ratification the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) covers. From that boundary the budget information action is gone as a separate step: a treasury withdrawal carries the requirements itself, so the sequence that had sunk two requests earlier in the series no longer applies. Definitions, immutable proposal documents and treasury audit safeguards on every withdrawal came with it.

[DeltaDeFi: Hydra Trading Infrastructure Budget (₳1,500,000)](/ga/a633504ddc47a852c4ae033c750597d011a3daf00a0f6bbd48de4c52ef1022dc00/) closed at the start of epoch 610 with 11.97% of counted DRep power behind it, on 22 abstaining pool ballots against two in favour. It asked for ₳1.5M over six months to take an order book exchange running on Cardano's layer two from beta into production, so that ada and the larger Cardano native tokens would have a venue with the latency and data feeds traders expect. At 11.97% it is among the weakest results in the series.

## Two ceilings for the same period

[Net Change Limit of 300 Million ADA for Epochs 613 to 713](/ga/73a4eb2148781c37ef37c90a33a1d3d00511a8eefe9cdfaa1ea593b090f23f9600/) was filed in epoch 611, proposing a lower ceiling for exactly the period that [Net Change Limit (Epoch 613 to Epoch 713)](/ga/dc4c679c8cf1cec49817d4d2c1c96cd802ec8a047a11dc0b0bb125b5af0a76cd00/) already covers, which as of the close of epoch 611 was in its last epoch of voting. Two ceilings for one period is a pattern from the [review of epochs 545 to 547](/governance-review/epochs-545-547/) onwards, and the [review of epochs 612 to 614](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/) has the first result.

The naming signal for the next hard fork, the first half of the memory increase and the untitled withdrawal against the liquidity budget were also open. The window recorded 446 ballots, 305 from DReps, 130 from pools and 11 from committee members. The treasury reading rose ₳7.7M to ₳1.63B.
