## The no came in the last epoch

For six epochs [CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.3](/ga/c882f194684d672316212f01efc6d28177e8965b7cd6956981fe37cc6715961e00/) drew far more ballot power for yes than for no. Between its submission in epoch 586 and the end of epoch 591 the yes ballots carried ₳3.40B of DRep power and the no ballots ₳0.12B, superseded ballots included, with ₳1.26B of the yes arriving in epoch 591 alone. Epoch 592 was its last epoch of voting, and in that epoch twelve no ballots were cast carrying ₳1.11B. The revision expired at the start of epoch 593. Its 173 yes votes were 64.85% of counted DRep power against a required 75%, with 29 no votes and 10 abstentions beside them.

[Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/), the largest DRep, [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and [Army of Spies](/dreps/drep1ygqgayvx8yzsaj9hprja3l6jy3v4px9z3u8uvecuvm3f92ce7mckx/) all cast their recorded no ballots in epoch 592. They were the three no voters among the action's twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 593, and held ₳1.03B between them at that epoch. The other nine voted yes. In the final tally ₳3.59B voted yes, ₳1.23B voted no and ₳0.08B abstained.

The committee had no objection on constitutionality. Five members voted yes, Eastern Cardano Council and KtorZ abstained, a share of 100%. Stake pools do not vote on a constitution. An earlier revision, [CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.0](/ga/207b226e110e13bb18b119fcd313520e0fcd060b2bc9fb9a5e5bc6e94ab10f3b00/), had expired at the start of epoch 581 on 55.61%, which the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) covers.

## A majority for GovTool, and not enough

[Withdraw ₳1,150,000 for GovTool 12 months active maintenance and development](/ga/d2db60c5307cb517c735e2d0138d2b6f10fc5b221d610fa187719bdc82af9a0300/) expired at the start of epoch 591 with 55.96% of counted DRep power behind it, on 142 yes votes, 37 no and 11 abstain, against a 67% bar. According to its text, the withdrawal would have paid for a year of maintenance and development of the governance tool by the consortium that runs it, following an earlier budget signal for the same amount, with the money to be administered by Intersect.

Epoch 590, its last epoch of voting, brought 44 yes ballots, 15 no and 4 abstain across the roles, and the DRep yes ballots of that epoch carried ₳1.80B. ₳2.79B voted yes in the final tally, ₳0.71B voted no and ₳0.63B abstained. Five committee members voted yes, Eastern Cardano Council voted no and one cast no ballot, a share of 71.43%.

## Four open, none new

Nothing was submitted in these three epochs, and four earlier proposals were collecting ballots at the close: a signal on compensating committee members, a ₳5M withdrawal described as a loan, a signal on reimbursing the deposit of an early information action and a signal on registering top level domains. The [review of epochs 594 to 596](/governance-review/epochs-594-596/) has the first, the [review of epochs 597 to 599](/governance-review/epochs-597-599/) the other three.

Two expiries meant no payment, and the treasury held ₳1.63B at epoch 593, ₳8.1M more than at epoch 591. The window recorded 443 ballots, superseded votes included, 428 of them from DReps, and at epoch 593 the Gini coefficient across the DReps holding delegated power reached 0.9348, the highest in the record up to then.
