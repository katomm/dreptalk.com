## The same change, refused a second time

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.3](/ga/c882f194684d672316212f01efc6d28177e8965b7cd6956981fe37cc6715961e00/) expired at the start of epoch 593 with 64.85% of DRep power behind it, on 173 yes ballots, 29 no and 10 abstain, and five committee members in favour with two abstaining. A new constitution needs 75%. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no.

Its central change was the one the [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) described: removing the budget information action as a separate step and folding its requirements into the treasury withdrawal action itself. Under the rule as it stood, a project needed a budget endorsed first and a payment approved second, and the committee had already refused two requests for skipping the first step.

Around that, the revision added things the earlier attempt had not. It would have deleted provisions its own text calls aspirational rather than enforceable, among them codes of conduct for DReps, stake pool operators and the committee itself. It would have added definitions for terms the constitution uses without defining, such as active voting stake and net change limit. And it would have required a proposal's documents to stay immutable after submission, and applied the treasury audit safeguards to every withdrawal rather than to some of them: the duties to appoint independent auditors, publish reports, designate an administrator and keep the money in separate auditable accounts had applied only to withdrawals above ₳1M in a two year period, and the revision would have made them the general rule.

## Three passages, named by the two largest no votes

The yes side agreed with that list. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), who led the revision, wrote that it removes "non-binding expectations", the budget information action and mandatory committee conduct rules, and the [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) and the [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) voted yes on the same grounds, the committee thanking Yuta "for putting in the work".

The no side was specific about what had to change. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) wrote that the new phrasing on audits, "appoint independent auditors", created "practical uncertainty over who is responsible for selection, funding, and timing", and that letting custodians "vote or delegate" for the holders behind them, together with the shift from "owners" to "holders", could "concentrate voting power among large custodial entities". [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) named the same audit and custodian clauses and added that the revised net change limit "weakens the explicit link between approved budgets and treasury withdrawals". Both said the points could be addressed. [Army of Spies](/dreps/drep1ygqgayvx8yzsaj9hprja3l6jy3v4px9z3u8uvecuvm3f92ce7mckx/) voted no because the changes were still hard to track, with only one attached document showing them side by side, and hix_coffeepool☕️ because a constitution deserves "the same depth of discussion, inclusivity, and deliberative process" as the original convention. On the committee KtorZ abstained "due to lack of bandwidth". What became of the three passages Yoroi and EMURGO named is in the [review of epochs 600 to 602](/governance-review/epochs-600-602/).

## The tools do not get paid

[Withdraw ₳1,150,000 for GovTool 12 months active maintenance and development](/ga/d2db60c5307cb517c735e2d0138d2b6f10fc5b221d610fa187719bdc82af9a0300/) expired at the start of epoch 591 with 56.0% of DRep power behind it, on 142 yes ballots to 37 no, with five committee members for it and one against. A treasury withdrawal needs 67%.

It is the second half of a pair. The budget was endorsed in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/), covering a year of maintenance and development on the open source governance applications used to register as a DRep, delegate, submit proposals and vote, run by a consortium of the builders behind them. The withdrawal's own document names that budget by id, says Intersect would administer the money through a smart contract and return anything unused, and itemises the sum: hosting and infrastructure, development of each part of the tools priced in full time equivalents, ₳150,000 for contributors paid by their contributions and ₳100,000 for other open source governance tools built on the same code.

The price was the objection. The Eternl DRep Committee abstained because "₳1,150,000 is too high" and said it would vote yes on a revised proposal "closer to 50% of the current amount". [Sebastien Guillemot セバ](/dreps/drep1y2csyxt7u2hl4674pl9cef5lknafaw5nraxvyx033kmd0es3awuv0/) voted no with one line: the amount "doesn't match the value" the tools bring. Dave voted no because the reported metrics, among them 50,000 unique users, were "not sufficiently supported by transparent data", and EMURGO abstained to "observe how these operational improvements perform over time". YUTA voted yes, writing that governance "is in a very difficult situation" and that the tools and wallet providers have to work together so that holders can find a DRep that matches their values. The one committee no, from the Eastern Cardano Council, was procedural: the request allocated no ada for the periodic audits and oversight metrics the constitution requires.

Both proposals expired below their own bars, a constitution needing 75% of DRep power and a treasury withdrawal 67%, and the endorsed budget behind the tool withdrawal had not carried the payment with it. For the builders of the tools, the window ended with an endorsed budget and no money.

## Still open

Four proposals were open at the close, all filed in epochs 589 and 590, and the [review of epochs 588 to 590](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/) describes each from its own document. The committee pay proposal is a ₳1M budget for five of the seven members, up to ₳200,000 each for the 73 epoch term. The listing loan request is the ₳5M withdrawal that follows the loan budget, at 2.44% interest with the balance due within five years. The deposit refund asks for ₳103,000 to make good a ₳100,000 deposit lost to a node bug. And the domain plan asks for no money, only for endorsement before the Cardano Foundation applies for the .ada and .cardano domain endings at its own expense. Their deadlines fall at the start of epochs 596 and 597, and the [review of epochs 594 to 596](/governance-review/epochs-594-596/) has the first of them.

The window recorded 443 ballots, 428 from DReps, 6 from pools and 9 from committee members, with final ballots from 152 distinct DReps. The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.63B at epoch 593 across 858 DReps, the ten largest holding 49.3% of it. The treasury reading rose ₳8.1M to ₳1.63B, with nothing paid out of it since epoch 578 and ₳78.0M of the ₳350M ceiling still unused.
