## A deadline moved by eight epochs

According to the text of [2025 Net Change Limit Extension](/ga/d16dffbae9d86a73cb343506e6712d79c278096dc25e8ba6900eb24522726bba00/), the spending period for treasury withdrawals had been due to end with epoch 604. The proposal asked to keep the same ₳350M ceiling in force for eight more epochs, to the end of epoch 612. A withdrawal ratified at the start of epoch 604 would be paid at the start of epoch 605, outside that period. Voting on the extension ended at the start of epoch 604 with 85.9% of counted DRep power behind it, on 221 yes votes, 6 no and 1 abstain. Six committee members voted yes and Eastern Cardano Council abstained. Stake pools recorded 32 yes ballots and nothing else, 6.23% of stake.

At epoch 605, 39 withdrawals had used ₳277.0M of the ₳350M, and the ₳70M withdrawal ratified in that epoch would take almost all of the remaining ₳73.0M.

## The budget signal, then the withdrawal, one epoch apart

[Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/8f54d021c6e6fcdd5a4908f10a7b092fa31cd94db2e809f2e06d7ffa4d78773d00/) closed at the same boundary, with 85.82% of counted DRep power behind it on 267 yes votes, 10 no and 7 abstain. The proposal text describes a ₳70M fund for bringing stablecoins, custody, analytics, bridges and oracles onto the chain, overseen by five organisations and paid out in milestones through Intersect. As an information action it sought the endorsement of that budget and could not itself authorise a payment. Six committee members voted yes and KtorZ abstained. Stake pools cast 58 yes ballots and 1 abstain, 9.9% of stake.

A separate action carried the money. [Withdraw ₳70,000,000 for Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/f8393f1ff814d3d52336a97712361fed933d9ef9e8d0909e1d31536a549fd22f00/) was ratified at the start of epoch 605 on 84.79% of counted DRep power against a 67% bar, from 196 yes votes, 10 no and 5 abstain. It received six committee yes votes and one no, a share of 85.71%. The no came from Eastern Cardano Council in epoch 604, one epoch after the same member had voted yes on the budget signal. The payment follows one epoch after ratification, at the start of epoch 606, which the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) covers. Without the extension that payment date would have fallen outside the period.

The same twelve DReps headed the voter list of each action by delegated power at epoch 605, and all twelve voted yes on all three, from [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) with ₳636.6M down to [BTBF_XSP](/dreps/drep1y2vyrqh0fa4qh4tmy7322va6ryk78uh6szppezaft0watyqp5hdwx/) with ₳102.3M.

## The ten largest hold 49.8%

The same twelve DReps who carried all three votes sit at the top of a distribution that grew more concentrated: the ten largest held 49.79% of delegated power at epoch 605, up from 49.21% at epoch 603 and last at least that high in epoch 522, and the Gini coefficient across DReps holding power reached 0.9360, its highest reading in the record from epoch 508 through 605.

Across the three epochs DReps cast 309 ballots, pools 35 and committee members 9, superseded votes included, 353 in all, with final ballots from 111 DReps and 20 pools. The treasury paid nothing and rose ₳8.0M between the epoch 603 and epoch 605 readings.
