## A deadline, a budget and a payment

Read separately, the three decisions of this window look like routine business. Read in order, they are one manoeuvre.

The spending period that began at epoch 532 was defined to run 72 epochs and close at the end of epoch 604. By epoch 603 it had ₳73.0M of its ₳350M ceiling unused. [Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/8f54d021c6e6fcdd5a4908f10a7b092fa31cd94db2e809f2e06d7ffa4d78773d00/) wanted ₳70M of that, and [Withdraw ₳70,000,000 for Cardano Critical Integrations Budget](/ga/f8393f1ff814d3d52336a97712361fed933d9ef9e8d0909e1d31536a549fd22f00/), the payment against it, had been filed in epoch 599 and not yet decided.

At the boundary into epoch 605 two votes closed. The budget was endorsed with 85.82% of counted DRep power, and [2025 Net Change Limit Extension](/ga/d16dffbae9d86a73cb343506e6712d79c278096dc25e8ba6900eb24522726bba00/) with 85.9%, moving the end of the period eight epochs to the end of epoch 612. One epoch later the withdrawal was ratified, with 84.79% of counted DRep power and six committee members for it. It was paid at the start of epoch 606, which the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) covers, and that is after the deadline the period had until this window.

Nothing about that is irregular. A ceiling is a ceiling on a period, a period is whatever the DReps say it is, and a payment that fits under the old ceiling would have fitted under the new one too. But it is the first time in this series that a deadline was moved while a payment was queued against it, and it is worth knowing which of the three votes made the other two possible.

## What the ₳70M is for

The budget asks for an integration fund rather than a project. Its text names the pieces it wants to bring onto Cardano: tier one stablecoins, institutional custody and wallet infrastructure, on chain analytics, cross chain bridges and pricing oracles. The argument is that these are the plumbing other things need, and that stablecoins in particular are the precondition for the kind of transaction volume the rest of the ecosystem is built to handle.

One committee member found the withdrawal unconstitutional while the rest did not, and that member had voted for the budget it draws on. The record holds the two ballots and not the reasoning between them, so the difference is visible and unexplained here.

## Two more asks

[DeltaDeFi: Hydra Trading Infrastructure Budget (₳1,500,000)](/ga/a633504ddc47a852c4ae033c750597d011a3daf00a0f6bbd48de4c52ef1022dc00/), filed in epoch 603, asks for ₳1.5M over six months to take an order book exchange built on Cardano's layer two from beta to production, so that ada and Cardano native tokens have at least one venue with the latency traders expect. Its deadline was the start of epoch 610.

[Net Change Limit (Epoch 613 to Epoch 713)](/ga/dc4c679c8cf1cec49817d4d2c1c96cd802ec8a047a11dc0b0bb125b5af0a76cd00/) followed in epoch 605 and sets up the next period, from epoch 613 to epoch 713, which begins where the extended one ends. Its deadline was the start of epoch 612, and the [review of epochs 612 to 614](/governance-review/epochs-612-614/) has the result.

Three proposals carried over: the request for an eighth committee member, the strategy framework and the third attempt at revising the constitution. The [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) has all three.

## The numbers

The window recorded 353 ballots, 309 from DReps, 35 from pools and 9 from committee members, with final ballots from 111 distinct DReps. The same large DReps backed all three of the decisions above, which is a fact about their ballots rather than a demonstration that the results turned on them. The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.59B at epoch 605 across 857 DReps, the ten largest holding 49.79% of it. The treasury reading rose ₳7.96M to ₳1.67B, with ₳73.0M of the ceiling unused and ₳70M of that already committed.
