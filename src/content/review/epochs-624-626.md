## ₳50M for a fund that proposes returns to the treasury

[Cardano x Draper Dragon: Orion Fund](/ga/8807e3cee3be5647742c329c1dea242f0c1c29b8ad050e15169e3b0302fcbfd600/) drew ₳50M from the treasury at the start of epoch 624, the largest single withdrawal since the ₳70M paid in epoch 606. It had been ratified at the start of epoch 623, before this window, on 74.89% of counted DRep power from 109 yes votes, 37 no and 10 abstain, with all seven committee members voting yes. Ranked by delegated power at epoch 626, eleven of the twelve largest DReps on its ballot list voted yes and one abstained.

The money is the first tranche of a multi-year investment fund. The proposal describes a vehicle managed by Draper Dragon that invests in companies built on or connected to Cardano, draws on the treasury in capped tranches, and returns capital to the treasury over time, with this first tranche put at roughly fifteen million dollars at the ada price the proposal assumed. The treasury balance fell by ₳46.1M between the readings at epoch 623 and epoch 624, the payment less the epoch's inflow.

Dingo took ₳6.9M for a new block producing node from Blink Labs, on 70.3% of counted power from 133 yes votes, 59 no and 13 abstain, and the liquidity budget ₳800,000 on 74.13%. The committee was unanimous on both. Across the three epochs the treasury paid ₳57.7M in all, and by epoch 626 four withdrawals had drawn ₳67.8M of the ₳350M net change limit for the spending period that began at epoch 613, a ceiling the [review of epochs 647 to 649](/governance-review/epochs-647-649/) sees raised.

## The Foundation is backed to run Catalyst

[Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst](/ga/b518efa466593906ab388f978b3695449809aacdf28d2aaf4eb5ba16b3c24a1e00/) closed at the start of epoch 626 with 83.41% of counted DRep power behind it, on 186 yes votes, 11 no and 4 abstain. All twelve of the largest DReps on its ballot list, ranked by delegated power at epoch 626, voted yes. Six committee members voted yes and one abstained. The stored pool tally holds 22 yes ballots and 2 no, with the yes side at 5.47% of stake, and only one of those pool ballots was cast inside this window, in epoch 625.

It is an information action, so the chain neither ratifies nor executes it. It records support for a question the proposal put plainly: whether the Cardano Foundation should take over Project Catalyst from the organisation that has run it so far, with that organisation's support, so that milestone reviews and payouts for the teams funded in rounds ten to fourteen carry on without a gap. The proposal is careful to say the request has nothing to do with the role of Intersect as budget administrator. The proposal calls for a transition that happens off chain.

## Nine requests for ₳162.1M

On the last epoch of the window, nine treasury requests were submitted together in a single transaction, as governance action indices zero to eight of one hash. They asked for ₳162.1M between them, from ₳3.6M to ₳62.1M apiece, and all nine were still open when the window closed, alongside two earlier requests. They were already collecting ballots in epoch 626 and remained open at the close. The [review of epochs 630 to 632](/governance-review/epochs-630-632/) picks them up.

DReps cast 122 ballots in epoch 624, 78 in epoch 625 and 286 in epoch 626. Committee members cast 9 and a single stake pool 1, for 496 in the window, superseded votes included, and 148 DReps cast a final ballot somewhere in it. The count with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs read 254 at epoch 624 and 260 at epoch 626.
