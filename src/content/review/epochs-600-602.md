## The seat is filled

[Add Constitutional Committee Member](/ga/4dab331457b61b824bbc6ba4b9d9be4750e25c0b5dd42207aeb63c7431a6b70400/) was ratified and took effect inside this window, and at the start of epoch 602 the committee had seven members again. The seat went to Cardano Curia, chosen by participating DReps in a snap election held over ten days in late November and early December, after the resignation the [review of epochs 597 to 599](/governance-review/epochs-597-599/) covers left the body one short of the seven the protocol requires. The proposal reports the audited result of that election: 88 DReps took part with ₳3.16B of stake behind them, and 33 of them, with ₳1.68B, chose Cardano Curia. The new member's term runs to epoch 653, the same end as the seat it fills, and the other six seats are untouched.

The stored tally shows 79.4% of DRep power behind it, on 173 yes ballots and a single no, and it includes votes recorded after ratification. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no. 321 stake pools voted yes and none voted no. The stake share the record stores for that pool vote is 49.45%, below the 51% a committee change needs, which cannot be squared with a ratification that happened. The historical denominator may differ from the one behind that figure, or the figure may be wrong, and this edition does not have the reconstruction to say which. What is certain is the outcome and the counts. The record also dates some ballots on the action, including that single DRep no, to epoch 601, after the ratification boundary, so the stored tally is not the tally the decision rested on either.

## The only election on offer

The yes votes were about necessity as much as the candidate. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) wrote that not adding a member "would not only make treasury withdrawal impossible, but would also make parameter updates and hard forks more difficult", and that "no other entity has the resources to hold a similar election", so rejecting the result "would likely be a waste of time". It added that the elected members had told it they would work without compensation, which removed its earlier preference for an individual over a consortium. [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) wrote that the committee below its minimum "has effectively paused Constitutional Committee ratification for most governance actions" and that the election "followed constitutional guardrails". [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) called the result "a valid community decision", and the [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) described the appointment as a bridge from the resignation of the Cardano Atlantic Council to the next committee elections at epoch 653. The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) had taken part in the election itself. The single no came from Phil UPLC, who wrote that he "personally" did not want more "committee-based" members.

## Then a proposal for an eighth

[Add Constitutional Committee Member - Christina](/ga/eb8ef28ca80dbd9ced6f577f013bdfc64897d1f4e326beb37a50fb6b62c38ccd00/) was filed in epoch 600, before the seventh seat had even been filled, and its argument is the lesson of the previous window. A committee sitting exactly at its minimum stops being able to ratify anything the moment one member leaves, which is what had just happened. An eighth member is a spare.

The candidate it names is Christina, the other contender in the snap election, which its text says was exceptionally close, and it gives her the same term to epoch 653. It is chained to the Curia action as the one it must follow, so by its own design it could not be ratified before that seat was filled and cannot displace the result. It was filed by community participants independently rather than by whoever ran the election, and a DRep voting on it is being asked two questions at once: whether the committee should have a buffer, and whether a runner up in an off chain election should be seated by an on chain vote. Its deadline was the start of epoch 607, and the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) has the result and what the voters wrote.

## A third try at the constitution, and the strategy comes back

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.4](/ga/91a79f5c934b7c91e3027736d565080c2b6611fb8484b1156fdf16121fcfb41000/) was filed in epoch 601, the third attempt at the same revision after the versions that expired at epochs 581 and 593. It carries the same substance: removing the budget step and folding it into the withdrawal action, adding definitions, requiring proposal documents to stay immutable, and applying the treasury audit safeguards to every withdrawal. What is new is three reversions: EMURGO had voted no on the previous version over its wording on ada owners, on custodians and on independent audits, and this version returns those three passages to the text of the original constitution, in response, its document says, to EMURGO's objections. Its deadline was the start of epoch 608.

[Cardano 2030: Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs](/ga/3a5be93bafd26d0dbb9bfe19f4acecaa38cef75e50201d19199e6cc856db660100/) arrived in the same epoch. The draft that the [review of epochs 588 to 590](/governance-review/epochs-588-590/) covers had been filed by a community member on the product committee's behalf, with most of the delegated power abstaining. This version comes with a mission, a strategy framework and measurable targets attached, and its text sets out the process behind it: more than 700 people through surveys, focus groups and workshops from late 2024 onwards, with the group holding authority and funding granted by DReps in the budget cycle. Its deadline was the start of epoch 608.

## Still waiting on the money

Three proposals carried over: the integrations budget, the extension of the spending period and the ₳70M withdrawal that depends on both. None was decided here. The ceiling still allowed ₳73.0M through the end of epoch 604, the ₳70M request would use almost all of it, and the period was due to close in two epochs. The [review of epochs 603 to 605](/governance-review/epochs-603-605/) has what happens to the deadline. For proposers, the window restored the one thing every withdrawal needs: a committee able to rule.

## The numbers

The window recorded 922 ballots, 537 from DReps, 371 from pools and 14 from committee members, with final ballots from 178 distinct DReps and 272 distinct pools. Pools turn out for committee votes and for almost nothing else, and two committee proposals were open at once. The power delegated to DReps read ₳5.58B at epoch 602 across 857 DReps, the ten largest holding 49.2% of it, and the count with a recent ballot held between 324 and 330 across the three epochs. The treasury reading rose ₳7.96M to ₳1.66B.
