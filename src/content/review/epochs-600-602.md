## Seven again

[Add Constitutional Committee Member](/ga/4dab331457b61b824bbc6ba4b9d9be4750e25c0b5dd42207aeb63c7431a6b70400/) was ratified at the start of epoch 601 and took effect at the start of epoch 602. According to the proposal text, the addition followed a resignation in epoch 597 that left the committee below its minimum size, and the new member was chosen by participating DReps in a snap election that Intersect ran from late November into December. At the close of epoch 602 the committee had seven members, one of them authorized from that epoch.

The DReps gave it 79.38% of counted power against a 67% bar, on 173 yes votes, 1 no and 1 abstain. [Phil UPLC](/dreps/drep1yg343cyuckglj48a6gpcey7fkfcy5x5f9g65wme3ne9q2mgaedmkm/) cast that one no, and the other eleven of the action's twelve largest voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 602, supported it. A committee change needs the DReps and the stake pools, and the members themselves have no vote on it. The pool tally is 321 yes, no no and no abstention. Across all actions, pools cast 246 ballots in epoch 600, 118 in epoch 601 and 7 in epoch 602, and 272 of them cast a final ballot somewhere in the window, alongside 178 DReps.

## A second addition, already collecting ballots

[Add Constitutional Committee Member - Christina](/ga/eb8ef28ca80dbd9ced6f577f013bdfc64897d1f4e326beb37a50fb6b62c38ccd00/) was submitted in epoch 600, while the first addition was still being voted on. Its text describes it as an independent community submission for the runner-up of the same snap election, which it calls exceptionally close, and argues that one member above the minimum gives the committee room if someone else resigns. In epoch 601, its first full epoch, it drew 118 yes ballots, 9 no and 5 abstain across the roles. In epoch 602 the newly cast DRep no ballots carried ₳404.4M against ₳59.8M behind the new yes ballots. It was still collecting ballots at the close, with its expiry set for the start of epoch 607, and the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) has the result.

With no withdrawal enacted in the window, the treasury balance grew ₳8.0M between the epoch 600 and epoch 602 readings, and ₳73.0M remained under the ₳350M spending limit after 39 withdrawals. The power delegated to DReps, the two default options excluded, went from ₳5.57B to ₳5.58B. The count of DReps with a ballot in the previous 12 epochs was 330 in epochs 600 and 601 and 324 in epoch 602. Stake pools contributed 371 of the window's 922 ballots, DReps 537 and committee members 14, superseded votes included.

## Two more arrivals in epoch 601

[Cardano 2030: Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs](/ga/3a5be93bafd26d0dbb9bfe19f4acecaa38cef75e50201d19199e6cc856db660100/) and [CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.4](/ga/91a79f5c934b7c91e3027736d565080c2b6611fb8484b1156fdf16121fcfb41000/) were submitted in the same epoch, the first an information action on a long term strategy with measurable targets, the second a revision of the constitution. The revision opened with 26 yes ballots, 1 no and 3 abstain in epoch 601, the framework with 46 yes and 3 abstain across the voting roles. Both remained open at the close, with expiry set for the start of epoch 608. Their votes are in the [review of epochs 606 to 608](/governance-review/epochs-606-608/) as well.

Three proposals from earlier epochs were also collecting ballots at the close: an information action on a ₳70M budget for critical integrations, the withdrawal for the same amount, and an extension of the spending period. The [review of epochs 603 to 605](/governance-review/epochs-603-605/) covers all three.
