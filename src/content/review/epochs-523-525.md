## Five no, seven yes

At the start of epoch 525 one parameter change expired and another was ratified. Under the bootstrap rules then in force both needed the committee alone, and DRep and pool votes did not count. [A parameter change without a title in the record](/ga/51f495aa23f4b3b3aa90afde4a0e67823bb7ac4ac65f5ffbb138373b863f2f7400/), submitted in epoch 518, expired at that boundary: Cardano Atlantic Council and Eastern Cardano Council voted no in epoch 520, Cardano Foundation and Emurgo in epoch 521 and Intersect Constitutional Council in epoch 524, two members cast no ballot, and nobody voted yes. With seven active members and no abstainer the share was 0% against a two thirds bar, the two missing ballots counting against it like no votes. The record gives it no title and holds no text for it.

[Plutus V3 Cost Model Parameter Changes Prior to Chang#2](/ga/b2a591ac219ce6dcca5847e0248015209c7cb0436aa6bd6863d0c1f152a60bc500/), submitted one epoch later, was ratified at the same boundary, with seven committee yes ballots in its stored tally, the first dated to epoch 521 and the last two to epoch 525 itself. It took effect one epoch later, in the [review of epochs 526 to 528](/governance-review/epochs-526-528/). Neither action drew a DRep or pool ballot.

## DRep ballots peak at 71

[Rename the Chang 2 Hard Fork to the Plomin Hard Fork](/ga/fff0df644d328a5367212f45bab59060bde3c4091dc96c723062896fd619731400/), an information action submitted in epoch 522 that remained open through epoch 525, was the only proposal the record lists as open to a DRep vote in the window. An information action registers a signal and is never ratified. DReps cast 71 ballots in epoch 523, the most in a single epoch up to then, 20 in epoch 524 and 35 in epoch 525, superseded votes included, and none of them on the two parameter changes. Stake pools cast 36, 4 and 4. The signal's voting was scheduled to end at the start of epoch 529, and the [review of epochs 529 to 534](/governance-review/epochs-529-534/) has its result.

## ₳1.60B delegated

At epoch 525, 569 DReps held ₳1.60B, up ₳158.8M from ₳1.45B at epoch 523 and the highest reading up to then. The count with at least one ballot in the previous twelve epochs increased from 163 to 181, and over the same interval the share of the ten largest DReps fell from 46.76% to 45.68%. The window recorded 178 ballots, superseded votes included, 126 from DReps, 44 from pools and 8 from committee members, with final ballots from 112 distinct DReps and 44 pools. The treasury gained ₳9.0M between the epoch 523 and epoch 525 readings, reaching ₳1.61B, and the record lists no enacted withdrawal.
