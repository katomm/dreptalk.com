## What ₳275M would buy

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/), filed in epoch 557, is the first proposal in this series that puts real money on the table. It asks for ₳275,269,340 over 73 epochs, roughly a year, and it is not one project but 39 of them bundled into a single question. The four largest take more than two thirds of it: ₳96.8M for core engineering on the node and the protocol, ₳69.5M for the community innovation funding programme, ₳26.8M for research, and ₳15.8M for the member organisation that would administer the whole thing. Below those sit maintenance contracts, marketing, developer tooling, hardware wallet upkeep and governance work, down to items of a few hundred thousand ada.

How the 39 were chosen matters as much as what they cost. Its text records 194 proposals submitted, a poll of DReps held off chain using their live stake, more than ₳3.8B of that stake taking part, which it puts at over 70% of actively delegated stake, and 40 proposals clearing 50% support. Thirty nine of those named Intersect as administrator and went into this bundle. Approving it would not release a single ada: each payment needs its own treasury withdrawal action, and every one has to fit inside a ceiling that is in force at the time. Its deadline was the start of epoch 564, and the [review of epochs 564 to 569](/governance-review/epochs-564-569/) has the result.

## A ceiling smaller than the budget under it

Which makes the other vote in this window awkward. The ceiling DReps had endorsed two windows earlier is ₳350M for the period from epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604, comfortably above ₳275.3M. [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/) asks for ₳200M over exactly that period, which is less than the budget it would have to accommodate, and states that it supersedes any earlier ceiling covering the same year. As of the close of epoch 560 it stood at 43.88% of counted DRep power, with its deadline at the start of epoch 561.

Its case is not that the sums are wrong but that the moment is early. This is the first time the community rather than the founding entities pays for core development, the budget processes and the audit arrangements are still being worked out, and nobody knows what selling that much ada over a year does to the market. Better, it argues, to let the treasury keep growing, spend less than it takes in, and set a higher ceiling later once there is experience to reason from. The [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/) has the result.

Four other proposals were open alongside them: a third ceiling covering epochs from 563 onwards, a budget for a second implementation of the node, a budget for putting treasury funds to work as liquidity, and one for community builders. All four had deadlines at the start of epoch 563.

## Six open votes, no decisions

Nothing closed and nothing was paid in these four epochs, which is why the ballot count is worth reading carefully. DReps cast 609 ballots, pools 69 and committee members 9, but across six open proposals rather than one, so the total says how much was on the table and not how contested any single question was.
