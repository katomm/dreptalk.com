## Voting without deciding

No action closed or was ratified in epochs 557 to 560, and no treasury withdrawal was enacted. DReps still cast 609 ballots across the four epochs, superseded votes included, and all roles together cast 687. The ballots went to six open information actions. Committee members cast 9 ballots and stake pools 69, the pools mostly in epoch 560, and 199 DReps and 23 pools cast a final ballot in the window.

## The aggregate budget arrives

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/) was filed in epoch 557, described in its proposal text as the aggregate budget for the first spending round, and had the boundary to epoch 564 as its deadline. Its first epoch on chain brought 19 yes ballots, 18 no and 1 abstention across the roles, with ₳208M of DRep power behind the no ballots against ₳82M behind the yes. In epoch 558 it received 29 yes ballots, 11 no and 3 abstentions, and the DRep yes ballots of that epoch carried ₳1,378.2M against ₳87.0M behind the no. By epoch 560 the ballots had thinned to 21 yes and 4 no. The closing is in the [review of epochs 564 to 569](/governance-review/epochs-564-569/).

## A lower limit for the same period, in its last epoch

[2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/) proposed, according to its text, a spending limit of two hundred million ada for the current period, lower than the ₳350M ceiling of the current period, on the argument that the treasury should keep growing while the community sorts out its funding processes. Its voting was scheduled to end at the start of epoch 561, and as of the close of epoch 560 it stood at 43.88% of counted DRep power. Its twelve largest DRep voters, ranked by delegated power at epoch 560, had split five yes and seven no, [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/), [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) and [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) among the no. In epoch 560 alone the no ballots amounted to ₳760M of DRep power. The [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/) has its closing, together with the four other information actions filed in epoch 556 and open here: a liquidity budget for decentralised finance, a limit proposal for epochs 563 onward, a budget for the Amaru node and a budget for community builders.

## ₳5.33B delegated across 823 DReps

The power delegated to DReps rose from ₳5.14B at epoch 557 to ₳5.33B at epoch 560, ₳193.7M more, and both the total and the count of DReps holding some of it, 823, were highs up to then. The count of DReps with a recent ballot did not follow: it read 366, 366, 367 and 360 across the four epochs, below its 409 at epoch 548. The delegated power of [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) fell ₳45.7M between epochs 556 and 560, from ₳342.6M to ₳296.9M, the largest decline listed for that interval. The treasury rose ₳12.9M between the epoch 557 and epoch 560 readings to ₳1.76B, and none of the ₳350M limit for epochs 532 to 612 had been consumed.
