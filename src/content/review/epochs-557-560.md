## What ₳275M would buy

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/), filed in epoch 557, is the largest request the series has seen. It asks for ₳275,269,340 over 73 epochs, roughly a year, and it is not one project but 39 of them bundled into a single question. The four largest take more than two thirds of it: ₳96.8M for Input Output Engineering, for core engineering on the node and the protocol, ₳69.5M for Catalyst, the community innovation funding programme, ₳26.8M for Input Output Research, and ₳15.8M for Intersect, the member organisation that would administer the whole thing. Below those sit maintenance contracts, marketing, developer tooling, hardware wallet upkeep and governance work, down to items of a few hundred thousand ada.

The bundle rests on an earlier vote: its text names the roadmap DReps endorsed at the boundary to epoch 549, in the [review of epochs 548 to 553](/governance-review/epochs-548-553/), as the foundation the budget was drawn up to deliver. How the 39 were chosen matters as much as what they cost. Its text records 194 proposals submitted, a poll of DReps held off chain using their live stake, more than ₳3.8B of that stake taking part, which it puts at over 70% of actively delegated stake, and 40 proposals clearing 50% support. Thirty nine of those named Intersect as administrator and went into this bundle. Approving it would not release a single ada: each payment needs its own treasury withdrawal action, and every one has to fit inside a ceiling that is in force at the time. The administrator role is conditional too. The document says every proposer has to pass identity and sanctions checks before a withdrawal is filed, that Intersect may decline the role for a proposal that fails them, and that payments out of the escrow can be paused if contractual terms are breached. Its deadline was the start of epoch 564, and the [review of epochs 564 to 569](/governance-review/epochs-564-569/) has the result.

## The first ballots argue about the bundle

The ballots cast in these four epochs already show the shape of the argument, and no share is given here because the vote had three epochs to run. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the largest DRep, voted yes in epoch 558 with a condition attached to every payment that would follow: withdrawals "must be performed in reasonably granular chunks", and large proposals with several parts should be broken down and put to the DReps item by item before the money is drawn. The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) voted yes in the same epoch and called the budget "just pro forma", since each withdrawal still needs 67%.

The early no votes were about the form. [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) wrote in epoch 558 that proposals should come "in smaller, categorized groups" once a ceiling is set, because "it's not just about what we fund" but how. [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) had supported 35 proposals in the off chain signalling and found 14 it did not support inside this bundle, "unacceptable" as a package. The Tempo DRep objected that many of the bundled proposals had not reached the 50% mark on their own in the signalling vote, and InputEndorsers that one vote on 39 proposals "flattens nuance and imposes an all-or-nothing decision".

## A ceiling smaller than the budget under it

Which makes the other vote in this window awkward. The ceiling DReps had endorsed in the previous window is ₳350M for the period from epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604, comfortably above ₳275.3M. [2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/) asks for ₳200M over exactly that period and states that it supersedes any earlier ceiling covering the same year. The budget's own period runs 73 epochs from the close of its vote, so the two periods overlap rather than coincide, and a ₳200M ceiling would constrain the withdrawals that fall inside the overlap, which is most of them. As of the close of epoch 560 it stood at 43.88% of DRep power, with its deadline at the start of epoch 561. The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no.

Its case is not that the sums are wrong but that the moment is early. This is the first time the community rather than the founding entities pays for core development, the budget processes and the audit arrangements are still being worked out, and nobody knows what selling that much ada over a year does to the market. Better, it argues, to let the treasury keep growing, spend less than it takes in, and set a higher ceiling later once there is experience to reason from.

The DReps who voted no did the arithmetic against the budget above. SASA wrote in epoch 555 that the requests for core development, the community funding programme and research alone came to 193,000,000 ada, so a ₳200M limit "would nearly exhaust the limit" with little left for anything else. [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) wrote that cutting a ratified limit "would undermine governance predictability" and delay the ₳275M budget a majority of DReps already backed, and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) that repeated attempts to redefine the cap risk keeping funding from projects, the ₳350M being a maximum and not a target. The Eternl DRep Committee's no was one sentence: a ₳200M limit for the rest of the year would delay funding for every project except those of the largest proposer. [BTBF_XSP](/dreps/drep1y2vyrqh0fa4qh4tmy7322va6ryk78uh6szppezaft0watyqp5hdwx/) had supported 250,000,000 ada for the term and found 200,000,000 "a bit too low". CardanoYoda voted no while calling it one of the toughest decisions yet, having itself asked for a lower limit. The yes side wanted restraint on principle. Dave wrote that it would prefer a limit of 100,000,000 to 150,000,000 ada and took ₳200M as "a sensible step for now". YUTA voted yes on this ceiling and on the overlapping one in epoch 556, reasoning that ₳350M is too much for half a year but that ₳200M alone would not cover budgets running into the next year. Martin Lang voted no for the simplest reason on the page: it had voted yes on the limit that passed the day before, and a limit is a limit. The [review of epochs 561 to 563](/governance-review/epochs-561-563/) has the result.

Four other proposals were open alongside them, all with deadlines at the start of epoch 563. A third ceiling asks for ₳300M for epochs 563 to 635, on the reasoning that the limits on the table only ran to the end of 2025 while the budgets being proposed were built on twelve month timelines. A ₳1.5M budget for a second implementation of the node, administered by the project's own maintainer committee, sits outside the ₳275.3M bundle by the bundle's own rule, which excludes every proposal that went on chain on its own. A budget for putting treasury funds to work as liquidity was also open. And the budget for community builders overlaps with the bundle rather than adding to it: it carries the community proposals that cleared a ₳2.69B support bar in the same off chain poll, computed against all active voting stake rather than the stake that voted, and items such as Midgard and zkFold appear in both.

## Six open votes, no decisions

Nothing closed and nothing was paid in these four epochs, which is why the ballot count is worth reading carefully. DReps cast 609 ballots, pools 69 and committee members 9, but across six open proposals rather than one, so the total says how much was on the table and not how contested any single question was.
