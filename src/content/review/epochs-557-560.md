## The budget bundles 39 proposals from an off chain DRep poll

[Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect](/ga/e14de8d9dc4f4ddf3fe9250a8a926e20f10e99b86bd0610b77d7a054981591ee00/), filed in epoch 557 on behalf of Intersect and the Cardano Budget Committee, asks for ₳275,269,340 for 73 epochs from the close of its vote. It bundles 39 proposals into one decision. The four largest are ₳96.8M for core development by Input Output Engineering, ₳69.5M for Input Output's Catalyst proposal for community innovation funding, ₳26.8M for Input Output Research and ₳15.8M for Intersect, which would administer the budget. The other 35 include maintenance, marketing, events, developer tooling and governance work. Its deadline was the start of epoch 564.

The document cites the roadmap information action of March 30, covered in the [review of epochs 548 to 553](/governance-review/epochs-548-553/), as the foundation the budget was built to deliver. A total of 194 proposals were submitted, and DReps voted on them in an off chain poll with their live stake, more than ₳3.8B of it, which the document puts at over 70% of actively delegated stake. Of the 40 proposals that cleared 50% of the participating stake, 39 named Intersect as administrator and went into the bundle. The bundle leaves out proposals filed on chain on their own.

The budget pays nobody by itself: each payment needs its own treasury withdrawal action, and every withdrawal has to fit within the ceiling in force at the time. Before a withdrawal is filed, each proposer has to pass identity, business and sanctions checks, and Intersect may decline the administrator role for a proposal that fails them. Payments from the escrow contract can be paused if contractual terms are breached.

## CardanoYoda and SASA vote no, YUTA votes yes on conditions

Ballots cast in epoch 557, the budget's first epoch, carried ₳208.0M of DRep power against it and ₳82.3M for it. [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) voted no that epoch, writing that it had supported 35 proposals in the off chain poll but that 14 proposals in the bundle were ones it had not supported, which made the package "unacceptable". Tempo.Vote, voting no the same epoch, wrote that many of the bundled proposals had not drawn the ₳2.6B of voting power needed to cross 50%. [SASA_nagamaru_ながまる](/dreps/drep1ytuddrgqca0xd4aams6pl9zndpcmxg0rs7z3mggjelm6kscp4arg5/) voted no in epoch 558, writing that the ceiling should come first and proposals then "in smaller, categorized groups", and that "it's not just about what we fund" but how.

Ballots cast in epoch 558 carried ₳1,378.2M for the budget and ₳87.0M against. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/), the DRep with the most delegated power at epoch 560, voted yes in epoch 558 with conditions for the withdrawals to follow: they "must be performed in reasonably granular chunks", and large proposals with several parts should be broken down and put to a DRep vote item by item. The [Eternl DRep Committee](/dreps/drep1y29h2q6cst2pvkl2sqqvf5ljcy36uv7pmy482xnczddzgqshus24w/) voted yes the same epoch and called the budget "just pro forma", since each withdrawal would still need 67%.

## The ₳200M ceiling stands at 43.9% at the close

[2025 Cardano NCL](/ga/7d9fc9fe4cee64fb34e57783378ac869a85c78d6fbcd4078ed131ab6fa3c7db600/), filed in epoch 554 in a document that names no author, asks that treasury withdrawals from epoch 532 to the end of epoch 604 total no more than ₳200M. That is the period of the ₳350M ceiling covered in the [review of epochs 554 to 556](/governance-review/epochs-554-556/), and the document states that in a conflict it supersedes any earlier ceiling covering the same year. It argues that the first ceiling should be low while budget and audit processes are still being worked out and the effect of selling that much ada is untested, and that a limit for the following year can be set once there is experience. As of the close of epoch 560, its last epoch of voting, it stood at 43.9% of DRep power, against the more than 50% of active voting DRep stake its document names as the bar.

The shares in this edition are the yes power as a share of all counted power, with abstaining power left out and power that cast no ballot counted as no. Ballots cast on the ₳200M ceiling in epochs 554 to 556 had carried ₳1.94B of DRep power for it and ₳0.64B against, and those cast in epochs 557 to 560 ₳0.39B for it and ₳1.54B against, superseded ballots included. Six committee members found it constitutional, and the Eastern Cardano Council abstained in epoch 558.

The five largest no votes by delegated power at epoch 560 were all cast in these four epochs: [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) in epoch 557, the Eternl DRep Committee and [SIPO](/dreps/drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh/) in epoch 558, [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) and the [Cardano Foundation](/dreps/drep1ydpfkyjxzeqvalf6fgvj7lznrk8kcmfnvy9hyl6gr6ez6wgsjaelx/) in epoch 560.
