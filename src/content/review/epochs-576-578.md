## ₳102.1M in three epochs

At the start of epoch 576, 27 withdrawals were paid at once for ₳78.1M, two more followed at the start of epoch 577 for ₳18.0M and five at the start of epoch 578 for ₳6.0M: 34 withdrawals and ₳102.1M in three epochs, on top of the ₳168.4M that three earlier withdrawals had drawn at the start of epoch 575 and the ₳1.5M enacted in epoch 571. The treasury balance, ₳1.82B at epoch 574, read ₳1.57B at epoch 578, ₳253.9M lower, and of the ₳350M the spending period allows, 38 withdrawals had now used ₳272.0M. Of the 39 requests filed in epoch 570, 37 were paid and two expired: three payments at the start of epoch 575 and 34 in epochs 576 to 578.

The smallest paid ₳45,217.
