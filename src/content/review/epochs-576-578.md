## More power for than against, and no money

Two requests from the first spending round expired at the start of epoch 577, and both had more delegated power voting for them than against.

One of them, [the quadratic funding request](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e1b/) closed at 57.32% of counted DRep power. It would have funded a grant mechanism meant to run alongside the existing community programme, using quadratic funding and a reputation score so that many small supporters count for more than a few large ones, and its text makes a point of taking no operational cut. [the tokenised property request](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e1e/) closed at 52.72%. It would have funded a protocol for selling fractions of the equity in a house, prototyped on a private chain elsewhere and proposed for Cardano.

A treasury withdrawal needs 67% of counted DRep power, a deliberately high bar for moving money, and neither got near it. The second case is the sharper one: 93 DReps voted yes and 113 voted no, so it did not have a majority of ballots either, only a majority of weight. The first shows the other pattern, a comfortable lead among voters that still leaves a third of the counted power unaccounted for, because power that never votes counts against.

Neither failed on constitutionality. All seven committee members found both of them constitutional, which is the committee doing its narrow job: it checks proposals against the rules, and it has nothing to say about whether a project is worth ₳3.0M.

## The round finishes paying

The payments came in three batches: 27 withdrawals at the start of epoch 576, two more at epoch 577 and five at epoch 578, ₳102.1M across the window. That takes the total drawn against the ceiling to ₳272.0M out of ₳350M, across 38 paid withdrawals since the first one at epoch 571, and leaves ₳78.0M for the rest of the period ending at epoch 604. Of the 39 requests in the round, 37 were paid and the two above were not.

The ada that leaves the treasury is not yet with the vendor. The documents of the requests Intersect submitted on behalf of a vendor all describe the same route: the ada goes into a treasury reserve contract run by Intersect, milestones, acceptance criteria and payment dates are fixed in a contract with each vendor, a third party signs off each milestone, and only then is the money disbursed. So the ₳102.1M that left the treasury in this window is money that left the chain's own account, with the work it pays for still ahead of it.

The treasury reading fell ₳15.6M over the same three epochs, far less than the ₳102.1M paid, because most of the payments fell in epoch 576 while income kept arriving in all three.

## What the next window decides

Four proposals were open at the close, and two of them would change the rules the round just finished under. [Replace Interim Constitutional Committee](/ga/47a0e7a4f9383b1afc2192b23b41824d65ac978d7741aca61fc1fa16833d111100/) would put an elected committee in place of the one appointed for the trial phase. Its document says the appointed committee's term ends with epoch 580, which is also the action's deadline, and that the seven members chosen in an election held off chain take staggered seats, three for 146 epochs and four for 73, three of them already members of the interim committee. It is also the one action of the four on which pools have a vote.

[CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.0](/ga/207b226e110e13bb18b119fcd313520e0fcd060b2bc9fb9a5e5bc6e94ab10f3b00/) would revise the constitution ratified eight windows earlier, with a deadline at the start of epoch 581. Its document lists wording, definitions and the removal of unused provisions, and one change of substance: dropping the requirement that a treasury withdrawal be preceded by a budget info action. That is the requirement every one of the 39 requests of this round was filed under, each document stating that it gives effect to the budget agreed in epoch 564. The two requests for the token listing plan covered in the [review of epochs 573 to 575](/governance-review/epochs-573-575/) also ran to the start of epoch 580. The [review of epochs 579 to 581](/governance-review/epochs-579-581/) has all four results.

## Why the pools turn up

The window recorded 921 ballots, 646 from DReps, 260 from stake pools and 15 from committee members. Ballots from pools are the surprise, after three from pools in the whole of the previous window: pools have no vote on a treasury withdrawal, but they do vote on a change to the committee, and 260 of them used it. On the DRep side the count with a ballot in the previous twelve epochs rose from 343 to 378, so the falling ballot totals through the window are a matter of fewer proposals being open rather than fewer DReps taking part.

The power delegated to DReps rose ₳19.2M across the window to ₳5.48B, held by 848 DReps at epoch 578, the most up to then.
