## Socious's quadratic funding request expires at 57.3%

[Socious's quadratic funding request](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e1b/) expired at the start of epoch 577 with 57.3% of DRep power behind it, on 124 yes ballots to 81 no and 15 abstain, against the 67% a treasury withdrawal needs. On the ballots cast alone, ₳3.06B of delegated power voted yes and ₳1.41B no, above 67% of the two together, and counting the power that cast no ballot as no put the share at 57.3%. All seven committee members found the request constitutional.

Its document, which Intersect filed on behalf of Socious, asked ₳1.5M for a funding programme beside Catalyst that weights each backer's vote by the square root of the donation and a reputation score, and takes no operating costs out of the money. [Yoroi W₳llet](/dreps/drep1ygr9tuapcanc3kpeyy4dc3vmrz9cfe5q7v9wj3x9j0ap3tswtre9j/) called it "a larger-scale testbed for governance innovation" and [EMURGO](/dreps/drep1ytvlwvyjmzfyn56n0zz4f6lj94wxhmsl5zky6knnzrf4jygpyahug/) "a timely contribution" to the infrastructure around Catalyst, and the Eternl DRep Committee and the Cardano Foundation voted yes too. The DRep Socious wrote that it voted yes on all 39 requests of the round. [YUTA](/dreps/drep1y2200we9c904un36tzaearntzzl63snffuul9qsk0te4utqfkke0w/) voted no on this request and on Haus's.

Dave and Hephaestus Stake Pool voted no, both writing that Catalyst's fourteenth funding round already included a quadratic voting proof of concept at its tallying stage and that its results should come first. HOSKY wrote that weighting donations by their square root and a reputation score moves influence away from proportional ada ownership, and that one ada should equal one vote. Tempo.Vote wrote that the cost was too high and that Catalyst's own operating funding should cover it, and [CardanoYoda (MANDA Pool)](/dreps/drep1y2m0g4r66pyaw3p7u454wc0p4f0ygm8ueaev0mgd3tvwm7sskqwqp/) scored the request 4 out of 12 on its own scale, with 0 for value for money.

## Haus's tokenised property request expires at 52.7%

[Haus's tokenised property request](/ga/8ad3d454f3496a35cb0d07b0fd32f687f66338b7d60e787fc0a22939e5d8833e1e/) expired at the same boundary with 52.7% of DRep power, on 93 yes ballots to 113 no and 20 abstain, and all seven committee members found it constitutional. Fewer DReps voted yes than no, but with more delegated power: ₳2.54B for and ₳1.64B against, below 67% of the two even on the ballots cast alone, with ₳0.78B abstaining. Its document, filed by Intersect on behalf of Haus, asked ₳3.0M to move Haus's protocol for selling fractions of home equity from a private Ethereum blockchain, where it was prototyped, to Cardano and to launch HausCoin there.

Hephaestus Stake Pool called it a large ask for a relatively unknown project and found some supporting documents behind a request for an email address. Kyle Solomon moved to abstain after meeting Haus at Rare Evo, writing that Haus should still be a venture capital play or get retroactive funding rather than treasury money.

[Tempo for Cardono Governance - Maintenance & Development Budget for 2025](/ga/495bd3cd44ea6bc0b4c2dbb1e4cdc7e214874c2c07490a4e2476a627ef91128000/) closed at the start of epoch 576 with 41.0% of DRep power, on 119 yes ballots to 75 no and 22 abstain.
