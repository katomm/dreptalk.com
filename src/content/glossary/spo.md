An **SPO** (stake pool operator) runs a stake pool: infrastructure that produces blocks for the Cardano network on behalf of the ada holders who stake with it. In governance, SPOs form one of the three voting bodies, alongside [DReps](/glossary/drep/) and the [constitutional committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee/).

SPOs do not vote on every [governance action](/glossary/governance-action/). Their vote is required for a [motion of no confidence](/glossary/motion-of-no-confidence/), for [updates to the constitutional committee](/glossary/update-constitutional-committee/), and for a [hard fork initiation](/glossary/hard-fork-initiation/). They also co-vote on [protocol parameter changes](/glossary/protocol-parameter-change/) that touch security-relevant parameters, and their votes are recorded on info actions, which carry no threshold and are not binding. An SPO's [voting power](/glossary/voting-power/) is the stake delegated to their pool, which is independent of the governance delegation the same holders give to a DRep.
