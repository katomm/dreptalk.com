A **DRep** (delegated representative) is a registered participant in Cardano's on-chain governance who votes on [governance actions](/glossary/governance-action/) on behalf of the ada holders who delegate to them. The role was introduced by CIP-1694 in the Conway era: instead of every wallet voting on every proposal, holders hand their [voting power](/glossary/voting-power/) to a representative whose vote then carries that combined weight.

Anyone can become a DRep by registering on-chain with a refundable deposit. A DRep can publish a metadata profile (name, objectives, contact details) so delegators know who they are; registration and a public profile are separate steps. Alongside individually registered DReps, the protocol provides two predefined options every holder can delegate to instead: [Abstain](/glossary/abstain/) and No Confidence (see [motion of no confidence](/glossary/motion-of-no-confidence/)).

DReps vote on almost every type of governance action, and many publish a [vote rationale](/glossary/vote-rationale/) explaining each decision. You can browse all registered DReps and their voting records in the [DRep directory](/dreps/).
