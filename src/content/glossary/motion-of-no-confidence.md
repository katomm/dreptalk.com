A **motion of no confidence** is a [governance action](/glossary/governance-action/) declaring that the community no longer trusts the current [Constitutional Committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee/). It is voted on by [SPOs](/glossary/spo/) and [DReps](/glossary/drep/) only; the committee itself does not vote, since the action challenges its legitimacy. If ratified, the committee is dissolved and a new one must be installed before normal governance can resume in full.

The same idea also exists as a standing [delegation](/glossary/delegation/) choice: **Always No Confidence** is one of the two predefined options an ada holder can delegate to instead of a DRep. Stake delegated this way automatically counts in favor of any open motion of no confidence and against keeping the current committee.
