**Update the constitutional committee** is a [governance action](/glossary/governance-action/) that changes the composition of the [Constitutional Committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee/): it can add members, remove members, or change the threshold of committee votes required for the committee to approve an action.

It is voted on by [SPOs](/glossary/spo/) and [DReps](/glossary/drep/). The committee itself does not vote, because it is the subject of the action. Together with the [motion of no confidence](/glossary/motion-of-no-confidence/), this action is how the community keeps the committee accountable: membership is granted and revoked on-chain, not appointed permanently.
