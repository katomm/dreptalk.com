**New constitution or guardrails script** is a [governance action](/glossary/governance-action/) that adopts a new version of the Cardano Constitution or updates the guardrails script. The constitution is the document all governance actions are checked against; the guardrails script is its enforceable on-chain counterpart, a script that constrains which protocol parameters may change and within what bounds.

The action is voted on by [DReps](/glossary/drep/) and the [Constitutional Committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee/). Because the constitution defines the rules the committee itself enforces, changing it is one of the most consequential actions in the system. Constitution and guardrails topics are discussed in the [Constitution and Guardrails category](/c/constitution/).
