The **Constitutional Committee** (CC) is the governance body whose job is to check that proposed [governance actions](/glossary/governance-action/) conform to the Cardano Constitution. Its members vote Constitutional or Unconstitutional on an action; an action that the committee rejects cannot be ratified even if [DReps](/glossary/drep/) and [SPOs](/glossary/spo/) approve it.

The committee votes on most action types, with two exceptions where it has a conflict of interest: a [motion of no confidence](/glossary/motion-of-no-confidence/) (which challenges the committee itself) and [updates to the committee or its threshold](/glossary/update-constitutional-committee/) (where it is the subject of the action). Members are added and removed through those same on-chain actions, so the committee's composition is itself under community control.

Discussion of the constitution and its guardrails happens in the [Constitution and Guardrails category](/c/constitution/).
