**Voting power** is the weight a participant's vote carries in Cardano governance. Votes are not counted per person but per ada: every holder's stake contributes voting power, and outcomes are decided by the share of stake voting each way, not by the number of voters.

A [DRep's](/glossary/drep/) voting power is the sum of all stake delegated to them by ada holders, so it changes as people delegate to or away from them. An [SPO's](/glossary/spo/) voting power is the stake delegated to their pool. Whether a [governance action](/glossary/governance-action/) passes depends on the share of participating voting power that votes Yes, with thresholds set per action type by protocol parameters; stake delegated to [Abstain](/glossary/abstain/) is left out of that calculation.

The [DRep directory](/dreps/) shows each DRep's current voting power and lets you compare how concentrated or spread out delegation is.
