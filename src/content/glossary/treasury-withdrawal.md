A **treasury withdrawal** is a [governance action](/glossary/governance-action/) that moves ADA from Cardano's on-chain treasury to one or more stake addresses. The treasury accumulates a share of transaction fees and monetary expansion each epoch; withdrawals are how the community spends it, typically on approved budgets, development work, and grants.

It is voted on by [DReps](/glossary/drep/) and the [Constitutional Committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee/). Withdrawals are expected to conform to the constitution and to a community-approved budget, which is what the committee checks when it reviews them. The [treasury overview](/treasury/) tracks enacted withdrawals against the net change limit, and budget topics are discussed in the [Budget and Treasury category](/c/budget/).
