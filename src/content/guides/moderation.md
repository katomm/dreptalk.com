DRepTalk keeps moderation light and transparent. There are no appointed
moderators today: the community itself decides what stays. We may bring in
moderators later as the forum grows, but for now a single, simple rule governs
what gets hidden.

## How flagging works today

Any signed-in member can flag a post they consider abusive, spam, or off-topic.
Once a post has been flagged by **3 different people**, it is
automatically hidden from the forum. No single person can take a post down on
their own: it always takes three independent flags, so one bad actor cannot
silence a discussion.

## Looking ahead

Appointed moderators with additional tools are a possibility for the future,
especially as the community scales. Until then, community flagging is the only
moderation mechanism, and the threshold is kept deliberately simple.
